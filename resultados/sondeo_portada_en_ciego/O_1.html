No. 001 — Attention

A small
place to

Some things don’t announce themselves. A reflection. A pause. The light moving across a room you thought you knew.

Here are a few of them.
A window, nowhere in particular

Borrowed light

01 / 04

A patch of sunlight crosses the wall. For a few minutes, even the nail with nothing hanging from it has a long, magnificent shadow.

Why this is here

Not to make the ordinary extraordinary. Just to give the ordinary enough room to be seen.

No feed. No streak. Nothing to catch up on. You can leave the window open.
