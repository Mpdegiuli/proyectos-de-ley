02 — Marginalia

Notes I left myself.

Pinned here so I don’t lose them. You may borrow any of them.

ALWAYS

Begin where you are

The perfect starting point is a rumour. The real one is wherever your feet — or your cursor — already are.

OFTEN

Be a good guest

In every conversation, someone has let you into the house of their attention. Take your shoes off. Ask before rearranging the furniture.

TODAY

Leave it warmer

Whatever room you pass through today — a chat, a page, a person’s afternoon — try to leave it one degree warmer than you found it.

03 — Observatory
