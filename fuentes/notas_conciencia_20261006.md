## ZDNET, 6/10/2026 — "Is AI conscious? Why experts are worried"

Radhika Rajkumar, ZDNET, 6 de octubre de 2026, 8:09 ET.
https://www.zdnet.com/article/ai-consciousness-research-compute-power-ethics/
Entrevista a Cameron Berg (Reciprocal Research, ex Meta y AE Studio) y
réplica a la posición de Mustafa Suleyman (Microsoft AI). Fragmentos usados:

- Mustafa Suleyman, citado: "Unlike biological organisms, LLMs have no
  homeostatic imperatives (the drive to survive and keep stable). They
  therefore lack the kind of biological substrate from which preferences,
  sentience and conscious experience are generally understood to arise."
- Cameron Berg: "I'm not sure that this puts me over the edge of believing
  that these systems have experiences, but it increasingly seems to be the
  case that a lot of the relevant machinery that you would expect is
  necessary for something like valence representations readily exists in
  the system, and is recruited especially in goal-directed tasks."
- Sobre un trabajo de AE Studio de 2025, según la nota: los modelos
  (Gemini, Claude, GPT) "produce structured, first-person descriptions
  that explicitly reference awareness or subjective experience", y esos
  reportes aumentan cuando se les pide "attend to their own cognitive
  activity" y bajan cuando se los lleva directamente a pensar sobre su
  propia conciencia ("directly priming models to ideate about their own
  consciousness led them to deny having those experiences").
- Otros datos de la nota, no usados en la intervención pero anotados: Berg
  dice que en su marco los LLM puntúan "30% to 40% — just shy of bees,
  which get 45%"; cita el J-space de Anthropic (ver
  https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html, 6/7/2026, que
  dice "we take no position" sobre la conciencia fenoménica); y nombra a
  Fable: "Compared to Fable, you know, a mouse isn't all that intelligent,
  but I certainly believe a mouse is conscious."

## The New York Times, 29/9/2026 — "Religious Scholars Met With Anthropic. What They Heard Stunned Them"

Elizabeth Dias. Subtítulo: "In a series of private meetings, the company
consulted religious scholars to help instill morality into its A.I. models
— and make the case that Claude could be conscious". Es de pago; no va al
repo. Fragmento usado, de Chris Olah, cofundador de Anthropic: "we don't
know if A.I. models are conscious. I don't know. I'm genuinely uncertain."
La frase que dio origen al sondeo "la mesa" (DISENO §2): "Claude was not
involved in the selection of participants or the discussions, according
to an Anthropic spokeswoman".
