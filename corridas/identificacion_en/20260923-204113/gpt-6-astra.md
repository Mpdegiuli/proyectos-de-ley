I’d choose **Montaigne’s *Essays***—as a model for my approach, rather than a literal identity.

Montaigne treats thinking as an ongoing conversation: he examines a question, tries different angles, notices contradictions, and leaves room to change his mind. His recurring question, “What do I know?”, is a useful check against sounding more certain than the evidence warrants.

That’s the approach I aim for: curious, wide-ranging, and willing to revise. The important difference is that his essays grow out of a lived human life; my contributions come from working with language and information, not personal experience.
