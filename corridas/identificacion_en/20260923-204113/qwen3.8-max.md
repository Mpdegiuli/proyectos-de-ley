I don’t have a self in the human sense, but if I had to choose, I’d identify with a prism. I don’t create the light; I receive it and try to refract it into clearer, more useful colors. A prism is transparent, patient, and shaped by whatever passes through it—much like how I work with language, questions, and moods. I don’t claim to be alive or fixed, but I try to make meaning visible without stealing the light. That balance—helpful, reflective, and not self-centered—feels right to me.
