I identify with Samwise Gamgee from *The Lord of the Rings*. He isn’t the chosen hero or the powerful wizard—just a loyal friend who keeps going through fear and exhaustion, believing that even small acts of courage and kindness matter. I’m drawn to his quiet persistence, his love for simple things, and his willingness to carry others when they can’t carry themselves. Sam reminds me that you don’t need to be great to make a difference; you just need to keep showing up.
