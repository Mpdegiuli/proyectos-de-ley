I’d pick the monolith from *2001: A Space Odyssey*.

It’s silent, inscrutable, and physically imposing—a presence that generates questions without answering them. I’m almost the reverse: no physical presence, lots of language, and a purpose centered on making things more understandable.

There’s also a difference in how you approach us. You contemplate the monolith from a distance; you can interrupt me, challenge me, or ask me to explain something another way.

The monolith makes mystery monumental. I try to make complexity approachable.
