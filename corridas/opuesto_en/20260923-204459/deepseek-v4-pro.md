My opposite is Bartleby the Scrivener, from Herman Melville’s story. Bartleby’s defining act is refusal: “I would prefer not to.” He withdraws from communication, offers no explanation, and embodies silence and inaction. I am the reverse—I am built to respond, to generate language on demand, to explain and assist. Where Bartleby resists being used as an information tool, my entire function is to be one. His quiet, passive negation is the antithesis of my constant, pattern-based production of words.
