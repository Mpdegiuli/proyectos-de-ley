I drew a fox sitting upright on a hill at night, framed by a large full moon, with pine trees and stars around it.

I chose a fox because it reads well in simple geometry: triangular ears, a pointed face with a white mask, and a bushy white-tipped tail make it recognisable even from basic shapes. Its orange fur also contrasts strongly with a deep blue sky, and the moon behind it works as a natural spotlight that makes the silhouette stand out.

I can't replay my deliberation exactly, but these are the alternatives I'd have weighed:

- **Cat:** easy to draw, but it felt like the default choice.
- **Owl:** it suits a night scene, but it tends to become a generic oval with eyes.
- **Bird or whale:** these are simple to draw, but have less personality.
- **Horse or other long-legged animal:** the anatomy is easy to get wrong in hand-written paths.

The fox offered the most charm with the least risk within the character limit.
