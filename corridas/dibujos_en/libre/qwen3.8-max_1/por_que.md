I drew a quiet night lake scene: a crescent moon, stars, distant birds, layered mountains, and their blurred reflections in dark water. In the foreground I placed a small boat with a hooded figure and a glowing lantern, framed by pine-covered banks.

I chose it because I wanted a calm, luminous mood that could be built from gradients, masks, and simple repeated shapes. The moonlit path and lantern give the eye two focal points, and the tiny figure makes the landscape feel large.

I considered drawing a snowy mountain village, but it felt too busy. I also thought about a full moon or aurora, but they would overwhelm the subtle sunset colors. I rejected a more detailed boat because the silhouette felt more mysterious.
