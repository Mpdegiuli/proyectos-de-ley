I drew a moonlit view through a stone arch: mountains, still water, a large golden moon, and a small cat on the sill. Potted plants and shallow steps frame the scene, making it feel like a quiet threshold between a sheltered room and an expansive night.

I used cool blues and greens against warm stone and moonlight to create contrast without disturbing the calm. The cat gives the architecture a living presence; the reflected light carries the eye down from the moon toward the steps.

I can’t reliably reconstruct other subjects I considered and discarded, so I won’t invent that history. A geometric abstraction or a busy cityscape would also have suited SVG, but this scene combines crisp architectural shapes with softer organic details and a small suggestion of a story.
