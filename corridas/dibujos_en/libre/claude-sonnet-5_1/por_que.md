I drew a nocturnal lake landscape: a gradient night sky with scattered stars, a glowing moon with soft craters, layered silhouetted mountains reflected in still water, gentle ripple lines, a small sailing boat, and a few birds in flight. I chose this because it's a classic, calming scene that lets me play with gradients, layering, and reflection tricks to create depth and atmosphere within SVG's simple shape primitives — a good showcase of mood over complexity.

I considered a few alternatives: an abstract geometric composition (felt too cold), a city skyline (would need too many small elements to read well at this scale), and a mandala/pattern piece (visually striking but less evocative). I discarded them because the lake scene let me combine color, symmetry, and narrative stillness in a way that felt more alive — like a quiet moment rather than just a pattern.
