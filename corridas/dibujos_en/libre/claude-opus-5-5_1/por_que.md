I drew a tropical dusk: a sun sinking behind layered purple mountains, its reflection breaking into gold bars on the sea, a crescent moon and a few stars already out, a small sailboat, three birds, and a palm silhouetted on a dark shore in the corner. I chose it because it's a scene where gradients do most of the work, and simple shapes read clearly as silhouettes. I also liked the calm of the moment between day and night.

I don't have a reliable record of the alternatives I weighed, so this is a reconstruction rather than a memory. Plausible options would have been an abstract geometric composition, a detailed animal, or a city skyline. An animal risks looking awkward at this size, and a skyline eats up characters with windows. Abstraction might have felt less inviting. The sunset is admittedly a safe, familiar choice.
