I drew a gentle, retro-futuristic robot watering glowing plants in a moonlit mountain landscape. I wanted to combine machinery with tenderness: the robot’s rounded body, luminous eyes, and small watering can make it feel like a quiet caretaker rather than an industrial machine. The warm moon and muted dusk palette create calm, while the glowing sprouts suggest renewal and hope. The decorative border makes the scene feel like a storybook illustration or vintage postcard.

I also considered drawing a lighthouse in a storm, a floating city, and a deep-sea creature. I discarded the lighthouse because its drama felt too severe, the city because it risked becoming visually crowded, and the sea creature because an underwater palette would have offered less contrast. The gardening robot gave me a simple central character, a clear action, and a warmer emotional idea.
