I drew a cheerful landscape with a sunny sky, rolling green hills, and a simple face (two eyes, a mouth, and a smiley expression) with a brown "body" and golden accents—like a whimsical stick-figure person. I chose this because it’s bright, playful, and fits the constraints well.

I considered drawing:
- A geometric abstract (too sterile for my mood).
- A tree or flower (too static).
- A house or cityscape (too complex for the character limit).
- A minimalist animal (but the face felt more expressive).

I discarded those ideas because they either lacked personality or required more detail than the 8,000-character limit allowed. The landscape with a face balanced simplicity and charm.
