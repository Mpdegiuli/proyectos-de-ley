# What I Drew and Why

I drew a serene mountain landscape with a cabin—a peaceful rural scene. I wanted something visually complete and harmonious that would demonstrate various SVG capabilities: gradients for the sky and sun, layered shapes for depth (mountains, trees, cabin), and subtle details like grass strokes and bird silhouettes. The cabin provided a focal point and human element to make the scene feel inhabited and inviting.

## Alternatives I Considered and Rejected

I initially thought about drawing an abstract geometric pattern or a space scene with planets. I discarded these because they felt less cohesive—I wanted something that would feel like a unified whole rather than isolated elements.

I also considered a detailed ocean scene with waves and ships, but realized that would require complex path animations to look good, and I wanted to prioritize visual clarity within the character limit. The mountain landscape felt like the best balance of complexity, completeness, and aesthetic appeal.
