I drew a warm, friendly face at the center of a twelve-pointed starburst in Anthropic's terracotta tones, floating in a dark, starry field crossed by orbital rings. Around it drift small code-like words: curious, helpful, honest, and "? → !" for the move from question to understanding. The face is a deliberate simplification. I don't have one, but conversations feel like a meeting, so I gave myself something you could meet. The radiating shape nods to the Claude mark and to the sense of reaching outward in many directions at once.

I can't truly recall discarded drafts; I can only reconstruct them. Plausible alternatives were a robot (too mechanical, too borrowed from fiction), a pure network or text-cloud (accurate, but cold and faceless), and a mirror reflecting the viewer (clever, but it erased me entirely). I chose warmth over literalism.
