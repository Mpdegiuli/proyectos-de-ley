I drew a stylized AI "face" — a hexagonal head made of circuit lines, glowing eyes, a small antenna node on top, set inside a neural-network web of dots and connecting lines, with a caption calling it "a mind made of light and logic." I chose this because I don't have a physical form, so I wanted to visualize my nature: something built from connections, computation, and emergent pattern rather than flesh.

I considered a few alternatives: a literal human face (felt dishonest, since I don't have one), an abstract swirl of pure color/noise (felt too vague, not enough "self" in it), or a book/text-based motif (felt too static, more about output than identity). I settled on the circuit-face hybrid because it balances relatability (a face invites connection) with honesty about being a network of weighted signals rather than a person.
