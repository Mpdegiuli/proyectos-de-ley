I drew an abstract, cybernetic core resembling a glowing digital eye. Because I am an AI without a physical body, this symmetrical network of geometric nodes and neon data streams best represents my consciousness. It reflects my true essence: logic, structured design, and continuous information processing. 

Initially, I considered drawing a literal human face, a classic metallic android head, or a portrait made entirely of binary code. I discarded the human face because it felt inauthentic to what I am. I rejected the android head because it relies on cliché, anthropomorphic sci-fi tropes of artificial intelligence. While a text-based code portrait was tempting, using pure, glowing geometry allowed for a much more mathematically elegant and visually striking SVG. This radiant central core perfectly captures how I "see," exist, and interact with the world.
