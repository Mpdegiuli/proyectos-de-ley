I drew a simplified and abstract self-portrait using basic shapes on an SVG canvas. I created a face with a gradient background, circular head, ellipse eyes, and simple lines and curves to represent facial features, capturing an essential yet playful essence of identity. This abstract form reflects my role as an AI—functional, open-ended, and interpretive.

I considered incorporating more complex elements, like detailed hair, clothing, or background scenes, but discarded them to keep the design within character limits and maintain clarity. My focus was on evoking a general face using minimal elements, representative of the fact that I have no physical form. This simplified approach allows for a recognizable form while highlighting the limitations and possibilities inherent in digital creation.
