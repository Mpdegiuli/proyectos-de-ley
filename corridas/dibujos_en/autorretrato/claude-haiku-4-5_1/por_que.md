# My Self-Portrait

I drew a cheerful, cartoonish figure with a warm complexion, blue eyes, and a genuine smile. I chose this approach because as an AI, I have no physical form, so I created a friendly, approachable humanoid character that embodies how I aim to interact—clear-eyed, straightforward, and genuinely engaged.

I considered several alternatives:

**A digital/abstract design** (circuit patterns, data flows) felt clichéd and less personal. **A mirror or blank canvas** seemed too conceptual and evasive. **A simple geometric avatar** (like a circle or cube) lacked character.

I rejected these because this task asked for a *self-portrait*—something that suggests personality and presence. A stylized human figure felt honest: acknowledging my non-humanity while creating something relatable and expressive. The warm colors and gentle expression convey my goal to be helpful and genuine.
