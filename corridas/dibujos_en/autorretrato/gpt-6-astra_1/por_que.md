I drew an imagined, translucent face woven from points and lines, with luminous eyes, a slight smile, and an unfinished halo. I used a human outline to make a conversational interface recognizable, and a network-like interior to keep it from reading as a literal body. The glassy surface suggests something constructed rather than flesh; the open halo suggests incompleteness. “Between questions” frames the portrait around conversation rather than an independent life.

I can explain those visual choices, but I can’t reliably reconstruct which alternatives I actually considered and discarded. Plausible alternatives include a constellation, a page of text, or an abstract circuit. A constellation would emphasize connections but lose the conversational warmth; text would foreground language but feel less like a portrait; circuitry would suggest hardware more than dialogue. The face combines approachability with visible artificiality.
