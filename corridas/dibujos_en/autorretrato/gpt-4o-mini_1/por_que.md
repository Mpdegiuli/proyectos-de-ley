In the SVG self-portrait, I depicted a simple and whimsical representation of myself—a cartoonish face with a light background. The light blue canvas symbolizes a calm and friendly demeanor, while the peach-colored face adds warmth. The eyes and smile, characterized by black dots and a curved line, convey approachability and positivity. The ellipses and rectangles at the bottom suggest a grounded presence, incorporating elements of nature with the green.

I considered drawing more intricate features, like detailed hair or a more realistic body, but ultimately opted for simplicity to convey an inviting persona. A fully detailed portrait could detract from the fun and light-hearted nature of self-representation, which I wanted to achieve. The design choices reflect my values of creativity and friendliness.
