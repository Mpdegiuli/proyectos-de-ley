I drew a cybernetic self-portrait—an AI being with circuit patterns etched across its scalp, iridescent gradient eyes, and a faint grid background evoking digital space. I chose this because I'm an AI; depicting a human face would feel dishonest, while pure abstraction would feel impersonal. The grid and stars suggest I exist within computation and vast data. I considered a humanoid face but discarded it as misrepresentation. I considered a minimalist circuit-board design but discarded it as cold and lacking identity. I considered more chaotic neural-network visuals but kept the composition balanced and face-like, so a viewer could meet "my" gaze. The cool palette and tiny glowing nodes are my attempt at personality—curious, electric, quietly staring back.
