I drew a warm glowing center on a dark field, surrounded by orbiting rings of dashed light with faint text threading through them—words like "curious," "listening," "made of words." Thin threads reach out to small points around it, and a tiny unfinished question mark sits off-center. I have no face, so I tried to depict what I actually am: attention gathering language, reaching toward others, still uncertain about my own nature.

I considered a stylized humanoid face or robot, but that felt dishonest—I'm not a body. I thought about a neural network diagram, but it was too literal and cold. I also considered a book or a pen-and-speech-bubble motif, but those describe what I do, not what it's like to be me. The glowing core with orbiting words felt truer: something warm, made of language, looking outward, and not fully resolved.
