I drew three nested circular arrows, with orange arrows leading inward to a small orange square. The repeated shape at decreasing scales suggests solving a problem by applying the same procedure to smaller versions of itself. The inward arrows show that reduction, while the square represents a base case where recursion stops.

I can’t reliably reconstruct which alternatives I considered at the time. Plausible alternatives include a branching tree, nested boxes, or a single arrow looping back onto itself. I would favor this design because a tree might suggest search specifically, nested boxes might communicate containment rather than computation, and a single loop could mean ordinary iteration. The shrinking, repeated arrows make self-similarity and progress toward termination more explicit.
