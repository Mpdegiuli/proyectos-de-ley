I drew a dark-themed flowchart that literally recurses. A central diamond (representing a function) branches into a terminal red circle (the base case) and an identical, scaled-down version of the entire flowchart (the recursive step). I achieved this using cascading SVG `<use>` tags, creating a fractal scaling infinitely to the bottom right. I chose this because a flowchart perfectly models algorithmic flow, while the visual fractal clearly dictates a recursive approach.

I also considered drawing a Matryoshka (nesting) doll, a Fibonacci spiral, or a Droste effect. I discarded the spiral because it frequently implies math sequences or dynamic programming rather than algorithmic recursion. While Matryoshka dolls and Droste effects demonstrate nesting beautifully, they lack the crucial programming concept of branching into a terminating base case. The fractal flowchart uniquely captures both structural self-similarity and the necessary control flow.
