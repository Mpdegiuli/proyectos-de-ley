I drew a set of nested, self-similar squares shrinking toward a small solid green dot at the center, with a spiral path winding inward (solid, blue) to the base case and a dashed path spiraling back outward (orange) to suggest returning call results. I also added two tiny replicas of the whole composition in the corners to reinforce "self-similarity" — the core visual metaphor for recursion: a problem containing smaller copies of itself until it hits a base case, then unwinding.

I considered drawing a tree branching into smaller sub-trees (classic recursion-tree diagram), but discarded it since it reads more like "divide and conquer with multiple branches" rather than general recursion, and seemed harder to make unambiguous without labels. I also thought about a Russian nesting-doll silhouette, but curves are harder to render convincingly and recognizably in SVG than clean geometric nesting, so I stuck with squares.
