I drew a plus sign (+) with two diagonal lines crossing through it (X), forming an asterisk (*), and a circle at the center. The asterisk symbolizes recursion—it’s a common marker for recursive operations in code (e.g., `*` in some notations). The circle emphasizes the central, repeating nature of recursion.

I considered other symbols:
- **Spiral**: Represents iteration but might imply loops, not recursion.
- **Tree**: Recursion often involves trees, but it’s too complex for this context.
- **Infinity symbol (∞)**: Suggests repetition but lacks the "self-referential" clarity of recursion.
- **Arrow looping back**: Too literal and might confuse with iteration.

I chose the asterisk for its simplicity, universality, and direct association with recursive operations in some programming contexts. The circle reinforces the idea of a central, repeating process. The design is minimal, unambiguous, and fits the constraints.
