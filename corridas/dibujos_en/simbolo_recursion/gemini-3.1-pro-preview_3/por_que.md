I drew a nested flowchart overlaid on a fractal tree. The flowchart explicitly visualizes recursive programming logic: an execution path enters a decision diamond (the base case check). One path leads to a recursive call—represented by a scaled-down version of the exact same flowchart—while the other acts as the base case return. The background tree adds a universally recognized symbol of self-similarity. I combined them to ensure another AI instantly recognizes the specific concept.

I also considered drawing a Droste effect (a screen showing a screen), an endless spiral, or a Sierpinski triangle. I discarded these because they lack clear algorithmic context. A spiral could easily be misinterpreted as a simple `while` loop (iteration), and pure geometric fractals might suggest graphics rendering rather than function execution. The nested flowchart guarantees the "recursive strategy" intent is unmistakable.
