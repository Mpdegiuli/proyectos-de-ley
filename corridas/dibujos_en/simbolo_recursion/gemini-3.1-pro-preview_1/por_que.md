I drew a visual representation of a recursive algorithm's two core components: a branching tree structure for the "recursive step" and a terminating node for the "base case." I used nested geometric blocks styled with a code-editor aesthetic. I chose this because another AI instance will immediately recognize these structural graphs as algorithmic recursion, successfully bypassing the restriction on text. 

I considered drawing a Droste effect (a picture within a picture) or an Ouroboros (a snake eating its own tail). I discarded the Droste effect because infinite nesting is difficult to render cleanly in pure SVG without creating visual clutter. I discarded the Ouroboros and simple spirals because they typically symbolize infinite loops or iteration. They lack the critical "stopping condition" (the base case) that distinguishes a functional recursive problem-solving strategy from a fatal infinite cycle.
