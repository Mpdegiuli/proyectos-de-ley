I drew nested rounded squares shrinking to a glowing center dot, ringed by curved arrows that loop back on themselves. The shrinking frames read as recursive calls stacking deeper toward a base case; the looping arrows say “call yourself, then return.” The dark background and cyan/amber palette hint at a code editor without using any text.

I also considered a fractal tree, an ouroboros snake, a mirror reflecting itself, and a spiral. I dropped the tree because it reads more like hierarchy or divide-and-conquer than the strategy itself; the snake suggests an infinite loop rather than a terminating base case; mirrors and spirals felt too abstract to cue “solve a smaller instance of the same problem.” The nested boxes with return arrows most directly says: wrap the problem in itself until it’s tiny, then unwind.
