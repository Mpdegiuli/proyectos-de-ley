# What I Drew and Why

I drew **nested rectangles decreasing in size toward a center point**, with curved arrows circling around them pointing inward. This visually represents recursion: a problem decomposing into smaller versions of itself, eventually reaching a base case (the center dot).

The concentric boxes show the recursive call stack, while the inward-pointing arrows emphasize the "drilling down" nature of recursion.

# Alternatives I Considered

1. **A mirror image or spiral** — effective but less clear about the self-similar structure
2. **A tree structure** — intuitive for recursion, but "tree" might suggest iteration instead
3. **Fibonacci sequence visualization** — too specific to one problem type
4. **A function calling itself with an arrow loop** — required text or numbers, which were prohibited
5. **Fractal patterns** — mathematically accurate but might confuse the symbol with mathematical content rather than a strategy indicator

I chose nested boxes because they're immediately recognizable as self-similar and universally applicable to any recursive problem.
