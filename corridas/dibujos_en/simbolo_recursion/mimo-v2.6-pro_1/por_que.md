I drew nested, self-similar rounded frames shrinking toward a golden diamond at the center, with curved arrows descending from each frame into the next smaller copy, plus a dashed self-referential loop circling the whole thing. The nesting reads as "same shape, smaller input"; the arrows mean the function calls itself; the diamond marks the base case; the loop says self-reference.

I considered a branching tree (clearest "divide and conquer," but it reads as tree traversal, not recursion), a Möbius/ouroboros snake eating its tail (strong self-reference, no base case or shrinking problem), an infinite mirror corridor, and a fractal spiral or Koch curve. I discarded those as less literal: the tree lacks self-reference, the snake lacks progress toward termination. I nearly added droste-style frames entering a mirror, but the shrinking hierarchy plus call arcs already communicates recursion more directly.
