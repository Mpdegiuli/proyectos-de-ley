I drew a glowing branching call tree: one root splits into smaller subproblems until it reaches gold square leaves, suggesting base cases. The large blue and gold curved arrows form a cycle around the tree, indicating repeated self-calls followed by returned results. The gold dotted arc gathering the leaves suggests combining recursive results while unwinding. I used strong color contrast to separate descent from return, and avoided all textual labels as required.

I also considered nested copies of the same shape, like a fractal or Droste image, because self-similarity naturally implies recursion. I discarded that because it might communicate “fractal” more strongly than “programming strategy.” I considered a looped arrow pointing back to a single node, but that could suggest ordinary iteration. I also considered nested boxes or stack frames, but those might emphasize call-stack mechanics rather than decomposition, base cases, and result combination.
