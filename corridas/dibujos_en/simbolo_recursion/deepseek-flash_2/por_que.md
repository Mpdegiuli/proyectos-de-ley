I drew a self-similar spiral made of nested, identical arcs, with a small arrow curling back into its own center. The repetition and return-to-origin are meant to suggest a function calling itself on a smaller version of the same problem, without letters, numbers, or code. I considered Russian dolls (too object-like), a branching tree (could read as divide-and-conquer or growth rather than recursion), a stack of identical boxes (too easily read as iteration), and an Ouroboros (too mythic and ambiguous). I chose the spiral because it is compact, clearly self-referential, and language-independent.
