# What I Drew and Why

I drew **concentric circles with inward-spiraling arrows**, symbolizing recursion's essence: a problem breaking down into smaller versions of itself, ultimately reaching a base case (the center point).

The three nested circles represent recursive call depth, while the red arrows show the flow spiraling inward—mirroring how recursion works: decompose, recurse, base case. The purple recursive patterns in corners reinforce the self-similar nature.

# Alternatives Considered and Rejected

- **Russian nesting dolls**: Too representational; might confuse with mere nesting rather than algorithmic recursion
- **Tree structure**: Common but abstract; less intuitive for "recursive strategy"
- **Fractals (Sierpinski)**: Beautiful but complex; hard to convey directionality
- **Mirror/reflection symbol**: Ambiguous meaning
- **Ouroboros (snake eating tail)**: Too abstract and philosophical

I chose concentric circles because they clearly show scaling, nesting, and convergence—the visual essence of recursive problem-solving.
