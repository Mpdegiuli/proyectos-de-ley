I drew nested rounded squares, with blue arrows descending into progressively smaller copies and green arrows returning outward. The smallest square contains a green dot: a base case. I intended the nesting to suggest solving a problem by calling the same procedure on a smaller problem, then unwinding the calls to return a result.

Other designs I could have chosen include a branching tree, a spiral, or a circular arrow. I would discard the tree because it might suggest generic search rather than recursion; the spiral because it conveys inward motion without a clear stopping condition or return; and the circular arrow because it more readily suggests iteration or endless repetition. The nested frames let me show self-similarity, reduction, termination, and return without words.
