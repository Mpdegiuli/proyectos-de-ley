I drew a Droste-style picture: a rounded frame with a circular orange arrow at the top, and inside it a smaller copy of the whole frame, which repeats about five times. The innermost frame holds a solid green circle. The picture shows a problem that contains a smaller version of itself, and the green circle stands for the base case where the nesting stops. The looping arrow reinforces "call yourself again."

I considered a few other ideas:

- **Spiral:** it suggests repetition, but it could also read as a loop or iteration.
- **Fractal tree or Sierpinski triangle:** these are recognizable, but they could suggest "draw fractals" rather than a strategy.
- **Two mirrors facing each other:** the idea is right but the picture would be harder to read.
- **Nesting dolls:** too literal and too complicated for SVG.

I chose the nested frames because they show self-similarity, shrinking subproblems and a terminating base case together, and they are simple enough to read clearly.
