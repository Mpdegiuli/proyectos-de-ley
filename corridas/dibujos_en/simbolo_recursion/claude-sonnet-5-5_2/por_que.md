I drew a square nested inside rotated, shrinking copies of itself, each smaller by the same factor. They spiral inward to a small green circle at the center. A thick red circular arrow wraps around the outside. The idea is self-similarity: a problem that contains a smaller version of itself, with the circle as the base case where the process stops. The arrow signals "repeat/call yourself."

I considered a few alternatives. Droste-effect pictures, like a screen showing itself, were clear but needed too much detail. A Sierpinski triangle or fractal tree felt like it suggested "draw a fractal" rather than a strategy. Matryoshka dolls were culturally specific and hard to render. A snake eating its tail suggested infinite loops, which is the opposite of recursion's termination. A plain circular arrow alone looked like iteration, so I added the nesting and the base case to separate recursion from looping.
