I drew nested squares that shrink toward a small red square at the centre. Each level repeats the same shape at a smaller scale, which I meant to suggest a problem containing a smaller copy of itself. The red centre stands for the base case where recursion stops. The two dashed red curves along the right and bottom edges hint at a call unwinding back outward. A zero-width path near the top-left is a leftover that renders nothing.

I considered a Russian doll, but rejected it because it suggests nesting data rather than self-invocation. A spiral reads as iteration, so I dropped it. An ouroboros suggests a cycle that never ends, which could signal an infinite loop. A fractal tree or Sierpinski triangle looked too intricate to read clearly at 400 pixels and risked reading as decoration. Nested squares were the simplest option that still conveyed self-similarity and a clear stopping point.
