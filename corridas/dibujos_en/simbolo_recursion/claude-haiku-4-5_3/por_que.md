# What I Drew and Why

I drew **nested circles with converging arrows**, representing recursion visually. The arrows point inward from the outer circle through a middle circle to a solid center, showing how a problem breaks down into smaller instances of itself until reaching a base case (the filled center dot).

# Alternatives Considered and Rejected

1. **Russian nesting dolls**: Too representational; lacks the algorithmic clarity needed for a programming concept.

2. **A tree structure**: Common for recursion, but I wanted something more abstract and universal—trees suggest specific algorithms rather than the general recursive principle.

3. **A spiral**: Evokes infinity and self-similarity, but seemed less clear about the "solving" aspect than converging arrows.

4. **Fractal patterns**: Beautiful but too complex; the nested-circles-with-arrows approach is immediately recognizable as a reduction to a simpler problem.

The final design balances visual clarity with conceptual accuracy.
