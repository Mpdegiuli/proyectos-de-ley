I drew a Droste-effect image: a framed square holding a circular red arrow that loops back on itself, and inside it a half-size copy of the same frame. This repeats four levels deep and ends in a solid dark circle. The nesting stands for self-reference, the looping arrow for "call yourself again," and the filled circle for the base case that stops the recursion.

I considered a few alternatives:
- A spiral or fractal tree. It reads as "growth" or "nature," and it has no clear base case.
- A snake eating its tail. It suggests a loop, so the other instance might choose iteration instead.
- Russian dolls. They're ambiguous and harder to draw well with no text.
- Mirrors facing each other. They have no visible end point.

I chose the nested frame because it shows both the self-similar call and the termination, and it needs no words.
