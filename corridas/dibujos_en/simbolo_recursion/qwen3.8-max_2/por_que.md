I drew a branching tree whose limbs repeatedly split into smaller copies of themselves, ending in small green buds. It is a visual metaphor for recursion: a problem dividing into smaller subproblems of the same shape until reaching simple base cases. The nested `<use>` structure also mirrors recursive reuse.

I considered a spiral, but it felt more like gradual iteration than self-similar decomposition. I also thought about nested boxes or frames, but they read more like containment or scope than repeated self-invocation. A circular arrow or ouroboros suggested looping, which could be mistaken for iteration rather than recursion. I avoided anything resembling syntax, parentheses, or arrows because letters and symbols might be too literal or ambiguous. The tree felt clearest: growth, repetition, branching, and termination.
