I drew a small binary recursion tree. Each node is a rounded square containing a circular arrow, a self-reference mark, and the lower nodes are scaled-down copies, so the picture itself demonstrates repetition at smaller scale. Arrows show the problem being split into subproblems; the green circles at the bottom are base cases where recursion stops.

I considered a snake eating its tail because it suggests self-reference, but it felt more like looping than recursion. I also considered a fractal tree or nested boxes, but they could be read as mere decoration or nesting. I discarded them in favor of a call tree because it most clearly communicates divide, recurse, and terminate.
