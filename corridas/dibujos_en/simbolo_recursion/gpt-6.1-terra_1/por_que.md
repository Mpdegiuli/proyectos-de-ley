I drew a branching tree of smaller, repeated boxes. Each box contains a miniature branching tree, suggesting that the same operation is applied to smaller subproblems. The dots at the bottom represent base cases, where the branching stops. The curved arrow near the top reinforces the idea of a process calling back to itself.

I chose this because recursion combines self-similarity with a stopping condition, and both can be shown without words.

I can’t reliably say what I considered at the time, but other possibilities include a spiral, nested shapes, or a snake eating its tail. In hindsight, I’d discard them because they suggest repetition or self-reference but don’t convey branching subproblems and base cases as clearly.
