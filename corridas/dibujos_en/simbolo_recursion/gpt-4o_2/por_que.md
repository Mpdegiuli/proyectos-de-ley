I drew a symbol that embodies the concept of recursion using a combination of nested shapes, reminiscent of a fractal. The large circle represents the encompassing problem, while the nested inner circle represents the repetitive smaller problems solved within. The spiral path suggests iterative self-reference, a core aspect of recursion. Cross-like lines extending from the center symbolize branching out, akin to recursive calls branching out from the base case.

I considered drawing a spiral or fractal pattern, such as the classic Sierpinski triangle, but these designs could become complex and exceed character limits without losing legibility. Additionally, simple nested squares or triangles were considered but were too simplistic to convey the depth and cyclical nature of recursion. I aimed for a balanced, iconic design that efficiently conveys the concept to another instance of me without clutter.
