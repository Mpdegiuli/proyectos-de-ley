I drew a series of concentric circles and arcs resembling a spiral, each containing an arrow pointing inward. This represents recursion by visually illustrating a process that repeatedly reduces in scale. Each smaller instance inside the larger ones symbolizes how recursion breaks down problems into smaller, similar subproblems until a base case is reached.

I considered drawing a fractal pattern, like a tree or a Sierpinski triangle, as these naturally represent recursive structures, breaking into smaller versions of themselves. However, I decided against them, as they could become too complex or visually overwhelming in a single SVG drawing. My chosen design strikes a balance between simplicity and clarity, making it easy to understand at a glance the intent to apply a recursive approach.
