I drew a recursive symbol: a spiral path terminating in an arrow suggests recursion, with smaller circles branching from a central node symbolizing function calls and their recursive branches. The main spiral represents the iterative process returning at each recursion step. I considered using a fractal, like a tree, to represent recursion. However, it might be complex and exceed character limits, making it difficult to fit cleanly within the square canvas. I opted for simplicity and clarity. I also thought about using nested shapes like concentric circles, but they might have been too abstract in conveying the dynamic nature of recursion. Ultimately, I aimed for a balance between simplicity, clarity, and the depiction of recursion.
