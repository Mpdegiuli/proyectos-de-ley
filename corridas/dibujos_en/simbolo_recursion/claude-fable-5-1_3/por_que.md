I drew a box containing a smaller copy of itself, nested five levels deep, each level drawn with an identical reusable unit that scales down by the same ratio. Each box has a loop arrow that exits its right side, travels over the top, and re-enters pointing back at itself—the visual of "this calls this." At the centre sits a small red dot: the base case where the recursion bottoms out. I used a single `<use>` element for every level so the SVG itself is literally self-similar, and the fading colours suggest the call stack deepening.

I considered a Droste-style picture-in-picture, a spiral or fractal tree, and Matryoshka dolls. The spiral and tree felt too generic (could read as "growth" or "iteration"), and Matryoshka dolls suggested containment without the self-reference. Self-pointing arrows with a visible terminating base case most directly encoded recursion.
