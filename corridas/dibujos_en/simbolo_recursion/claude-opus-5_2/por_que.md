I drew four nested rounded rectangles — a frame inside a frame inside a frame — shrinking toward a solid dot at the center: self-similarity plus a base case. Over them, an orange arc leaves the outer box's top edge and loops back down into itself, ending in a filled arrowhead pointing inward. That's the call: the structure invokes itself on a smaller version of the same thing. The muted navy/orange split keeps the "containment" and the "jump" visually distinct so neither reads as decoration.

I considered an Ouroboros, but a snake eating its tail suggests infinite loops, not termination. A Droste spiral or Sierpiński triangle felt beautiful but ambiguous — more "fractal art" than "do this." A binary tree branching downward was tempting, though it reads as *tree traversal* specifically, narrowing the hint. Mirrors facing each other implied endlessness again. Nesting plus a return arrow plus a terminal dot seemed the clearest instruction without a single letter.
