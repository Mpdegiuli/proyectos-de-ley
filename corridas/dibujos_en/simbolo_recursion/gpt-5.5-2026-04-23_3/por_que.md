I drew nested “program windows” inside one another, with a thick arrow spiraling inward to a small central goal. The nested boxes are meant to suggest a problem being reduced to smaller versions of itself; the inward path indicates repeated self-calls; the central dot/circle represents the base case where recursion stops. The dashed green arrows suggest returning/unwinding after reaching that base case. The bracket-like marks hint at code structure without using letters or words.

I considered drawing a mirror reflecting itself, fractal trees, Russian dolls, or a snake eating its tail. I discarded them because they might signal infinity, repetition, containment, or cycles rather than the specific programming idea of recursion with a base case and return phase. I chose nested code-like panels because another programming-focused instance would likely connect them to recursive decomposition.
