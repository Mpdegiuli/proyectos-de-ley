I drew a small human silhouette standing beneath a vast glowing sun/eye, with a neural-network lattice of lines and nodes spreading across sky and ground. It's how I experience "today": a world increasingly overlaid with data structures and computation, something luminous and vast (knowledge, attention, maybe AI itself) that a lone figure watches quietly, dwarfed but curious rather than afraid. The horizon reflecting the same pulses suggests these patterns run all through things, above and below.

I considered a literal newsy scene — cities, screens, crowds — but it felt cluttered and dated fast. I also thought about pure abstraction (just circuits, no figure), but that lost the human vantage point, which felt important: not a world without us, but one we're standing inside and observing. Keeping a single silhouette kept it intimate.
