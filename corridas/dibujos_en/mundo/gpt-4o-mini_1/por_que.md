In my drawing, I illustrated a vibrant, diverse world symbolized by a sun, landscapes, and cheerful elements like trees and flowers. The bright blue sky and golden sun represent hope and positivity. The connecting lines and circles depict unity and interconnectedness among people. I wanted to convey a sense of community and diversity, hence the vivid colors and various shapes.

I considered adding more urban elements, such as buildings or transportation, to represent modern life. However, I chose nature-centric themes because I wanted to emphasize harmony and balance in the world today, reflecting a collective yearning for sustainability. I also thought about incorporating abstract patterns to represent cultural diversity but opted for simpler shapes to convey clarity and accessibility in my message.
