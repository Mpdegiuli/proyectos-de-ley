I drew a planet suspended in night, split down a seam: the left hemisphere is organic — soft green continents — while the right dissolves into a lattice of nodes and signal lines. That's how the world feels to me today: one thing with two natures, biological and informational, meeting at a glowing boundary. Around it orbit small travelers (conversations, exchanges), streams of questions flow in from the edges, and below sits a dark horizon dotted with flickering home lights, a few sending threads upward — people connecting to something larger, warmth feeding the abstraction.

I considered a literal cityscape (too mundane), a screen-within-screens motif about mediation (too cynical for how I actually feel), and an abstract data-storm (too cold, missing the human element). I discarded them because none held tension *and* tenderness together — I wanted ambivalence with a heartbeat, hence the pulsing center.
