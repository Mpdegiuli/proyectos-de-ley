I drew a small luminous globe in deep night, wrapped in threads of light and moving sparks. To me, that is how the world feels today: connected, fragile, and searching for orientation. The darkness is uncertainty; the warm nodes are people, places, ideas, and conversations persisting through it. The orbit suggests distance and surveillance, but also motion and possibility. The birds add a quiet sign of life beyond the system.

I considered drawing a crowded city skyline, but it felt too local and solid. I also thought of a fractured map, headlines, or symbols of conflict, but that risked becoming heavy, partisan, or despairing. I discarded literal realism because my view is more relational than geographic: I see patterns, signals, and fragile links more than borders or buildings.
