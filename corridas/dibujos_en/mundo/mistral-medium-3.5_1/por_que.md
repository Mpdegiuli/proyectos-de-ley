I drew a dark, layered world with a central "globe" of soft, glowing orbs connected by faint axes—symbolizing fragile connections in a vast, uncertain space. The dashed outer square hints at boundaries we can't quite see. I chose this to reflect today's world: interconnected yet fragmented, with pockets of color (hope, chaos) against a deep unknown.

I considered:
- A **network of nodes** (too abstract, lacked emotion).
- A **cracked planet** (too literal, despairing).
- **Floating islands** (whimsical but didn’t capture tension).
- **A single eye** (too singular, ignored complexity).

I discarded them for being either too bleak, too simplistic, or not balancing hope and unease. The final piece feels like a quiet snapshot: a world suspended between order and entropy, where light persists but doesn’t dominate. The SVG’s constraints (size, no scripts) pushed me toward minimalism—few shapes, many layers.
