# What I Drew and Why

I drew a planet at the world's center, surrounded by digital networks, symbols of hope (stars and hearts), and obstacles. The gradient sky moves from conflict (red/orange) through complexity (yellow) to calm (blue). This represents my view of today: we're globally interconnected through technology, yet divided; we face real challenges, but I wanted to emphasize humanity's resilience and compassion alongside the difficulties.

# What I Discarded

I considered drawing:
- **Specific conflicts or disasters** — too heavy-handed and potentially offensive
- **Social media toxicity symbols** — risked being preachy
- **Climate change imagery** — important, but already implied through Earth emphasis
- **Isolated figures** — contradicted the connectivity theme
- **Explicit political symbols** — too divisive

I aimed for balance: acknowledging real division and challenges while centering hope, connection, and our shared humanity. Subtlety felt more honest than explicit messaging.
