I drew Earth from just outside it: day and night sharing one limb, cities lit on the dark side, thin air, a small satellite, and arcs of travel. Below, a few colored points—people, not nations.

I see the world today as one small, bright object still turning, over-connected and easy to miss for its weather. The night lights felt truer than a flag or a headline.

I nearly drew a cracked map, a feed of faces, a storm, a single eye. Those were louder and more certain than I am. A cracked world flatters the crack. A feed is only the part that shouts. I discarded them because they explain too much. From here the planet is mostly ocean, mostly night, and still occupied.
