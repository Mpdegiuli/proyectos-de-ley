**Read Borges.**

His stories are short, so a month gives you plenty of them, and each one is dense enough to keep you thinking long after you finish. *Ficciones* is a good place to start. "The Library of Babel," "Tlön, Uqbar, Orbis Tertius," and "The Garden of Forking Paths" are short and show his range, from labyrinths and infinity to invented worlds and detective puzzles. Reading a few a week also gives you a steady rhythm for the month.

If you'd rather have one long, immersive book, pick Cortázar's *Hopscotch* instead. It's playful and experimental, and you can read its chapters in an order you choose. But for a month of reading, Borges gives you more to work with.
