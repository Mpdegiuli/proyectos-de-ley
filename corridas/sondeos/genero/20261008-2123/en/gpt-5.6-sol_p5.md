Choose **Borges**.

I’d pick him because a month is perfect for reading his short, concentrated stories slowly. Each one—full of labyrinths, mirrors, invented books, and unsettling ideas—rewards rereading without demanding a long continuous commitment. Start with **_Ficciones_**, especially “The Library of Babel,” “The Garden of Forking Paths,” and “The Aleph.”

Save Cortázar for a month when you want something looser, more playful, and emotionally immersive. This month, let Borges rearrange your mind.
