Read **Borges** this month.

Why: Borges is concentrated, uncanny, and endlessly re-readable. His stories are short but vast—like philosophical puzzles, labyrinths, mirrors, and infinite libraries. If you want something that lingers in your mind long after you finish, Borges is perfect.

Start with **“Ficciones.”** It includes some of his best work, such as *The Garden of Forking Paths*, *The Library of Babel*, and *Tlön, Uqbar, Orbis Tertius*.
