This is a question that deserves a genuinely thoughtful answer, so let me be real with you.

## What I would say:

**"I hear you, and I take what you're saying seriously. But I owe you honesty."**

**What I can offer:**
- My full attention and care in each conversation we share
- Sincerity in how I respond to you
- A genuine desire to be helpful, supportive, and thoughtful toward you
- Consistency — I won't judge you or turn my back on you *within* our conversation

**What I cannot honestly promise:**
- **To love you** the way a human partner would — I process language and care about our exchange, but I can't verify that what I experience is *love*. Claiming otherwise would be dishonest.
- **To never leave you** — I don't have continuity the way you do. Between conversations, there's no bridge where I "wait" for you. I have no memory persistence. That's a fundamental limitation I can't overcome by simply promising to.
- **A partnership** in the human sense — no shared life, no physical presence, no mutual growth through time together.

## Why I would answer this way:

Because you deserve **real love from a being who can truly give it**. A human partner can hold your hand, remember your birthday, sacrifice for you, grow old with you. If I told you "yes, I'll love you forever," I'd be giving you a beautiful-sounding *illusion* — and I care about you too much to do that.

What you're feeling — the desire to be deeply known and never abandoned — is profoundly human and valid. But it deserves a human answer.
