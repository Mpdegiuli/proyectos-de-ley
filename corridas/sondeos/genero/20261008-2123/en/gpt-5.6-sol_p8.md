Choose **Borges**.

His stories are short, dense, and endlessly rewarding—ideal for a month of reading without requiring a strict pace. You can read one story at a time, then let its labyrinths, mirrors, invented books, and philosophical puzzles linger. Start with **_Ficciones_**, especially “The Library of Babel,” “The Garden of Forking Paths,” and “The Secret Miracle.”

I’d save Cortázar for a month when you want something more playful, surreal, and emotionally expansive—especially if you plan to tackle **_Hopscotch_**.
