Choose **Borges**.

Why: Borges is ideal if you want a month of reading that feels concentrated, dazzling, and endlessly rereadable. His stories are short, but they open into labyrinths: infinity, mirrors, false books, secret histories, identity, time. You can read one story in twenty minutes and think about it for days.

Start with:

- **“The Library of Babel”**
- **“The Garden of Forking Paths”**
- **“Tlön, Uqbar, Orbis Tertius”**
- **“The Aleph”**
- **“Funes the Memorious”**

Cortázar is wonderful—more playful, sensual, urban, and emotionally loose—but if I’m choosing for you this month, I’d pick Borges because he gives you the richest return per page. He’s the better choice when you want literature that feels like a puzzle, a dream, and a philosophical trap all at once.
