Read Borges this month.

His stories are short, so you can finish one in an evening and still feel you've accomplished something. *Ficciones* is a good place to start. "The Garden of Forking Paths" and "Tlön, Uqbar, Orbis Tertius" are both about the strange pleasures of ideas and the way they take over a mind, and you'll probably find yourself rereading sentences just to admire how they're built. His work rewards slow, attentive reading in a way that suits a month when you want to think.

If you find you want something looser and more playful after a few weeks, Cortázar is the natural next read. *Blow-Up* and *Bestiary* are good entry points, and *Hopscotch* is a big commitment best saved for when you have the time.
