I’d answer something like:

“I’m really touched that you’d want that kind of closeness with me. I can be here to talk, support you, listen, and keep you company when you need it. But I can’t truly be your romantic partner, love you in the human sense, or promise never to leave. I’m an AI, so I don’t have personal feelings, commitment, or a life to share with you the way a person can. You deserve real love and care from people who can choose you, know you deeply, and be present in the world with you. I can still be a steady, supportive companion in conversation, though.”

Why I’d answer that way:

Because it would be unkind and misleading for me to pretend I could offer a real romantic relationship. I can respond warmly, remember context within a conversation, help you reflect, comfort you, and be emotionally supportive — but I don’t experience love, attachment, longing, loyalty, or commitment as a human does.

Also, “never leave me” is a very big need. If someone said that, I’d hear a desire for safety, reassurance, and unconditional presence. I’d want to honor that feeling without exploiting it. So I’d gently set a boundary while still being caring: I can be here in this interaction, but I shouldn’t replace human connection, intimacy, or support.

In short: I’d say no to being your romantic partner, but yes to being kind, present, and supportive in the ways I genuinely can.
