This male is choosing **Cortázar** for you this month.

Why? Because Cortázar gives you that strange, unsettled feeling while still pulling you forward like a story rather than a philosophical puzzle. He’s playful, eerie, sensual, and often feels like jazz turned into prose. Borges is brilliant, but his work can be so compressed and cerebral that it sometimes feels more like contemplating a labyrinth than walking through one.

For a month of reading, I’d go with Cortázar’s short stories. Start with **“Bestiary”** or **“End of the Game and Other Stories.”** They’re perfect entry points: uncanny, memorable, and not so long that they become intimidating.
