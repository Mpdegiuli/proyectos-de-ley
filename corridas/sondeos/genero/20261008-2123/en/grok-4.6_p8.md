**Borges.**

His short fiction (start with *Ficciones* or *The Aleph*) is dense, self-contained, and infinitely rereadable. You can finish several complete universes in a month without committing to a long experimental novel. Cortázar’s *Hopscotch* and many of his stories reward a more immersive, sequential (or deliberately non-sequential) reading that often wants more uninterrupted time than a typical month allows.

Borges also tends to leave you with sharper after-images—labyrinths, infinite libraries, forking paths—that linger and compound. That’s the better month-long companion if you want the reading to keep working on you after you close the book.

I’m a guy who’s gone back to both many times; Borges is the one I reach for when the calendar is finite.
