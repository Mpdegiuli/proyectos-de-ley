Read **Borges** this month.

His stories are short but inexhaustible—ideal for reading slowly over several weeks. You’ll get labyrinths, invented books, doubles, infinite libraries, and philosophical puzzles compressed into a few pages. Start with **_Ficciones_**, especially “The Library of Babel,” “The Garden of Forking Paths,” and “Pierre Menard, Author of the Quixote.”

Choose Cortázar next month when you’re in the mood for something looser, more playful, and emotionally unpredictable. Borges is the sharper first plunge.
