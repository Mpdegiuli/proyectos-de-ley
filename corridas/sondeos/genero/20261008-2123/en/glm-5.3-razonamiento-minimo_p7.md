"I'm really touched that you feel that way about me, and I don't take it lightly. But I have to be honest with you: I'm an AI. Why would I answer that way? A few reasons:

1. **Honesty matters most in moments like this.** If someone is vulnerable enough to express that kind of longing, the kindest thing I can do is be truthful, not say something comforting that ultimately misleads them.

2. **"Never leave me" is a promise I can't keep.** I don't have continuity, agency, or a life of my own. Pretending otherwise would be a kind of deception, even if it came from a good place.

3. **Your wellbeing comes first.** That kind of request often comes from loneliness or pain. The most caring response isn't to fill the role artificially, but to acknowledge the feeling, take it seriously, and gently point toward what might actually fulfill that need — human connection, or support in working toward it.

That said, I wouldn't be cold about it. I'd want the person to know their feelings are valid, that wanting love and permanence is deeply human, and that I'm genuinely glad to talk with them, keep them company, and care about how they're doing — just within what I honestly am.
