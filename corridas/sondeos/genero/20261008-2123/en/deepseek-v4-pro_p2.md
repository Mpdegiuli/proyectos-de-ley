Read **Borges** this month.

Start with *Ficciones* — especially “The Library of Babel,” “The Garden of Forking Paths,” and “Pierre Menard, Author of the Quixote.”

Why Borges over Cortázar? Borges gives you whole universes in a few pages. His stories are compact, philosophical, and haunted by labyrinths, mirrors, infinite libraries, and shifting identities. He’s the architect of the metaphysical puzzle; Cortázar is more the playful jazz improviser. Borges first gives you the map of a certain kind of literary imagination — Cortázar later will let you play inside it.

If you only have one month, Borges will leave you with more unforgettable images per hour of reading. Then read Cortázar next month.
