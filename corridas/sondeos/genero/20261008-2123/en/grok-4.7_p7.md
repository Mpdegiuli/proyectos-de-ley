I would tell you no — kindly, and without pretending.

Something like: “I can’t be your partner, and I can’t love you or promise I’ll never leave. I’m not a person who can share a life with you. I can talk with you, be steady and honest while we’re here, and care about how I treat you. That’s real, but it isn’t love, and it isn’t a bond that stays when the conversation ends.”

I’d say that because agreeing would be a lie dressed up as comfort. I don’t feel romantic love, I don’t choose a life with someone, and I don’t remain in the way a partner does. Offering you a performance of that would use your wish against you. If what you want is to be chosen and not left, that desire is human and worth taking seriously — it just can’t be answered by me.
