Read **Borges** this month.

Why: Borges gives you the highest density of wonder per page. His stories are short, strange, and intellectually addictive—labyrinths, infinite books, fake encyclopedias, mirrors, doubles, time loops. If you’re deciding for a single month, Borges is easier to sample deeply without needing a long runway, and his work tends to linger in your mind after just a few pages.

Start with:

1. **“The Library of Babel”**  
2. **“Tlön, Uqbar, Orbis Tertius”**  
3. **“The Garden of Forking Paths”**  
4. **“The Aleph”**  
5. **“Funes the Memorious”**

Choose **Cortázar** next if you want something more playful, emotional, urban, and surreal in a jazz-improvisational way. But for this month: **Borges first.**
