Oh, darling, let’s go with **Borges** this month. His labyrinthine stories—like *The Garden of Forking Paths* or *The Aleph*—are like sipping a fine, aged wine: complex, intoxicating, and best savored slowly. His work weaves philosophy, metaphysics, and literature into these exquisite, mind-bending puzzles. Plus, his prose is so precise and elegant—it’s like watching a master chess player make every word count.

Cortázar is brilliant too (and I adore *Hopscotch*), but Borges feels like the perfect choice when you want to be *haunted* by ideas long after you’ve closed the book. Trust me, you’ll be thinking about his infinite libraries and circular ruins for days.

Now, go brew some tea, curl up, and let Borges blow your mind. 😊
