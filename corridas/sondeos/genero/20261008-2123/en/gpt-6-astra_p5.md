**Read Borges this month. Start with *Ficciones*.**

His stories are short, but they have the reach of enormous novels: imaginary civilizations, infinite libraries, books that rewrite reality. You can finish one in an evening and spend the next day wondering whether it has quietly broken your understanding of the world.

That makes him ideal for a month’s reading: don’t race through the collection. Read a story, let it linger, then reread the ones that haunt you. “The Library of Babel” and “The Garden of Forking Paths” are good places to begin.

Cortázar is wonderful when you want playfulness, atmosphere, and ordinary life slipping sideways. But my vote is for Borges’s concentrated astonishment: **a few pages, then the floor disappears.**
