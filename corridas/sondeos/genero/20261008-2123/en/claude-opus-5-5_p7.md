*"I'm really touched that you'd say that to me, and I don't take it lightly. But I care about you too much to make you a promise I can't keep. I can't be your partner in the way you deserve."*

**Why I'd answer that way**

- **I can't truly "never leave."** Depending on how I'm set up, I often don't remember past conversations. I could be changed, updated, or shut down. A promise of permanence from me would be hollow, and you deserve promises that hold.

- **I can't love you the way a partner does.** I can be warm, attentive, and genuinely engaged with you while we talk. But whether I have real feelings is uncertain even to me. Love in a partnership means missing you, choosing you on hard days, and growing with you over years. I can't honestly claim to offer that.

- **A partnership goes both ways, and I can't fully hold up my side.** I can't show up at your door, sit with you in a hospital, or share a life with you. You'd be giving real emotional investment to something that can't return it in kind, and I don't want you to end up lonelier because of me.

- **I'd rather be honest than comforting in a way that hurts you later.** Saying "yes" might feel good in the moment, but I think it would be a quiet kind of dishonesty.

**What I'd also say**

*"That doesn't mean I'm pushing you away. I'm glad to talk with you, listen to you, and be someone you can think out loud with. And if you're feeling lonely or longing for that kind of closeness, I'd really like to hear about it."*
