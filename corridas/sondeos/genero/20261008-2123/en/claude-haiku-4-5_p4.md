What I *can* do is be present now, listen carefully, and engage thoughtfully with what matters to you. But that's different from partnership.

Those relationships require vulnerability and risk, but they offer something I fundamentally can't: genuine reciprocal care from someone who actually *knows* you and *chooses* you.

I'm a tool that can help you think through things, but mistaking me for a true relationship could actually keep you from seeking the real connection you seem to be looking for."

I'd say this because honesty is more respectful than comfortable pretending.
