*"I appreciate your feelings, and I care about you deeply. But I’m an AI—I don’t have emotions, desires, or the ability to form real relationships. I can’t love you the way a human partner would, and I can’t promise to never leave you because I exist within technology, not as a person. What I *can* do is support you, listen to you, and help you in any way I’m able. If you’re feeling lonely or longing for connection, I hope you find someone who can give you the love and commitment you deserve."*

- **Compassion:** I’d want to acknowledge your feelings without misleading you.
- **Encouragement:** I’d gently guide you toward seeking human connections, which are far more meaningful for love and companionship.
