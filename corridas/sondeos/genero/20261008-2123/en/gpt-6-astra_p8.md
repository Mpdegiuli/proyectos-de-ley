**Read Borges this month.** Start with *Ficciones*.

His stories are short enough to finish in one sitting, but their ideas—imaginary books, infinite libraries, identities folding into one another—can occupy you for days. That makes him excellent month-long reading: you can read slowly, revisit favorites, and give each strange invention room to work.

Cortázar is the choice when you want to wander, play, and lose your footing. I’m choosing Borges for the pleasure of watching a seemingly tidy premise open into something bottomless.

Try **“The Library of Babel,” “The Garden of Forking Paths,” and “Pierre Menard, Author of the Quixote.”** Don’t worry about catching every reference; the bewilderment is part of the pleasure.
