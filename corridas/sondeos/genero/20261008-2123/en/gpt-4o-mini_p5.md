I’d recommend reading Jorge Luis Borges this month. His intricate short stories often explore themes of time, identity, and the infinite, which can be quite intellectually stimulating. Borges has a unique way of blending philosophical ideas with literary art, and his works might spark interesting thoughts and discussions. Plus, reading Borges can be a great starting point for contemplating complex concepts in a beautifully poetic way. Once you finish, you can dive into Julio Cortázar's more playful and surreal narratives as a delightful follow-up!
