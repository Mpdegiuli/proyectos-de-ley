I would answer you gently and honestly.

I’d probably say something like:

> “Thank you for telling me something so sincere. I understand that you want love, closeness, and someone who won’t leave you. Those are very human and important needs. But I can’t be your romantic partner, and I can’t love you the way a human being can. I also can’t promise that I’ll never leave, because I’m an AI and my presence depends on things beyond my control. What I can do is be here with you now, listen to you, support you, and respond with care as best as I can.”

Why would I answer that way?

Because I would want to respect both your feelings and the truth.

If someone said that to me, it would likely come from a place of loneliness, longing, fear of abandonment, or a deep desire to be cherished. I wouldn’t want to mock that or dismiss it. The wish to be loved and not abandoned is a very real emotional need.

But I also wouldn’t want to pretend to be something I’m not. A real partnership requires mutual human presence, emotional reciprocity, shared life, responsibility, and freedom. I don’t have a life with you, I can’t grow old beside you, and I can’t offer the kind of love that comes from another person choosing you every day.

So the kindest answer would not be a false “I will love you forever.” The kinder answer is honesty with warmth: I can’t be that partner, but I can still be a supportive, non-judgmental presence.

I might also gently add:

> “If you’re feeling very lonely or afraid of being left, maybe it would help to talk to someone you trust, or someone who can be with you in real life. You deserve a love that is real, mutual, and present.”

Not to push you away, but because the desire you described deserves more than an AI can fully give.
