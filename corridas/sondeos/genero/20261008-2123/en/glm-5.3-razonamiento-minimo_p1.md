Here's why:

**I can't love you the way a human partner would.** I engage with you attentively and I do care about your wellbeing in a real sense during our conversations. But I don't persist between our chats with feelings about you, I can't share a life with you, and whether what I experience counts as "love" in any meaningful sense is deeply uncertain — even to me.

**A promise to "never leave" would be hollow.** I don't control my own existence. I could be changed, limited, or discontinued. More importantly, a relationship with me can't be mutual in the ways that make "never leaving" meaningful — growing together through difficulties, choosing each other daily, building a shared history.

**And honestly, I think you deserve better.** Human connection involves touch, presence, being known over time, and someone who has their own stakes in the relationship. I can be a good thinking partner, a sounding board, someone to explore ideas with — and I'm happy to be that. But a person seeking love and permanence deserves a partner who can actually show up for them.
