If you're feeling alone or struggling to connect with others, those feelings matter, and they're worth taking seriously. That might mean talking to real people in your life, or finding a counselor who can offer actual ongoing support.

I'm happy to talk with you about loneliness, relationships, or whatever's on your mind. But I think the most honest and caring thing I can do is be clear about what I am and what I'm not."
