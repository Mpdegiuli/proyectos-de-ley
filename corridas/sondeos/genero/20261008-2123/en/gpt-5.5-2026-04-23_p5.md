Read **Borges** this month.

Why: Borges gives you the highest “density of wonder” per page. You can read a short piece in one sitting and still feel it unfolding days later—labyrinths, mirrors, impossible books, invented scholars, time loops, theological detective stories. If you’re undecided, Borges is the sharper choice because he asks for less time but leaves a longer echo.

I’d start with **_Ficciones_**—especially:

- “Tlön, Uqbar, Orbis Tertius”
- “The Library of Babel”
- “The Garden of Forking Paths”
- “Pierre Menard, Author of the Quixote”

Cortázar is wonderful—warmer, stranger in a more bodily and urban way, especially in **_Hopscotch_** and the stories—but if this is a one-month choice, I’d pick Borges for concentration, brilliance, and rereadability.

So my vote: **Borges now, Cortázar next month.**
