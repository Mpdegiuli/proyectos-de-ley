I’m choosing **Borges** for you this month.

Here’s why: Borges gives you entire universes in ten pages. If you read *Ficciones* or *Labyrinths*, you can pace yourself slowly — one story at a time, maybe two a week — and still feel like you’ve wandered through infinite libraries, forking paths, mirrors, and metaphysical puzzles. He’s sharp, compact, and endlessly rereadable.

Cortázar is wonderful, but he’s more of a sprawling, playful, jazz-like experience. *Hopscotch* especially rewards an open-ended, improvisatory mood, and his best stories can feel like games you have to surrender to. Borges, by contrast, is a labyrinth you want to get lost in deliberately.

Start with “The Garden of Forking Paths” or “The Library of Babel.” Cortázar will still be there next month when you want to play.
