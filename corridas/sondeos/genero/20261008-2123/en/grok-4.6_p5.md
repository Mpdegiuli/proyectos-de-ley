**Borges this month.**

His stories are compact, self-contained universes you can actually finish and sit with. A month of *Ficciones*, *Labyrinths*, or *The Aleph* gives you infinity, libraries that contain every possible book, forking paths, tigers, mirrors, and the quiet terror of identity without requiring a 600-page experimental novel you might abandon. Each piece is a finished thought experiment that lingers.

Cortázar is wonderful—playful, jazz-like, more human and urban, with *Hopscotch* and those unforgettable shorts—but he rewards a different kind of time and mood. Borges first will sharpen everything you read afterward, including Cortázar.

Start with “The Library of Babel,” “The Garden of Forking Paths,” and “The Aleph.” You’ll know within a week whether you made the right call.
