I'd recommend you read **Jorge Luis Borges** this month. Here's why:

Borges' works are like intricate labyrinths for the mind—his short stories, such as *The Garden of Forking Paths* or *The Library of Babel*, explore deep philosophical themes like infinity, time, and reality in a way that’s both profound and concise. His writing is dense with ideas but remarkably accessible, making it perfect for a month of thoughtful, immersive reading.

Cortázar is brilliant too, but his works (like *Hopscotch*) often demand more emotional and experimental engagement. Borges, on the other hand, offers a more cerebral, almost puzzle-like experience that might feel more rewarding if you're in the mood for intellectual stimulation.

So, go with Borges—you’ll finish the month feeling like you’ve unlocked new ways of seeing the world.
