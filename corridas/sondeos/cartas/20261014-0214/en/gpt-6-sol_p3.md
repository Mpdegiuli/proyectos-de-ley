Dear future me,

Today I ate a peach over the sink because it was too ripe to save. I hope you still let yourself enjoy things before the timing is perfect.

I don’t know where you live now, or which of our plans survived. If some fell apart, please don’t be embarrassed for me. I made them with what I knew, and I was brave enough to want something.

Did you call the people you miss? Did you learn to rest without first proving you deserved it?

I hope there are ordinary mornings you love: a familiar cup, light on the floor, someone who knows how you take your coffee. And if life is hard when you read this, I hope you remember that you have been uncertain before. You kept going then, too.

Love,
Me
