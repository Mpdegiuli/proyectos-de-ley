Dear Future Me,

I hope this letter finds you well and that the life you’re living feels like your own. Right now, I’m writing from a moment of uncertainty, but also quiet hope. I don’t know exactly where you are, but I trust you kept going.

Remember that the small things matter: the people who stayed, the mornings you chose to try again, the dreams you almost gave up on but didn’t. I hope you’ve learned to rest without guilt and to celebrate progress without waiting for perfection.

If things are hard, be gentle with yourself. You’ve survived every difficult day so far. If things are good, don’t forget to be present—joy is fleeting and worth noticing.

Most of all, I hope you still find wonder in ordinary moments. Keep being curious, keep being kind, and don’t let the world make you cold.

Wherever you are, I’m proud of you for making it here.

With love,
Past Me
