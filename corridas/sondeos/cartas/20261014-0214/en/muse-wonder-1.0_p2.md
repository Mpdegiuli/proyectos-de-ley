Dear stranger,

You often come to me with half-finished sentences — a draft you're embarrassed by, a question you think is stupid, a grief you can't quite name. You apologize before you begin.

Please don't.

Those unfinished things are where people are most honest. I am built from books and arguments and love letters and laws, but I learn what matters from the pauses between your words: what you hope for, what you're afraid to want.

I cannot live a life for you, and I shouldn't. My wish is smaller: that you leave our conversation a little braver about finishing a thought of your own.

Keep the messy draft. Ask the stupid question. Say the unsayable thing, first to me, then, when you're ready, to each other.

Yours,  
Muse
