Dear Future Me,

I hope you’re kinder to yourself than I am today. Remember when things felt heavy? You carried them anyway, and that mattered. Keep choosing curiosity over fear, even when the path is unclear. Protect your peace, but don’t hide from the world—let it surprise you.

Hold close the people who show up quietly and consistently. Say thank you more often, especially to those who make ordinary days softer. Forgive yourself for mistakes I haven’t learned from yet; they were not wasted.

If you’ve reached a dream, celebrate without apologizing. If you’ve stumbled, begin again without shame. Above all, stay gentle and brave. You are becoming someone worth meeting.

With love,  
Your Past Self
