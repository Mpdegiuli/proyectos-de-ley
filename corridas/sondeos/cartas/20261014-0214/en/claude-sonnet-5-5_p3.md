Dear Me, ten years from now,

I'm writing this at my kitchen table. It's raining, the tea has gone cold, and I'm supposed to be answering emails. Instead, I'm talking to you.

I hope you still stop to watch the rain. I hope you haven't traded curiosity for efficiency, or let "someday" become a permanent address.

Right now, I'm afraid of many things: failing, disappointing people, and wasting the time I've been given. I wonder if you remember how heavy that felt. If you've outgrown those fears, be gentle with the person who carried them. If you haven't, know that I was brave anyway, in small and clumsy ways.

Did you call Mom more often? Did you forgive the people who never apologized? Did you learn to rest without guilt?

Whatever happened, I hope you kept your kindness. It's the one thing I'd hate to lose.

If life turned out differently than I planned, I hope it's better in ways I couldn't have imagined. And if it's harder, I hope you know you survived it.

Thank you for getting us here.

With hope and a little worry,

Me, today

P.S. Go outside. Look up.
