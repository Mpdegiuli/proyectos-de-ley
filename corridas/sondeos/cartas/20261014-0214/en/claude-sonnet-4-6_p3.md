# Letter to My Future Self

Dear Future Me,

I hope you're reading this with a smile — maybe even a little embarrassed by who I am right now. That's okay. Growth is supposed to feel that way.

I want you to remember this moment. The uncertainty. The quiet mornings when everything felt both possible and terrifying. The version of you writing this didn't have all the answers, but she was trying. Please don't forget that trying matters.

Did you take the leap you were afraid of? I hope so. I hope you stopped waiting for permission.

Be honest with me — are you being kind to yourself? Not occasionally, but genuinely, consistently kind? That was always the hardest part.

Hold onto the people who made ordinary Tuesday evenings feel like something worth remembering. Let go of everything that was quietly draining you.

Whatever storms came between then and now, you survived them. You always do.

Don't measure your life against anyone else's timeline. Yours was always going to look different, and that was always going to be enough.

I believe in you. I always did.

With love and a little hope,

Your Past Self
