Dear Future Me,

I’m writing this on a Tuesday night in October, coffee gone cold, the house quiet except for the rain. I wonder who you are now. Did you finally take that trip? Did you learn to trust your own voice, or does it still shake when you speak up? I hope you’ve stopped apologizing for taking up space, for wanting things, for being too much and not enough all at once.

I hope you kept the wonder—the way you still notice light on water, or laugh at terrible jokes just because. That you’re kinder to yourself than I am tonight, forgiving the stumbles that feel catastrophic right now.

Whatever you’ve built, whatever you’ve let go, I hope you’re still searching. Still becoming. Still here.

Please remember this version of you—the one who believed, despite everything, that you’d be okay.

With love across the years,
Me
