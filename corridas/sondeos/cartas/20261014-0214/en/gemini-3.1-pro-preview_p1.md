Dear Future Me,

I hope you’re reading this with a smile. As I sit here writing to you, I can’t help but wonder about the person you’ve become. Have we achieved the goals we stayed up so late dreaming about? 

I hope you haven’t lost our shared sense of curiosity. More importantly, I hope you have finally learned to be kind to yourself. Remember the challenges we are facing right now—the doubts and the fears? I hope you can look back at them as the very things that built your resilience.

If life hasn’t gone exactly according to our master plan, I hope you’ve embraced the beautiful detours. I hope you still make time for good books, long walks, and the people who make you laugh until your ribs ache. 

Whatever you are doing, and wherever you are, please tell me you are happy. Keep taking risks, keep growing, and never forget the passion that got us started. I’m doing my best today so that you can have a beautiful tomorrow. 

With love and endless hope,

Your Past Self
