Dear Future Me,

I’m writing this from the past, filled with curiosity about the life you’ve built. Right now, I’m navigating a world of "what ifs," and I hope that by the time you read this, you’ve turned them into "remember whens."

First, take a deep breath. Are you happy? Not the fleeting kind, but the quiet, grounded peace that comes from knowing who you are. I hope you’ve learned to be kinder to yourself, letting go of the perfectionism that holds me back today. 

Did we take the leaps we were terrified of? Whether we soared or stumbled, I’m proud of you as long as you tried. Remember, regret weighs far more than failure ever could.

Don’t forget the little things that keep us sane: the smell of morning coffee, losing track of time in a good book, and laughing until your stomach hurts with the people who matter most. Hold onto your loved ones tightly; time is a thief, so be present with them.

Thank you for surviving the hard days we haven’t even met yet. Keep growing, stay curious, and never stop looking up at the stars. 

With love and hope,

Me
