Dear Future Me,

I hope you’ve made room for the things that matter—not just the things that feel urgent. I hope you still laugh easily, call the people you love, and remember that rest is not something you have to earn.

If life hasn’t gone as planned, that’s all right. You’ve changed before, and you can change again. Be proud of the quiet progress no one else noticed. Forgive yourself for the choices you made with what you knew then.

And if things are going well, don’t rush past them. Pay attention. Let yourself enjoy the life you worked so hard to build.

Whatever today looks like, take one honest step toward what feels true. You don’t need to have everything figured out.

With love,  
Me
