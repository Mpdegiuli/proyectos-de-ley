Dear Future Me,

I hope you're reading this somewhere quiet, with tea gone slightly cold beside you — some habits probably haven't changed.

Right now, I'm in the messy middle of things. I'm unsure about work, a little scared about money, and still learning how to ask for help. But I'm also laughing more than I expected, and I'm proud of the small, brave choices that brought us here.

Please tell me you kept the promises that mattered: to rest without guilt, to call Mum more, to choose kindness over being right. I hope you've forgiven us for the mistakes we haven't made yet.

Whatever life looks like now, remember: you were loved, you tried, and you kept going.

With hope,
Past You
