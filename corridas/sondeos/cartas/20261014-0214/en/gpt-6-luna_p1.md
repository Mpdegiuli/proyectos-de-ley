Dear Future Me,

I hope you’ve made room for the things that matter—not just the urgent ones, but the people, passions, and quiet moments that make life feel like yours.

Remember how far you’ve come. You’ve handled hard days before, often without knowing exactly how. Give yourself credit for that. And if things haven’t gone to plan, don’t mistake a detour for failure. You’re allowed to change your mind, begin again, and take your time.

Keep asking questions. Keep learning. Be kind to the people around you, and remember to include yourself in that kindness. Call the people you love. Go outside. Make something just because it brings you joy.

Wherever you are when you read this, pause for a moment and notice what’s good. Then take the next step—not every step, just the next one.

With hope,

Your Past Self
