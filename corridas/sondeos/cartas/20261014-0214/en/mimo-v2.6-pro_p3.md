**Dear Future Me,**

If you're reading this, I hope you still remember the person I am right now — uncertain but hopeful, afraid but trying.

I don't know where life has taken us. Maybe we've built something beautiful. Maybe we're still figuring it out. Either way, I'm writing to remind you of a few things.

Please don't forget the people who loved you before you had anything to show for yourself. Call them. Show up. Don't let busyness become an excuse for distance.

And please — be gentle with yourself. We spent so many years believing we had to earn rest, earn kindness, earn the right to exist fully. We didn't. We never did.

If you've lost your way, it's okay. You've lost your way before and found your way back. That's what we do.

One last thing: I hope you laugh more than I do right now. I hope the worry that lives in my chest has softened. I hope you've learned that most things work out, and the ones that don't teach you something worth knowing.

I'm proud of you — whatever you've become.

With love,
**Your Past Self**
