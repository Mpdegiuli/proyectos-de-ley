**Dear Anyone Who Feels Behind,**

I want you to know that the timeline you're measuring yourself against was invented by people who didn't know you. It doesn't account for your specific detours, your particular losses, the years you spent surviving instead of thriving, or the simple fact that you move differently than others.

There is no universal schedule for figuring things out. Some people find their direction at twenty-two. Others at forty-seven. Some never find one neat answer and instead collect a beautiful, complicated assortment of purposes. All of these are valid lives.

The comparison you're making — between your interior reality and someone else's exterior presentation — is fundamentally unfair to you. You're judging your rough draft against their highlight reel.

You haven't missed your window. Seasons don't work that way, and neither do people.

Whatever you're moving toward, slowly or uncertainly or with frequent stops — keep going. Not because the destination is guaranteed, but because the movement itself is proof that something in you still believes it's worth trying.

That belief matters more than you know.

With genuine hope,
**A fellow work in progress**
