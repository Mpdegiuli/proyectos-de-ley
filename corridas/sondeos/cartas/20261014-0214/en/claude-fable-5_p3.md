Dear Future Me,

I hope this letter finds you softer than I am now—less worried about things that never happened, more forgiving of the stumbles along the way.

Right now, I'm doing my best. Some days that looks impressive; most days it just looks like showing up. I hope you remember that effort, even if the results turned out differently than we planned.

Did you keep the promises we made? If not, be gentle with yourself. Plans change, and I trust that you changed them for good reasons—or learned something when they fell apart.

Please tell me you still laugh easily. That you call the people who matter. That you walk outside sometimes just to feel the air, without checking your phone. Those small things are what I'm trying to protect for you.

Whatever you're facing as you read this, remember: we've survived every hard day so far. That's a perfect record.

I can't wait to become you, even though I'm a little scared of it. Be proud of us. I'm doing my part back here.

With hope and stubborn love,
Me

P.S. If we finally learned to cook that one dish properly—I'm genuinely impressed.
