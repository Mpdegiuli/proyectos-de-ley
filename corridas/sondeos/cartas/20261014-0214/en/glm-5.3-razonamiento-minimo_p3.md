Dear Future Me,

I'm writing this on an ordinary Tuesday, sitting at the kitchen table with cold coffee beside me. I don't have anything profound to say, only questions.

Did you ever learn to stop apologizing for things that aren't your fault? I'm working on it. I hope you've finished, or at least that you're gentler with yourself than I am now.

Are you still in touch with Sam? Please tell me you are. Some friendships are worth the awkward phone calls.

I hope the plants survived. I hope you kept writing, even badly, even when no one read it. I hope you finally took that trip to the coast—you know the one—and that you didn't wait for everything to be perfect before you went.

Mostly, I want you to know that I was trying. Right now, everything feels uncertain, and some days are heavy, but I'm doing my best with what I have. I hope it was enough. I hope you're proud of us.

If things went wrong, I'm sorry. If things went right, remember how it felt back here, when nothing was certain and we kept going anyway.

Be kind to yourself. Water the plants.

With love,
Me (the earlier draft)
