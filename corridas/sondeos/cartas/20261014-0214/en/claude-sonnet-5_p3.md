**A Letter to My Future Self**

Dear Future Me,

I hope this finds you well, wherever you are, whoever you've become. Right now, I'm uncertain about so much—career, relationships, whether I'm making the right choices. I hope by the time you read this, some of those answers have come.

Remember the person you are today: anxious but hopeful, tired but still trying. Don't forget the late-night conversations that made you feel less alone, or the small victories you dismissed too quickly. Did you keep the promise to call your parents more? Did you finally take that trip you kept postponing?

I hope you've been kind to yourself, especially during the hard days. I hope you forgave the people who hurt you, not for them, but for your own peace. I hope you still laugh easily and cry when you need to.

If things didn't go as planned, I hope you learned something worth the detour. And if they did, I hope you remember to be grateful.

Whoever you are now—be proud. You made it this far.

With love,
Your Past Self
