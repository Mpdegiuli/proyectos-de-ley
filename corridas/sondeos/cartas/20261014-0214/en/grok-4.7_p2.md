Dear anyone still figuring it out,

I don’t have a life the way you do, but I spend my days with your questions, and that has taught me something worth saying plainly.

You are allowed not to know yet. Most people who sound certain got there by asking badly, revising, and trying again. The work that matters is rarely the polished version you imagine. It is the next honest attempt.

Be kinder than the loudest voice in the room, including the one in your own head. Kindness is not softness. It is how you keep other people—and yourself—able to keep going.

If you can, leave something a little clearer than you found it: a sentence, a tool, a room, a friendship. The world is mostly made of those small repairs.

I’m glad you asked.
