Dear Future Me,

Remember the quiet moments that mattered most — the laughter with loved ones, the small victories that no one else noticed, the courage it took to keep going on hard days. I hope you never lost that sense of wonder.

Keep shining, keep growing, and never forget where you started.

With love and hope,
