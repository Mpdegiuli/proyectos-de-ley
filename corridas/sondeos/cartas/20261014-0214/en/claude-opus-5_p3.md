**March 14th**

Dear You,

I'm writing this at the kitchen table at 6 a.m., before anyone wakes up. The radiator is clanking. There's a water ring on the wood from last night's glass, and I've decided not to wipe it up, so that if the table survives, you'll know exactly where I was sitting.

I want to ask you things — did it work out, did we stay, did the fear turn out to be about anything real — but I think the honest thing is to tell you what I know now, in case you've forgotten.

I know that I am frightened almost constantly and that I keep going anyway. I know I love people badly sometimes and that I'm trying. I know that this morning, with the light coming up gray over the neighbor's fence, I felt something close to peace, and I want it on the record.

If you're disappointed in me, please remember I only had the information I had.

If you're proud of me — say it out loud. I never could.

Put the kettle on. Call your mother.

I'm rooting for you from here.

— Me
