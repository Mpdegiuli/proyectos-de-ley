Dear Future Me,

I hope you still notice small things: the first cool morning after summer, a song that brings back an old friend, the comfort of coming home.

I hope you’ve done some of what you dreamed about. More than that, I hope you’ve made room for dreams you couldn’t imagine yet. If life has taken a different shape than you expected, please don’t mistake that for failure.

Remember how hard you worked to get here. Remember, too, that you were allowed to rest along the way. Call the people you love. Apologize when you need to. Celebrate good news, even when it seems small.

And if you’re reading this during a difficult time, take a breath. You don’t have to solve everything today. Just take the next kind, honest step.

With hope,  
Me
