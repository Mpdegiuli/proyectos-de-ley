Dear Future Me,

I'm writing this at the kitchen table at 11 p.m., the dishes still unwashed, because I'm afraid you'll forget what tonight felt like.

Right now I don't know if the job will work out. I don't know if I'll ever stop flinching when the phone rings. I'm tired in a way that sleep doesn't fix. But I also laughed so hard today that my ribs ached, and I want you to remember that both things were true at once.

Please don't be embarrassed by me. I know I'm clumsy and I say the wrong things and I keep starting over. But I'm trying, and I'd like you to be kind about it.

I hope you still call Mom on Sundays. I hope you kept the plant alive. I hope you stopped waiting for permission.

If you've become someone I wouldn't recognize, that's okay—just make sure it was your choice and not something that happened while you were looking the other way.

And if you're reading this on a hard day: you got here. That means I was right to keep going.

Go wash the dishes. Then do something you've been putting off.

With love,
Me
