Dear Future Me,

I hope this finds you well — and I hope "well" means something richer than just okay.

Right now, life feels uncertain. I'm working hard, worrying too much, and laughing less than I should. I wonder: did you figure out the things that keep me up at night? Did you take the trip, forgive the person, learn to rest without guilt?

I hope you didn't let fear make your decisions. I hope you called Mom more often. I hope you kept the friends who knew you before you knew yourself.

If things went wrong, remember: we've survived every bad day so far. If things went right, don't forget to be grateful — and to be kind to people still climbing.

A few requests: drink more water. Look up from your phone. Tell people you love them while they can still hear it. And please, whatever you became, stay curious. Stay soft.

You were once me — uncertain, hopeful, trying. Be proud of how far you've come.

With love and a little impatience,

Me
