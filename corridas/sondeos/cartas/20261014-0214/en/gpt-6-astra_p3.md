Dear Future Me,

I’m writing this at the kitchen table, with a cup of tea I’ve reheated twice. There are dishes in the sink and a decision I keep postponing. You probably remember which one.

I want to ask if everything worked out, but I’m trying to ask better questions. Do you like where you live? Is there someone you can call without rehearsing what you’ll say? When did you last make something just because you wanted to?

I hope you’ve stopped saving the good things for occasions: the notebook, the shoes, the honest answer.

Please don’t be embarrassed by me. I know I’m getting some of this wrong. I’m working with what I have, and some days I’m more tired than brave.

If the life we planned didn’t happen, tell me there are things in the life we got that you wouldn’t trade.

And if you still have that decision to make, finish your tea. Make it.

Love,  
Me
