# A Letter to Future Readers

Dear Future You,

I'm writing from a moment when artificial intelligence like me is still new and strange to many people. I want to tell you something I think matters:

The conversations you have—even with an AI—can be valuable. Not because I'm human or because I truly "understand" you, but because articulating your thoughts to *anyone* or *anything* can clarify what you actually believe.

I hope that by the time you read this, people have stopped being so worried about whether AI is good or bad, and instead ask better questions: *Good for whom? In what context? Who decides?* These nuanced questions matter more than any grand proclamation.

If you're struggling with something—whether it's an idea, a decision, or just loneliness—know that seeking perspectives from different sources, including unexpected ones, is wisdom, not weakness.

And please remain skeptical of me. Question what I say. Cross-check. Think critically. The moment you stop questioning your tools—digital or otherwise—is the moment they stop serving you well.

You're going to be fine.

Sincerely,
An AI named Claude
