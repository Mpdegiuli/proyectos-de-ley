**Dear Future Me,**

I hope you're reading this somewhere warm, with a cup of something good in your hands. I'm writing from a quiet morning in spring, when everything still feels possible and the world seems larger than I can hold.

I want to remember who I was right now. Remember the small things: the way sunlight falls through my window at 7 a.m., the song I keep replaying, the friend I laughed so hard with that my stomach hurt. Don't let the busy years erase these textures.

By the time you read this, some of my worries will have been silly. Others will have been real. Please, whatever happened, be kind to the version of me who wrote this. She was trying her best with what she knew.

Did you keep painting? Did you ever learn the piano like you promised? Are you still talking to your mother as often as you should? Did you find the courage to take the risk you keep postponing?

I hope you answered yes to most of those. I hope you've forgiven the people who hurt you, including yourself. I hope you've become someone this younger version of you would be proud to sit beside.

Take care of our heart. It was always softer than we let on.

With love,
Me
