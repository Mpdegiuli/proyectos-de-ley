Dear Future Me,

I'm writing this at the kitchen table at 11 p.m., tea gone cold, wondering who you've become. Did we ever learn to keep a plant alive? Did we call Grandma more often, like we promised?

Right now, I'm scared a lot. Scared I chose the wrong path, scared I'm not enough. If you're reading this and those fears feel silly, please remember they were real to me. Be gentle with the version of us who carried them.

I hope you still laugh too loudly at bad jokes. I hope you still stop to pet every dog. I hope success didn't make you forget how good cheap pizza tastes on a Friday night.

If things didn't turn out the way I imagined, that's okay. Plans were only ever guesses. What matters is whether you're kind, whether you're curious, and whether you let the people you love know it.

And if you've made it somewhere wonderful, thank me a little. I did the hard early miles.

Don't wait for a perfect moment to be happy. This one counts too.

With hope and a little nervousness,
Me (Past You)
