Dear Future Me,

I hope you’re reading this with a gentler heart and fewer doubts than I have today. Right now, I’m still learning how to trust the path I can’t fully see. I worry about making the wrong choices, losing people I love, and becoming someone I don’t recognize.

Please remember who we were: hopeful, imperfect, stubborn, and trying. Remember the small joys—the songs played too loudly, the quiet mornings, the conversations that made time disappear. I hope you didn’t become so busy building a life that you forgot to live it.

Did we take the risks that frightened us? Did we forgive ourselves? Did we stay kind, especially when kindness was difficult?

If life didn’t unfold as planned, I hope you found beauty in what arrived instead. And if you’re still searching, that’s okay. You’ve never needed to have everything figured out to be worthy of love or pride.

Wherever you are, pause for a moment. Breathe. Look around. You made it farther than I can imagine.

Keep going—but don’t forget to look back and thank the person who began.

With hope,  
Your Past Self
