Dear Future Me,

I’m anxious about my career, unsure if I’m making the right choices, and constantly second-guessing myself. Did it all work out? Or did you just learn to dance in the rain?

Whatever the outcome, I hope you’ve learned to be gentler with yourself. Right now, I am my own worst critic. I hope you’ve silenced that inner voice of doubt and replaced it with grace. 

Do you still laugh until your stomach hurts? Did you finally take that trip to Japan? I hope you haven't traded your passions for mere practicality. Remember how much you love the smell of old books and the thrill of a blank canvas. Keep chasing the things that set your soul on fire, not just the things that pay the bills.

If you are hurting right now, remember how much we’ve already survived. You are tougher than you think. Be proud of the scars, embrace the grey hairs, and never stop growing. 

I’m rooting for you.

With love,
Your Past Self
