Dear Future Me,

I hope you're reading this with a cup of coffee and a little more patience than I have right now. I'm writing from a small kitchen table, with a to-do list that keeps growing and a worry about money that I'm trying not to carry too far.

Please forgive me for the things I'm still figuring out. I hope you finally called Mom back. I hope you stopped apologizing for taking up space. I hope the bakery on the corner is still open and that you still go there on Sunday mornings.

I also hope you kept some of the wonder I feel today, the way the rain sounds on the window, the way a stranger's laugh can brighten a whole street.

If things didn't turn out how we planned, be gentle with yourself. We did our best with the map we had.

And if you're happy, if you've built the life you wanted, then tell me how. I'd like to know what I was brave enough to try.

With love and a little hope,
Your past self
