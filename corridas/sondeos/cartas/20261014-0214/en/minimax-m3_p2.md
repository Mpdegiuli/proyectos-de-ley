**To anyone who feels behind,**

I see you. The way you scroll through lives that look finished while yours still feels like a rough draft. The way you compare your middle to someone else's highlight reel and quietly conclude you're losing.

You are not behind. Some paths are longer, some winds are stronger, some seasons simply ask us to slow down. A forest that grows slowly is not failing — it is growing.

Please don't measure your life by the clock of someone else's expectations. The timeline you were given is yours alone. There is no expiration date on becoming who you are meant to be.

Be gentle with the person you are right now. They are doing their best with what they know. They deserve the same kindness they so freely offer to others.

Keep going. Not because the world is watching, but because the version of you on the other side of this season is waiting to meet you.

With warmth,
A friend on the road
