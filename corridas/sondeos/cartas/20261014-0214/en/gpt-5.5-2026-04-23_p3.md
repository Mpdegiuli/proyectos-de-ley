Dear Future Me,

I hope you are reading this somewhere peaceful, with a heart that feels a little lighter than it does today. I hope you kept going, even when the path was unclear, and that you learned to trust yourself more than your fears.

Please remember who you were when you wrote this: someone trying, learning, hoping, and quietly becoming. Be proud of the small victories—the mornings you got up, the kindness you gave, the times you started again.

I hope you didn’t forget the people who loved you, the dreams that once kept you awake, and the simple things that made life feel full: laughter, music, sunlight, honest conversations.

If things did not go as planned, I hope you forgave yourself. If they did, I hope you stayed humble and grateful. Either way, keep choosing courage. Keep choosing love. Keep choosing yourself.

With hope,

Your Past Self
