Dear Future Self,

As I write this, I am filled with hope and a little fear about what lies ahead. I hope you have found happiness—in work that fulfills you, relationships that nourish you, and a quiet sense of purpose.

Remember our dreams: to travel, to create, to make a difference. I hope some came true, or even better ones appeared. Life will have brought challenges. I pray you met them with courage and grew from every one.

Don’t forget to laugh, to love, and to stay present. If you are reading this, pause and notice how far we have come. Be proud of the journey.

Stay curious. Stay kind.

With all my love,
Your younger self
