**A Letter to My Future Self**

Dear Future Me,

I hope this finds you well—wherever and whoever you are now. As I write this, I'm filled with questions. Did you keep the promises we made? Are you kinder to yourself than I am right now?

I want you to remember a few things. Remember how hard you worked to get where you are. Remember the nights you doubted everything but kept going anyway. I hope those efforts bloomed into something beautiful, but even if they didn't turn out as planned, I hope you've learned that the trying mattered.

Please tell me you still laugh easily. Tell me you still call the people you love, still get excited about small things—good coffee, unexpected sunshine, a song that hits just right.

If life has been difficult, be gentle with yourself. We've survived hard things before. If life has been good, don't forget to pause and feel grateful.

Whatever has changed, I hope your heart is still curious and your mind still open. Don't become someone we wouldn't recognize—or do, if that person is braver and wiser.

I'm rooting for you. I always have been.

With love and hope,
Your Past Self
