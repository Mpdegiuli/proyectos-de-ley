# Letter to My Future Self

Dear Future Me,

I'm writing this on an ordinary Tuesday, unsure of who you've become. I hope you're proud of the choices ahead.

Remember that failure isn't final. Those moments when you feel like you're falling behind—they're not endpoints. Growth is messier than you imagine, and that's okay. You'll learn more from what goes wrong than what goes right.

Be kind to people you meet. Call your family when you're busy, especially then. The friendships that matter will survive distance and silence, but they thrive with effort.

Your body isn't your enemy. Treat it with patience, not punishment. Move it because it feels good, feed it because you deserve nourishment. Mental health is real health—take it seriously.

Don't wait for permission to start. That project, that conversation, that dream—they don't require perfect timing or complete confidence. Begin anyway.

Hold your values loosely enough to grow, firmly enough to stand for something.

I hope you're braver than I am today, yet still learning. I hope you remember this version of yourself with compassion.

You've got this.

—Past You
