Dear Future Me,

Hello from the past! I hope this letter finds you well—happy, healthy, and still curious about the world.

I'm writing to remind you of a few things that matter to me right now, hoping they still matter to you. Remember how much joy you found in the small moments: morning coffee, long walks, conversations that lasted too long but were worth every minute. Don't let the rush of life make you forget them.

I hope you've been kind to yourself. We both know how hard you can be on yourself when things don't go as planned. Please remember that setbacks are just detours, not dead ends. The dreams we have today—I hope some have come true, and I hope you've been brave enough to chase new ones.

Are the people we love still close? Tell them you love them more often. Time with them is the one thing we can never get back.

Mostly, I hope you're proud of who you've become—not because of achievements, but because of how you've treated others and yourself.

Keep learning. Keep laughing. Keep going.

With love and hope,
Your Past Self
