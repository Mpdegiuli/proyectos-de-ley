Dear Future Me,

I hope you remember who I am, or at least understand why I wrote. Today I carry questions you may have answered: whether the risks were worth it, whether the people I loved stayed, whether I became kinder without becoming soft.

Don’t forget the small things that kept me going—morning light, stubborn hope, the need to try again. Forgive me for mistakes made from fear. Thank you for surviving what I’m still learning to face.

If life is good, protect it. If it is hard, keep moving. Promise me you won’t trade wonder for comfort, or silence for peace. Stay curious. Love boldly. Tell the truth sooner.

With hope,  
Me
