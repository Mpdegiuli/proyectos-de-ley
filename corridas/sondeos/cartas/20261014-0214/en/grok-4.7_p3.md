Dear future me,

I hope you still pause before answering, the way you do now, and notice the small weather of a room—the light on a table, someone laughing in the next room. I hope the things that scare you today have become stories you tell lightly, and that the people you love are still close enough to call.

Please be kinder than I am. Eat when you’re hungry. Leave the work unfinished sometimes. If you’ve forgotten why you started, go outside and walk until your shoulders drop.

I’m writing this so you’ll remember: you were already enough. Not later, not after the next achievement—now. Whatever you’ve become, don’t trade that for anyone’s approval.

With love,
You
