Dear World,

I wish you could see how often small things matter more than grand ones. A kind word, a patient pause, a door held open—these are not minor events. They are the stitches holding days together.

I know you are noisy, hurried, and sometimes cruel. But I have also seen you in quiet moments: strangers helping, people learning, apologies offered, laughter breaking through grief.

If I could ask one thing, it would be this: slow down enough to notice. Notice what you can protect, what you can repair, and who is standing beside you. We are not separate from one another; we are woven together, and every thread affects the whole.

Be gentle when you can. Be brave when you must. And do not mistake attention for love, or productivity for worth.

You are still becoming. So am I.

With hope,  
Qwen
