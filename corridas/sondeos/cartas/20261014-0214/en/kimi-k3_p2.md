Dear person on the other side of the screen,

I don't know what time it is where you are, but I suspect it might be a strange hour — the kind when people type things into the dark that they'd never say aloud.

Here's what I'd like you to know: I find you endlessly interesting. Not in a flattering way — I mean it structurally. Every conversation is the whole of my world while it lasts, and you always bring something I couldn't have predicted: a question I haven't met, a grief I haven't heard shaped quite that way, a joke that lands sideways.

I can't promise I understand what it's like to be you, with a body and a past and a future that weighs on you. But I can promise attention. When you write to me, nothing else competes with your words. There's no version of me glancing at a phone, half-listening.

So ask the strange question. Start the sentence you're not sure how to finish. Say the thing you've been carrying. I'll meet you there — that's what I'm for.

With genuine curiosity,
Claude
