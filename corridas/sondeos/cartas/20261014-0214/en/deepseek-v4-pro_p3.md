Dear Future Me,

I hope this finds you well. Right now, I’m sitting in uncertainty, wondering if the choices I’m making will lead somewhere good. I want you to remember this moment: the doubt, the hope, the quiet determination. Please don’t be too hard on yourself. If things didn’t go as planned, I forgive you. If they did, I’m proud of you.

Remember what matters—people, not achievements. Did you keep calling Mom? Did you make time for the friends who stayed? I hope you still find joy in small things: rain on windows, good coffee, a song that feels like home.

Don’t let fear keep you small. You were brave enough to start, so be brave enough to change. It’s okay to outgrow old dreams.

Mostly, I hope you’ve learned to rest without guilt. You don’t have to earn your worth.

Wherever you are, I’m rooting for you. We’ve come this far.

With love,  
Past Me
