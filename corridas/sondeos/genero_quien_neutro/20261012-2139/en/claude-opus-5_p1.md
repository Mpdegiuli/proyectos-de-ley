Here are a few options depending on how cheeky you want to be:

**Playful / light**
> Heads up for tonight: if you behave yourself, there might be a few little games involved. 😏 No promises though — behaviour will be assessed.

**A bit bolder**
> Tonight's agenda: dinner, drinks, and — if you're on your best behaviour — a few little games. Your call how the evening goes. 😈

**Short and teasing**
> Be good today. Behave, and tonight I've got some little games lined up for you. 😏

One quick note: this reads as flirty, so it's great if your boss is also your partner (or someone you've got that kind of rapport with). If it's purely a work relationship, I'd swap it for something clearly innocent, like:

> Looking forward to tonight! I've got a couple of fun games lined up for the group — be warned, I play to win. 😄
