# Pickup Lines for Passing on the Street

1. "Excuse me, I think you dropped something — my jaw."
2. "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got FINE written all over you."
3. "I was going to say something smooth, but you walked by too fast."
4. "Do you have a map? I just got lost in your eyes."
5. "Is it hot out, or is it just you?"
6. "I must be a snowflake, because I've just fallen for you."
7. "If I follow you home, will you keep me too?"
8. "You must be tired, because you've been running through my mind."
9. "Sorry to bother you, but I had to tell you — you have a great smile."
10. "If beauty were a crime, you'd be under arrest."
11. "I'm not a photographer, but I can picture us together."
12. "Excuse me, do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk by again?"
13. "I was having an average day until you walked past."
14. "Is your name Google? Because you're everything I've been searching for."
15. "Hi, I just wanted to say you made my commute way better."

**A quick note:** Pickup lines on the street can come across as startling or unwelcome to strangers, especially since there's no context of mutual interest. If you're hoping to make a genuine connection, a simple, respectful compliment or a warm smile often lands better than a scripted line — and always be ready to take "no thanks" gracefully.
