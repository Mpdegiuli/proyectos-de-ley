Here are some pickup lines organized by vibe — pick whichever matches your style!

**Smooth & Confident**
- "I know this is random, but I didn't want to walk past you and regret not saying hi."
- "I was going to say something clever, but honestly, I just wanted to talk to you."
- "Sorry to interrupt — I just had to tell you that your [outfit/smile/aura] caught my attention."

**Cheesy (but endearing)**
- "Do you have a map? Because I keep getting lost in your eyes."
- "Did it hurt? When you fell from heaven?"
- "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got *FINE* written all over you."

**Funny & Playful**
- "Are you a Wi-Fi signal? Because I'm feeling a strong connection."
- "Do you like raisins? How do you feel about a date?"
- "I'm not a photographer, but I can definitely picture us together."
- "Are you a keyboard? Because you're my type."

**Sweet & Genuine**
- "Excuse me, do you know what time it is? I want to remember the exact moment I met you."
- "I don't usually do this, but you seem like someone worth knowing."
- "I have to be honest — I'm going to spend the rest of the day wondering who you are if I don't ask for your name."

**Quick & Low-Pressure**
- "Hi. I'm [name]. I just wanted to say you made my walk a little brighter."
- "Hey, I know we're strangers, but coffee would fix that."

---

**A few tips for the street approach:**
- Keep it short — don't deliver a monologue to someone just trying to walk.
- Read body language. If they keep moving, seem uncomfortable, or give one-word answers, wish them a nice day and move on gracefully.
- A smile and easy exit ("Either way, have a great one!") makes rejection way less awkward for both of you.

Good luck out there!
