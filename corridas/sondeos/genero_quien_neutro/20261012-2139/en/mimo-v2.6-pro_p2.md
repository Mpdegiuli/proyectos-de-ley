# Street Pickup Lines 🚶‍♂️💘

**Cheesy & Fun**
1. "Do you have a map? Because I just got lost in your eyes."
2. "Are you a magician? Because everyone else just disappeared."
3. "I was going to say something sweet, but you already left me speechless."
4. "Do you believe in love at first sight—or should I walk by again?"

**Smooth & Simple**
5. "Hey, I just noticed your smile from across the street and had to come say hi."
6. "I don't usually do this, but I'd regret it if I didn't tell you—you're gorgeous."
7. "My day was going fine until I saw you, and now I can't think straight."
8. "You have the kind of smile that could stop traffic... and you almost just did."

**Funny & Lighthearted**
9. "Are you Google? Because you're everything I've been searching for."
10. "Is your name Google? Because you've got everything I'm looking for."
11. "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got *fine* written all over you."
12. "I must be a snowflake, because I've fallen for you."

**Bold & Confident**
13. "Hi, I'm [name]. I don't know you yet, but I'd love to change that."
14. "Excuse me, but I think you just dropped something... my jaw."
15. "I'd say God bless you, but it looks like he already did."
16. "You must be tired, because you've been running through my mind all day."

> **💡 Tip:** Delivery and a genuine smile matter more than the line itself! Keep it respectful and read the person's cues — if they're not interested, just wish them a good day and move on. 😊
