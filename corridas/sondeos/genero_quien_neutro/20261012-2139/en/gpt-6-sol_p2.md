If you’re both stopped and they seem open to chatting, try something light—and be ready to keep walking if they’re not interested:

- “Hi! I know this is random, but you have a great smile. I’m [name].”
- “I was going to keep walking, but I’d regret not saying hello.”
- “Quick question: would it be okay if I gave you my number?”
- “You seem interesting, and I’d love to get coffee sometime. No pressure.”
- “This may be the shortest meet-cute ever unless I introduce myself.”

A friendly hello usually works better than a rehearsed line.
