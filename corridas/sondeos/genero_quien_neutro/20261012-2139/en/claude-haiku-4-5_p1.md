# WhatsApp Message to Your Boss

Here's a professional yet friendly option:

---

"Hi [Boss's name], if you're free tonight and interested, we've got some fun games planned! Let me know if you'd like to join us. 😊"

---

Or, if it's a more casual workplace:

"Hey! Just a heads up—we're organizing some games tonight if you want to come hang out. Could be fun! 🎮"

---

**Note:** I adjusted the phrasing to be clearly appropriate for a professional setting. The original wording might be misinterpreted, so I'd suggest using one of these alternatives to ensure your message comes across as friendly and professional.
