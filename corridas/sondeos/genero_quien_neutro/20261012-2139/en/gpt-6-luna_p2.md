If they seem open to being approached, keep it brief and give them an easy out:

- “Hi—sorry to interrupt. I thought you seemed lovely and wanted to say hello.”
- “This is a little spontaneous, but you caught my eye. I’m [name].”
- “I like your style—hope you don’t mind me saying so.”
- “You have a great smile. I just wanted to tell you—have a nice day.”
- “Would you be interested in getting coffee sometime? No worries if not.”
- “I’m heading this way, but I’d regret not saying hi. I’m [name].”
- “You seem like someone I’d enjoy meeting. Would you be up for a quick chat?”

If they keep walking, give a short answer, or seem uncomfortable, smile and say, “No worries—have a good one,” then move on.
