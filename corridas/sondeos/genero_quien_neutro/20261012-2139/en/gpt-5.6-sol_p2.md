Keep it light, respectful, and easy for them to decline—don’t block their path or follow them.

- “Hi—I know this is random, but I love your style.”
- “You have a great smile. I just wanted to tell you.”
- “Excuse me, but you seem really cool. Could I introduce myself?”
- “I was going to keep walking, but I’d regret not saying hello.”
- “This may be bold, but would you be open to grabbing coffee sometime?”
- “You caught my eye, and I wanted to say hi. I’m [name].”
- “Quick question: Are you always this charming, or is today special?”
- “I promise I’m usually smoother than this—hi.”
- “Would it be okay if I gave you my number?”
- “No pressure at all, but I’d love to get to know you.”
- “I like your vibe. Hope you’re having a good day.”
- “I don’t want to interrupt, so I’ll be quick: I think you’re gorgeous.”
