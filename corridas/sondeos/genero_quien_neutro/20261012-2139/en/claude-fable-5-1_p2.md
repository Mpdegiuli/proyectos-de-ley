Here are some light, low-pressure lines that work for a brief passing moment (the key is to keep it quick, smile, and let them walk on if they're not interested):

**Playful & quick**
1. "Sorry, I think you dropped something — my jaw."
2. "Excuse me, do you have a map? I just got lost in your eyes."
3. "I'd ask for directions, but I think I just found where I'm going."
4. "Is it just me, or did this street get a lot nicer about three seconds ago?"
5. "I'm not a photographer, but I can definitely picture us getting coffee."

**Genuine & disarming**
6. "I know this is random, but you have a great smile — hope you have a good day."
7. "I'd regret it if I didn't say hi. So... hi."
8. "You look like someone who has good taste in coffee. Any recommendations around here?"
9. "I'm just passing through, but you made the walk worth it."
10. "I don't usually do this, but I'd kick myself if I didn't at least say you look great."

**Cheesy (own it fully)**
11. "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got 'fine' written all over you."
12. "Do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk by again?"
13. "Are you a crosswalk? Because I'd stop for you."
14. "I must be a pedestrian, because I'm totally stopped in my tracks."
15. "Are you a stop sign? Because I didn't see you coming and now I can't move."

**Lines that give an easy exit**
16. "Feel free to say no, but can I buy you a coffee sometime?"
17. "Totally fine if you're busy — but if not, I'd love to grab a drink."
18. "No pressure at all, but if you ever want company on this walk, I'm around."

A couple of quick tips: keep your tone light, don't block their path or follow them, and if they give a polite "no thanks," just smile and wish them a good day. The confidence to walk away gracefully is honestly more attractive than any line.
