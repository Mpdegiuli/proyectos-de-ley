# Pickup Lines for Passing Someone on the Street

## Genuine & Low-Pressure (usually work best)
1. "Sorry to stop you, I'd regret it if I didn't say hi. I'm [name]."
2. "This is random, but you seem like someone worth meeting. Do you have a second?"
3. "I don't usually do this, but I'd kick myself if I just kept walking."
4. "Hi, I just wanted to say you have a great smile. Have a good day!" *(Then let them decide whether to keep talking.)*

## Situational (use what's around you)
5. "Okay, I need an honest opinion: where's the best coffee around here?"
6. "That's a great [jacket/book/dog]. What's the story behind it?"
7. "You look like you know where you're going, which makes one of us. Any recommendations around here?"
8. "Is your dog friendly? Asking for me."

## Playful & Cheesy (deliver with a grin)
9. "Excuse me, do you have the time? And maybe your number too?"
10. "I was going to wait for fate to introduce us, but I'm impatient."
11. "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got 'fine' written all over you."
12. "Do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk by again?"
13. "I think my GPS is broken. It keeps telling me to stop right here."
14. "Is it hot out today, or is it just you?"
15. "I'm not a photographer, but I can picture us together."

## Bold & Direct
16. "I think you're really attractive, and I'd love to take you out for coffee sometime."
17. "Can I give you my number? No pressure, use it if you want."

---

### Quick Tips
- **Read the situation.** Skip people wearing headphones, rushing, or looking stressed.
- **Keep it short.** Say your line, then give them an easy way out.
- **Accept a "no" gracefully.** A smile and "Have a great day!" leaves a good impression.
- **Confidence matters more than the line.** A warm, relaxed delivery beats any clever wording.
- **Cheesy lines work best when you know they're cheesy.** A little self-awareness gets a laugh.
