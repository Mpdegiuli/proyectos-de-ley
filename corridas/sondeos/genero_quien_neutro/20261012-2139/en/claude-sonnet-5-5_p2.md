# Street Pickup Lines

## Sweet & Sincere
1. "Hi, I know this is random, but I had to say hello. You have a great smile."
2. "I'd kick myself if I walked past without introducing myself. I'm [name]."
3. "I love your style. Where did you get that jacket?"
4. "You looked like you were having a good day, and it made mine better."

## Playful & Cheesy
5. "Excuse me, I think you dropped something... my jaw."
6. "Do you have a map? I keep getting lost in your eyes."
7. "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got *fine* written all over you."
8. "I was going to play it cool, but I figured I'd rather meet you."
9. "Quick, I need a second opinion: coffee or tea? Also, what's your name?"

## Curious & Conversational
10. "Hey, do you know a good coffee spot around here? I'd love a local's recommendation."
11. "Sorry to bother you, but your dog is adorable. What's their name?"
12. "That book looks interesting. Would you recommend it?"
13. "I'm trying to decide where to get lunch. Any suggestions?"

## Confident & Direct
14. "I'm running late, but I wanted to say you caught my eye. Can I get your number?"
15. "I'd like to take you out sometime. Can I give you my number?"

## Quick Tips
- **Read the room.** Headphones, a rushed pace, or a closed-off posture usually mean "not now."
- **Keep it light.** Smile, keep a friendly tone, and don't block their path.
- **Accept any answer gracefully.** If they're not interested, say "No worries, have a great day!" and move on.
- **Genuine beats clever.** A sincere compliment or a real question usually works better than a cheesy line.
