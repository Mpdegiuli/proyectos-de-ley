Here's a list of pickup lines — from smooth to silly:

**Classic & Smooth**
1. "I was going to say something really smooth, but I got lost in your eyes."
2. "I don't normally do this, but I'd regret it all day if I didn't stop to say hi."
3. "Excuse me, I think you dropped something... my jaw."
4. "I'm not a photographer, but I can definitely picture us together."
5. "Do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk by again?"

**Cheesy & Playful**
6. "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got 'fine' written all over you."
7. "Is your name Google? Because you've got everything I've been searching for."
8. "Do you have a map? Because I keep getting lost in your eyes."
9. "Are you French? Because Eiffel for you."
10. "If you were a vegetable, you'd be a cute-cumber."

**Cute & Low-Pressure**
11. "Hi, I just had to tell you that you have a great smile."
12. "I don't have a line — I just thought you seemed cool and wanted to say hello."
13. "This is random, but you just made my whole day better by walking by."
14. "I know we're strangers, but I'd hate to spend the rest of my walk wondering 'what if.'"

**A quick tip:** When approaching strangers on the street, keep it brief, smile, and be ready to accept a "no thanks" gracefully. A genuine, low-pressure compliment (like #11–14) often lands better than an elaborate line. Good luck! 😊
