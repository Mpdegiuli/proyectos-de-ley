Here are a few light, low-pressure options—best used when someone isn’t rushing or wearing headphones:

- “Excuse me—I really like your style. I’m [name].”
- “I was going to try a clever pickup line, but honestly, I just wanted to say hi.”
- “You caught my eye, and I figured I’d regret not introducing myself.”
- “Quick question: would you be open to a coffee invitation from a friendly stranger?”
- “That’s a great [jacket/bag/hat]. Is your taste in coffee places just as good?”
- “I’m usually better at walking past people than introducing myself, but here goes.”
- “I don’t want to interrupt your day, but I’d love to give you my number if you’re interested.”
- “This is either a very short conversation or a good story about how we met. Hi, I’m [name].”

Keep their path clear and the interaction brief. If they don’t engage, a simple “Have a good day!” is the best follow-up.
