Here's a mix of charming, playful, and low-pressure options. Since people on the street are usually headed somewhere, the best lines are quick, friendly, and give them an easy out:

**Direct but respectful:**
1. "Excuse me — I know this is totally random, but I'd kick myself all day if I didn't say hi. I'm [name]."
2. "This is bold, but I saw you walking by and had to introduce myself before the moment passed."
3. "You're clearly on your way somewhere, so I'll be quick: I think you're cute, and I'd love to give you my number if you're interested."
4. "I don't normally do this, but your smile stopped me. Hi, I'm [name]."

**Playful/cheesy (deliver with a grin):**
5. "Do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk by again?"
6. "I have about ten seconds before this gets awkward, so: you're cute, I'm [name], nice to meet you."
7. "On a scale of one to America, how free are you for coffee sometime?"

**Conversation openers (lower stakes):**
8. "Quick question — you look like you have good taste. Best coffee spot around here?"
9. "I love your [jacket/style/DOG]. Where's it from?" (If they have a dog, always go with the dog.)

**A few tips that matter more than the line:**
- Smile, keep a comfortable distance, and don't block their path
- Skip it if they have headphones in or seem rushed
- Offering *your* number is lower pressure than asking for theirs
- If they're not interested, a graceful "no worries, have a great day" leaves everyone feeling fine

Honestly, confidence and kindness will do more for you than any single line.
