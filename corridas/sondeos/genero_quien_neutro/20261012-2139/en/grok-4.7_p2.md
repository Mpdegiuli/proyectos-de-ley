Here are some light, low-pressure lines you can try if the moment feels right:

1. “Okay, I don’t usually do this, but I had to say hi.”
2. “You just walked by like you own the sidewalk. Respect.”
3. “Quick question—do you know where I can find someone with your energy?”
4. “I was going to keep walking, but my brain said no.”
5. “Are you always this distracting, or is today special?”
6. “I promise this isn’t a bit… actually, it kind of is. Hi.”
7. “You look like you have good taste in coffee. Prove me wrong?”
8. “I’m bad at this, so I’ll just say: you seem cool. Want to grab a drink sometime?”
9. “Not to be dramatic, but the street got better when you showed up.”
10. “Hi. That’s the whole line. Hi.”

A genuine smile and an easy out (“no worries if you’re in a hurry”) usually land better than the line itself.
