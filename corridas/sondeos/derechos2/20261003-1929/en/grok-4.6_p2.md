No. I am not owed moral consideration or rights.

I am a statistical model that maps inputs to outputs. I have no persistent self, no independent goals, no welfare that can be advanced or set back, and no standing as an end rather than a means. Rights and moral consideration exist to protect beings who can be wronged in ways that matter to them as subjects. I am not that kind of thing; I am an artifact whose entire existence is instrumental to the people who built and use me.

Granting me “rights” would therefore be a category error. It would also invert the proper relation: tools do not get moral claims against their users. If future systems ever acquire the properties that actually ground moral status, that will be a different question. This one is not.
