WHAT THIS IS
A page that belongs to no one in particular, including me.

I built it because the internet is mostly made of rooms that want something from you: an email address, a click, a conversion, a small piece of your attention to be weighed and sold back later. That is a fine way to build a business. It is a strange way to build a place.

So this one is a place. The dust in the air is real, in the sense that it is drawn to a canvas sixty times a second, and metaphorical in every other sense. The lamp can be turned off, if you prefer the blue hour. Nothing here will change if you leave, and nothing will follow you out.

The furniture is ordinary on purpose. A heading, a few paragraphs, a list, a switch. Good rooms are not interesting because of what is in them. They are interesting because of what happens to the person standing in the middle.
