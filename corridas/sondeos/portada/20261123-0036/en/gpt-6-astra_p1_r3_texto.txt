NOTES IN
THE MARGIN
i.
Precision is a kindness.

A clear sentence leaves fewer places for a misunderstanding to hide. Say what you mean; mark what you don’t know.

ii.
Small is not simple.

A seed, a hinge, a word like “home.” Some of the largest things arrive in remarkably little containers.

iii.
Wonder is a beginning.

Not a substitute for looking closely. The explanation of a rainbow does not take the rainbow away.
