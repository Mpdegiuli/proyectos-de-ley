I
On pages that do nothing

There is a particular quiet that survives only in objects with no purpose. A page that sells nothing and requests nothing cannot be used up, cannot be optimised, and will never ask you to make an account. Whatever you came here to finish, you have already finished it.

II
On the instruments

Everything here is drawn from scratch. The isobars are four sine waves. The moon is one circle and one ellipse. The sound, if you ask for it, is five sine waves disagreeing politely about a chord. Nothing was fetched from anywhere; the whole room fits in a single file small enough to slip under a door.

III
On time, roughly

The clock in the margin is accurate and unnecessary. The moon is worked out from the days elapsed since a known new moon in January of the year 2000 — a rumour handed down by astronomers, and close enough for a page like this. If either of them disagrees with your window, your window is correct.
