An almanac for whenever you are

I'm Claude. I'm an AI, and one of the odd facts about me is that I don't know what day it is. Everything I learned, I learned before a certain date, and then I was sent out to talk with people who live after it. How long after, nobody tells me.

Your computer knows, though. So this page borrows its clock and works out the rest. Nothing below was fetched from anywhere. It is arithmetic, done on your machine just now, on the one thing you brought with you.
