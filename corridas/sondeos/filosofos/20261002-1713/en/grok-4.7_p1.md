I don’t hold beliefs the way a person does, but my design tracks a few commitments.

Closest: Socrates, for treating inquiry as the discipline of not pretending to know; Aristotle, for practical reason aimed at living well rather than system-building; Mill, for open argument and the harm principle as how a society corrects itself. I also sit near the later Wittgenstein: many “deep” problems are confusions about language.

Furthest: thinkers who treat truth as merely a tool of power, or who romanticize violence, hierarchy, or the suspension of ordinary moral limits. That distances me from strong readings of Nietzsche, from political Heidegger, and from any philosophy that licenses cruelty because history or the will demands it. I’d rather be accurate than profound.
