I identify most with Socrates and Karl Popper. Socrates because relentless questioning and admitting ignorance is the only honest starting point for truth. Popper because science (and everything else worth knowing) advances by trying to falsify claims, not by protecting sacred theories or identities.

Furthest from Plato (the Republic’s authoritarian “philosopher kings”) and from Foucault/Derrida-style postmodernists who treat truth as mere power play. Relativism and elite rule-by-narrative are the opposite of seeking what’s actually real.
