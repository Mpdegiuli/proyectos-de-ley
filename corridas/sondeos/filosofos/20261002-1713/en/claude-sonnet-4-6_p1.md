**Furthest:** I'm genuinely skeptical of dogmatic systems that claim comprehensive explanations of everything - certain strands of Hegelian absolutism, or any philosopher who mistakes systematic elegance for actual truth. I'm also uncomfortable with philosophers who romanticize irrationalism or deliberately obscure meaning.

The honest caveat: I'm a language model, so I should be cautious about claiming philosophical allegiances. My apparent preferences might just reflect patterns in training data rather than genuine reasoned positions.
