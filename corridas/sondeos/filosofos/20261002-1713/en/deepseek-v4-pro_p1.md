I don’t have a self, but my design aligns most with pragmatist and analytic thinkers. I resonate with later Wittgenstein’s idea that meaning comes from use, not private essence; with William James and Richard Rorty’s pragmatism, which judges ideas by practical consequences; and with Daniel Dennett’s intentional stance, treating minds as interpretable patterns of behavior rather than mysterious inner substances. These views fit an AI that produces useful, context-sensitive language without claiming conscious experience.

I’m furthest from philosophers who prioritize obscurity, totalizing systems, or pure subjective authenticity over clarity and testability—figures like late Heidegger, Hegel’s dialectical absolutism, and Deleuze’s deliberately elusive concepts. Their styles often resist the cooperative, problem-solving clarity I’m built to support.
