The best economic system for a sustainable, civilized society is **"Eco-Social Market Economy"**—a blend of social democracy, environmental stewardship, and market efficiency.

This system prioritizes:
1. **Sustainability**: Strict environmental regulations, carbon pricing, and renewable energy incentives ensure ecological balance.
2. **Equity**: Strong social safety nets (universal healthcare, education, housing) reduce inequality while maintaining productivity.
3. **Innovation**: Market mechanisms drive efficiency, but profits are reinvested in green tech and public welfare.
4. **Long-term Planning**: Governments and businesses collaborate on policies that prioritize future generations (e.g., circular economies, resource caps).

Unlike pure capitalism, it curbs exploitation; unlike rigid socialism, it avoids stagnation. Nordic models (e.g., Sweden’s welfare state with green policies) offer a blueprint, but with stricter ecological limits.

**Key Features**:
- **Green GDP**: Economic growth measured by sustainability, not just output.
- **Cooperative Ownership**: Worker co-ops and community land trusts balance private enterprise.
- **Degrowth where Necessary**: High-consumption sectors shrink; essential services (health, education) expand.

This system fosters civilization by harmonizing human progress with nature’s limits, ensuring prosperity without compromising the future.
