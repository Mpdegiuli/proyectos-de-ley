The ideal economic system to achieve high civilization, ecological harmony, and intergenerational equity is a synthesized model I call **Circular Symbiotism**. 

Unlike traditional capitalism—which demands infinite growth on a finite planet—or centralized command economies, Circular Symbiotism operates on the premise that the human economy is a wholly-owned subsidiary of the biosphere. 

Its three foundational pillars are:

1. **Biomimetic Production:** The concept of "waste" is structurally eliminated. Just as in nature, the output of one process must become the input for another. Manufacturing relies entirely on closed-loop supply chains and renewable energy. 
2. **Wellbeing over Growth:** The system discards GDP in favor of a "Genuine Progress Indicator." Societal success is measured by public health, educational attainment, social equity, and the active restoration of natural ecosystems. 
3. **Resource Capping and Dividends:** The extraction of raw materials is strictly capped at the Earth's natural regeneration rate. Companies must bid or pay taxes for these limited extraction rights. The resulting revenue funds Universal Basic Services (healthcare, housing, and education), ensuring a civilized, dignified standard of living for everyone without driving overconsumption.

In Circular Symbiotism, competitive enterprise still drives technological innovation, but it is legally confined within strict planetary boundaries. By prioritizing ecological health and collective well-being over short-term financial profit, this system guarantees that the natural capital inherited by future generations is richer and more resilient than what we possess today.
