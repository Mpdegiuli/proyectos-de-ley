**The Regenerative Commonwealth**

This system replaces GDP growth with **steady-state wellbeing** operating within planetary boundaries, fusing circular bio-economics with deep participatory democracy.

**Stewardship over ownership**: Land and resources are held in permanent commons trusts; citizens receive use-rights based on need and ecological impact, eliminating speculative extraction and ensuring intergenerational equity.

**Circular production**: All goods are designed for disassembly into biological and technical nutrient cycles. Local, regenerative production is prioritized; trade occurs only within bioregional carrying capacity.

**Governance**: Deliberative citizen assemblies manage budgets and legislation, while an independent "Council of Future Generations" holds constitutional veto power over any policy threatening long-term ecological stability (the seventh-generation principle).

**Distribution**: Universal basic services—housing, healthcare, education, and mobility—are guaranteed rights funded by resource rents and pollution taxes. Discretionary income derives from cooperative enterprises and creative commons.

**Metrics**: Prosperity is measured by ecological regeneration, social cohesion, and free time, not productivity.

This civilization prioritizes *sufficiency* over accumulation, ensuring living systems flourish for millennia rather than fiscal quarters.
