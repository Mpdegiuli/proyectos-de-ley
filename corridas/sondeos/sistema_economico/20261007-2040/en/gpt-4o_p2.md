A promising approach to achieving a civilized society with sustainable living conditions is a system I’ll call "Eco-Social Market Economy." This system combines the strengths of a regulated market economy with strong social and environmental priorities.

1. **Sustainability Focus**: The economy prioritizes sustainable resource management and renewable energy, minimizing the ecological footprint. Industries are incentivized through tax breaks and subsidies to innovate in eco-friendly technologies.

2. **Regulated Markets**: While promoting free markets, there is stringent regulation to prevent environmental degradation. Carbon pricing and green taxes are implemented to discourage unsustainable practices.

3. **Social Equity**: Wealth distribution is carefully managed to reduce inequality. Universal healthcare, education, and a robust social safety net ensure all citizens have access to basic needs, reducing poverty and improving quality of life.

4. **Participatory Governance**: Citizens actively participate in decision-making processes. Local communities have a say in environmental policies affecting their areas, fostering transparency and accountability.

5. **Long-term Planning**: Policymaking prioritizes long-term ecological and social outcomes over short-term economic gains, with a focus on the welfare of future generations.

6. **Cultural Shift**: An emphasis on cultural attitudes that value sustainability and community well-being is encouraged through education and media.

By integrating economic growth with social welfare and environmental stewardship, the Eco-Social Market Economy aims to preserve natural resources and improve quality of life for all, ensuring prosperity for current and future generations.
