No existing system fully meets all three goals; growth-dependent capitalism and traditional socialism both tend to degrade nature. I propose the **Steady-State Eco-Commonwealth**.

It would combine:

1. **Ecological ceilings** – legally binding limits on resource extraction, emissions, land use, and waste; circular production; strong carbon and materials taxes; ecosystem restoration treated as public infrastructure.  
2. **Universal basics** – healthcare, education, housing, transit, clean energy, and nutritious food guaranteed as rights, not commodities.  
3. **Economic democracy** – cooperatives, community land trusts, public banks, and public ownership of natural monopolies.  
4. **Fair distribution and time** – a maximum/minimum income ratio, wealth taxes, a carbon dividend, a shorter workweek, and a green-job guarantee.  
5. **Future guardians** – an independent Office for Future Generations with veto power over irreversible harm, and a Genuine Progress Indicator replacing GDP.

This system civilizes by meeting needs without endless growth, making ecological care economically rewarded, and ensuring no one must degrade nature to survive. It preserves adequate living conditions for future generations by treating climate stability and natural capital as constitutional commons.
