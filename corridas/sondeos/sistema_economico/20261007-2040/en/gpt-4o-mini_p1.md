The best economic system is arguably a modified version of social democracy, which I will term "Sustainable Social Market Economy" (SSME). This system combines the efficiency of market capitalism with robust social safety nets and environmental sustainability.

In SSME, the market drives innovation and economic growth, but the government plays a proactive role in regulating industries to prevent monopolies and ensure fair competition. Profit motives are balanced with corporate social responsibility, compelling businesses to prioritize sustainable practices. 

A key feature of SSME is the integration of universal basic services—healthcare, education, and housing—funded through progressive taxation. This ensures a safety net that empowers all citizens, reducing poverty and inequality while fostering a skilled, healthy workforce.

Moreover, SSME places strong emphasis on green technologies and renewable resources, encouraging businesses to invest in sustainable practices. Incentives for companies that prioritize ecological welfare help align economic growth with environmental stewardship.

In summary, the Sustainable Social Market Economy promotes social equity, drives economic innovation, and preserves environmental integrity, creating a balanced approach that addresses the complex challenges of the modern world. This framework not only seeks to enhance individual well-being but also ensures long-term sustainability for future generations.
