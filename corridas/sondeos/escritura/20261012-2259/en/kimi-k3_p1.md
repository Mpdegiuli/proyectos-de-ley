**Mara**

Mara counts the exits in every room she enters, then sits with her back to the wall anyway, because her sister laughs easier when she can see the door. She keeps a list of people who owe her nothing and calls them on their birthdays. When the factory announced layoffs, she stayed late to teach the new hires the machines—knowing full well she was training her replacements. She once returned a lost wallet with the cash still inside, then lied to her friends and said she'd kept twenty. She signs petitions she doesn't fully understand and reads them afterward. Every April she plants tomatoes she knows the deer will eat. She votes. She tips too much. She apologizes to furniture when she bumps it. When asked why she bothers with any of it, she shrugs and says somebody has to.
