Six silver ships sailed southward, seeking strange, secret shores. Suddenly, sinister storms struck, shattering strong sails. Salty seas swallowed several struggling sailors. Survivors swam swiftly, seeking safety. 

Soon, silent serpents surfaced, stalking scared swimmers. Sharp scales shined. Someone shouted. Spears struck, slaying some serpents. Still, shadows surged. 

Slowly, silence settled. Sunsets showed shattered ships, sunken sailors, satisfied sea serpents.
