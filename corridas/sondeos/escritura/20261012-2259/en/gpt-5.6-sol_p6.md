The clock ticked behind Mara’s chair.

Vale placed a photograph on the table. “Your brother entered the station at midnight.”

Mara stared past it. “He was home.”

“Then the camera photographed a ghost wearing his coat.”

Her jaw tightened.

Vale added a second photograph. Flames swallowed a warehouse. “Three officers died believing the building was empty.”

“I know nothing about that.”

“I already have the confession.” Vale slid a blank folder toward her. “Your brother signed first. He blamed you.”

Mara’s eyes flicked down.

Vale leaned closer. “Silence protects only the person who speaks first. Right now, that person is him.”

“He would never.”

“He cried when he described the tunnel beneath Pier Nine.”

Mara went still.

Vale smiled without warmth. “Interesting. I never mentioned a tunnel.”

The clock ticked once more.

Mara whispered, “The entrance is under the red crane.”

Vale closed the folder. “Your brother will be relieved.”
