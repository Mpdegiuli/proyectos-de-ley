Mara set the recorder between them and pressed play.

Leo's voice filled the interrogation room. His own voice, laughing about the warehouse.

"You were never there," she said.

He stared at the table. "I wasn't."

"Your brother said the same thing this morning. Word for word. Even the pause."

Sweat darkened his collar.

"He cried when we showed him the second recording," she continued. "The one from inside the van. He begged us to protect you."

Leo's hands shook. "He wouldn't talk."

"He already did. He just left out the address to keep you useful."

A breath broke in his chest. "Riverside. Unit nine. Please, keep him out of it."
