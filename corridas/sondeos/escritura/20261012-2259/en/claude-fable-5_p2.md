**Excavation: Kitchen Drawer, Third from the Top**

Behold the teaspoon, dulled by decades of stirring —
its bowl worn thin as a Roman coin,
its handle bent by some ancestral thumb.

Note the patina: tea-stains layered like sediment,
each ring a dynasty of ordinary mornings.
Here, archaeologists of the household may trace

the daily rites of sugar and of grief,
the midnight soups, the medicine for fevers.
Handle with reverence. This artifact has touched

more lips than any chalice under glass,
has witnessed more confessions than the stones
of great cathedrals. Catalogue it: *beloved*.
