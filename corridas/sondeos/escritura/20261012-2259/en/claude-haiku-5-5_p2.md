**Rubber Band, Found in the Third Drawer**

Unearthed from the third drawer, stratum of takeout menus,
this rubber band lies where it last fell,
its latex faded to the color of weak tea,
a ring cracked at the curve like salvaged pottery.
No inscription survives. We can only guess
what it once bound: letters, perhaps,
or asparagus, or coins for some forgotten errand.
We lift it with cotton gloves and photograph its ruin,
store it in a box of acid-free tissue,
and marvel at the hands that stretched it wide,
then trusted it to hold, and let it go.
