The lamp hummed. Detective Mara set the photograph face-up between them.

"I know you were at the dock, Elias. Your boots left mud in my hallway—the same gray clay they dredge from Pier 9."

"I'm not saying anything."

"You don't have to. Your left eye twitches when you lie. It twitched twice already."

She leaned back, letting the silence do the work.

"Here's what happens now. You tell me where the girl is, and I walk into that courtroom and use the word 'cooperative.' Or you sit there twitching, and I use the word 'monster.' The jury loves a monster, Elias. It lets them feel righteous."

"She's alive. That's all I—"

"Alive is good. Alive is a start." Mara slid a pen across the table. "Write the address. Make your one good choice."
