The bulb hummed above the table. Mara laid a red mitten between them.

“We found it in your stove.”

Elias stared at the blackened cuff. “It isn’t hers.”

“I never said it was.”

His hands vanished beneath the table.

“The river team starts at dawn. They’ll search downstream first.”

“Then they’ll waste their time.”

Mara folded the mitten, tucking its thumb inside. “Her mother will be there. I’ll tell her you watched us search the wrong water.”

Elias’s chair creaked. “She fell. I tried to pull her out.”

Mara waited.

“The old ferry chain,” he said. “The ice broke there.”

Mara pocketed the mitten. Elias reached for it too late.
