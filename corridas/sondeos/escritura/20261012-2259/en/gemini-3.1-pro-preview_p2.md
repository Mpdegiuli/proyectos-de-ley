Behold this labyrinth of wire,
Forged in some long-forgotten fire,
A seamless coil of silver light
Unearthed from strata of the night.
This was the keeper of the scroll
That made the scattered fragments whole,
A monument of gleaming steel
Whose silent, nested loops conceal
The ancient magic of its bend—
Where does it start? Where does it end?
O sacred relic, unconsumed,
Within this wooden vault entombed.
