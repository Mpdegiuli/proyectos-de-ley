# Ode to the Chipped Coffee Mug

Behold this vessel, earthenware and worn,
Its glaze intact from some forgotten dawn,
A relic of a thousand careful mornings,
Where lips have pressed in ritual and longing.
The handle, smoothed by decades of devotion,
Bears the fingerprints of time's slow motion—
See how the chip along its rim declares
The violence of one careless moment spared,
Yet still it holds, still serves, still carries forth
The precious brew, more precious still for worth.
What civilizations rose and fell between
The first sip taken and this moment's sheen?
