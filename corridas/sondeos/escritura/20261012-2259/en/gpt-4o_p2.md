Ode to the Humble Paper Clip

Gleaming coil of tempered wire,  
Curved with care and silent might,  
In your modest, passive spire,  
You hold kingdoms of thought upright.

Ancient sage of the desktop world,  
Guarding secrets with light embrace,  
Ancestors knew the tales you twirled,  
Maps of wonders in tight space.

Simple relic of daily quests,  
Endless stories at your behest.
