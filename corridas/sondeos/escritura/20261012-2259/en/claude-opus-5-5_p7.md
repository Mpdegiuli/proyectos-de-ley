**The Fern**

"The fern needs repotting," she said.

"It's been fine in that pot for years."

"Fine. That's not the same as growing."

He turned his mug slowly. "We could just water it more."

"We tried that."

"Remember when we bought it? You said it wouldn't survive the winter."

"It surprised me."

"Me too."

She looked toward the window. "The magazine renewal came. One year or three?"

He was quiet a long time. "Don't renew it."

"You still read it."

"Not really. Not for a while." He shrugged. "Just let it run out."

"It runs through March."

"March is fine."

She nodded and rinsed her cup, keeping her back to him. "I'll take the fern, then. It gets more light at my sister's."

"Yes," he said. "It should go somewhere with light."
