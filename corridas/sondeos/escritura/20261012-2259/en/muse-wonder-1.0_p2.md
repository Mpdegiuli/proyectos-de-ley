From the dust of the junk drawer, we raised you,
bent paperclip, relic of a vanished age,
your single wire still looped in its perfect prayer.
Let future scholars marvel at this colossus,
no larger than a thumb, yet built to bind worlds—
contracts, love letters, homework, unpaid bills.
Rust crowns you now like laurel on an emperor.
O monument to almosts, to all held briefly together!
We lay you behind glass, upon dark velvet,
so generations may whisper: here, someone tried
to keep the scattered pages from flying apart.
