# The Ballpark Mustard Packet

Here it lies — a yellow relic,
foil-sealed, the size of a thumbnail,
stamped with the year's small numerals
like coins pressed in the reign of August.

Archaeologists might brush it clean
with the finest horsehair tools,
document its sealed interior,
catalogue its weight in grams.

What civilization bent above this?
What hands, distracted, laughing,
tore the corner carefully, anointed
a cheap hot dog like a king?
