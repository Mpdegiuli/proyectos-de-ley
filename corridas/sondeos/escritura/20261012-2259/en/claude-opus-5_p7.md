**Kitchen, Thursday**

"They had the big bag of coffee on offer. The kilo one."

"Did you get it?"

"No. It goes stale before you finish it."

"We always finish it."

"You always finish it. I only have one in the morning now."

A pause. The fridge hums.

"The Hendersons asked about August. The cottage."

"August is a long way off."

"They need to know by Friday."

"Tell them we're not sure yet."

"That's what I said last year."

"And we went."

"We did." She rinses a cup that's already clean. "There's a crack in that blue mug, by the way. The handle."

"I can glue it."

"You could. It'd hold, probably."

"Probably."

"Or we could just get new ones. They're hardly expensive."

He looks at the mug for a while.

"Right," he says. "New ones, then."

"I'll put it in the recycling."

"Yes. Easier."
