**Ode to the Paperclip**

Unearthed from the layers of forgotten days,  
This humble loop of steel, a time-worn artifact,  
Bends in the light of ancient office desks,  
A clasp of memories, binding thoughts intact.  

Once, it held the dreams of letters yet to send,  
A silent sentinel in the chaos of the mundane,  
Each twist and turn a testament to intent,  
A relic of the hands that crafted and contained.  

In a world of fleeting screens and fleeting glare,  
It whispers tales of what we dared to share,  
Oh, paperclip, modest in your shining grace,  
A monument to order in a cluttered space.
