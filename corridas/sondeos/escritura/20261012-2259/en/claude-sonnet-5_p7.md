**"Do you still want the plant?"**

Sarah looked up from her coffee. "Which one?"

"The fern. By the window."

"I don't know. Does it need a lot of water?"

"Twice a week. Maybe less in winter."

She turned her cup slowly on the table. "I've never been good with keeping things alive."

"It's not that hard. You just have to remember."

"I forget things, Tom. You know that."

He nodded, looking at the fern instead of her. "I could take it, if you want. I have space now."

"Since when?"

"Since the new apartment."

"Right." She pressed her lips together. "You should take it, then. Better there than here, dying slowly because I forgot."

"Okay." He stood, lifted the pot carefully, like it might break. "I'll come back for the rest this weekend."

"Sure," she said. "Whenever."
