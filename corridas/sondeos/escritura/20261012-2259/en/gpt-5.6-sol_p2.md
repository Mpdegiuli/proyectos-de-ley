**Paper Clip**

Unearthed beside the keyboard’s ruined temple,  
a silver loop, intact after the age of bills.  
What careful hands once bent this slender gate  
to guard a sheaf of vanished names?  
I hold it up: a monument to order,  
a tiny arch no emperor could cross.  
Rust freckles its forgotten dynasty.  
Still, it remembers how to gather  
the loose white leaves of history  
and keep them from the wind.
