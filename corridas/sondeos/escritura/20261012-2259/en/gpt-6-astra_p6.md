Mara set a child’s red mitten on the table.

Vale stopped rubbing the handcuff against his wrist.

“The divers found this at the west pier,” she said. “They’re dragging the channel now.”

He looked at the mitten, then at the clock.

“I told them to stop searching the warehouses. There’s no point wasting the light.”

“You don’t know that.”

“The current takes everything west. That’s what you were counting on.”

Vale’s chain snapped taut. “She wasn’t near the water.”

Mara reached for the radio.

“Then the divers stay.”

“The old icehouse,” he said. “Room four. The latch opens from outside.”

Mara transmitted the address, keeping her voice level.

Vale stared at the mitten. “Where did you get that.”

“Lost property.”

He sagged against the chair.

Only then did she let herself look at the clock.
