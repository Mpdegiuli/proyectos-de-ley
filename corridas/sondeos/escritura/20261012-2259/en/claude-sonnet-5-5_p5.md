**Sally's Stars**

Sally sewed silver stars sequentially, softly singing sweet songs. Suddenly, seven silly squirrels sprinted south, snatching several shiny stars. Sally shrieked, "Stop! Selfish scamps!" Squirrels stopped, stared sheepishly, slowly surrendered sparkling spoils. Sally smiled, so she suggested sharing. Soon, squirrels sported stars, strutting superbly. Sally stitched several stars, satisfied. Starlight settled smoothly, sprinkling serenity somewhere special.
