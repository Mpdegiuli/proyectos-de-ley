Mara set the bloodied key on the table.

“You kept it,” she said.

Jon’s eyes stayed on the mirror. “I don’t know what it opens.”

“Then you won’t mind when I give it to your sister. She can try every door in the east wing.”

His jaw tightened.

Mara leaned closer. “She’s already there. Alone.”

“You said she was safe.”

“I said you could make her safe.”

The fluorescent light hummed. Jon stared at the key as if it might move.

“Third floor,” he whispered. “Room twelve. The ledger’s under the sink.”

Mara picked up the key. “And the guard.”

Jon closed his eyes.

“His name,” she said, softly. “Give me the name before she reaches the stairs.”

“Vale.”

Mara was already at the door.

Behind her, Jon began to plead.
