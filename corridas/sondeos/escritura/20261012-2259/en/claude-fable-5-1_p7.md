**The Fern**

"The fern's looking bad."

"It just needs water."

"I've been watering it."

"Then it needs less."

"I don't know what it needs anymore."

"Try the back window. Better light."

"That's your window."

"It's a window."

"Do you want it? The fern?"

"It'd do better with you. You're home more."

"I'm not sure I'm home enough."

"It's hardy. It'll be fine for a while."

"And after a while?"

"Then it's a plant, Mara. It's not—"

"I know what it's not."

"..."

"I'll take the ficus, then."

"Okay."

"I can come by Saturday. For the ficus."

"Saturday's fine."

"Okay."

"Okay."
