The tap dripped. Ines slid a photograph across the steel table and said nothing for a full minute.

"Your brother signed a statement an hour ago."

Daniel's jaw tightened. "Then you don't need me."

"I don't. That's the kindness of this moment." She straightened the photo. "He says you drove. He sounds relieved, Daniel. Relieved people have found someone to blame."

"He's lying."

"Maybe. Then record your version before his hardens into truth." She clicked her pen. "I know the warehouse. I know the blue van. I don't know where Marta is, and that's the only part still worth bargaining over."

His knee bounced.

"Judges are generous to men who lead them to a door. Not to men who make them kick it in."

He stared at the photograph. "Pier nine. Under the loading office."

Ines capped the pen. "Thank you."
