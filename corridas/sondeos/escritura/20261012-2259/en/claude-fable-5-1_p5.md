**Serpent Surprise**

Sarah silently sipped steaming soup. Suddenly, someone screamed. Sarah stood, startled. Shadows shifted. Something slithered softly—snake! Sarah shrieked, stumbled sideways, spilling soup. Sam, stalwart stranger, strode swiftly, scooped serpent, smiled. "Simply Sylvester, sister's slippery sidekick." Sarah sighed, speechless. Sam suggested supper somewhere safer. She smiled shyly. Sunset shimmered. Sweet serendipity.
