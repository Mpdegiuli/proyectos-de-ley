Stormy Saturday, shy sailor Silas spotted something shimmering seaward. Sharks? Sirens? Silas sailed straight, skimming squalls, swallowing spray. Suddenly, seven silver seals surfaced, surrounding someone’s shattered schooner. Someone sobbed. Silas scrambled, saved stranded siblings, secured sails. Sunrise spread saffron splendor. Safe, smiling strangers shared sandwiches, stories, songs; Silas silently thanked sea.
