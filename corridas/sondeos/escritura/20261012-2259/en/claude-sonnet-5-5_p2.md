**Ode to a Paperclip, Found Beneath the Couch**

Behold, unearthed from strata of lost dust,
a single curve of silver, twice-looped, wise.
No pharaoh's gold could match its quiet trust:
it held a thousand pages, and their sighs.

What hand once bent this wire into grace?
What tax return, what love note did it bind?
The scholars hush. They brush its humble face
and find the fingerprints of humankind.

Let glass be raised around its velvet bed;
let schoolchildren file past in reverent rows.
"Here," says the plaque, "a civilization said
*Hold fast*, and asked for nothing more than those."
