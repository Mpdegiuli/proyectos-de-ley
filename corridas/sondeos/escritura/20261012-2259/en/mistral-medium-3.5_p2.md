**Ode to a Rusty Key**

Oh, relic of forgotten doors,
your teeth worn by time’s slow gnaw,
what secrets lie behind the jaws
you once unsealed with whispered lore?

A warder of wood, a guard of grain,
you turned in locks now turned to dust.
Yet still you bear the touch of hands
that trusted you—now lost, now rust.

What vaults did you, in prime, command?
What treasures slept beneath your sway?
Now just a weight in palm or pocket,
a silent keep of yesterday.
