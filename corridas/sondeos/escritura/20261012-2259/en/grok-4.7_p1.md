They arrived early and left the door unlocked for anyone still looking for a place to sit. When the meeting ran long, they stayed after the others had gone, rewriting the notes so the quietest voice in the room would not vanish from the summary. They chose the longer way home because it passed the shelter where a name had been called twice without an answer, and they waited until someone answered. At the market they paid for the fruit a child had already bitten into, then walked away before thanks could become a scene. Offered a promotion that required someone else to be blamed, they declined and wrote the refusal so carefully that the person who would have been blamed never had to know. Before sleep they set an alarm they did not need, in case the call came again.
