**Paperclip**

From the silt of the junk drawer  
I lift a paperclip, rust at its bends.  
Here is a bridge no river remembers,  
a little iron arch against forgetting.

Once it held two letters face to face;  
their words are dust, their names unmoored.  
I set it upright in a square of light  
and watch its shadow cross the desk.

O small monument to staying near,  
how gently your ruined arms still close.
