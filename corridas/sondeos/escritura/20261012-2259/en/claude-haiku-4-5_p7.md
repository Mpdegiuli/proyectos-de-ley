# Coffee

"So I've been thinking about the apartment."

"Yeah?"

"I could take the east side. You'd have more room with your desk setup."

"That's... considerate."

"We should probably talk about the furniture. The couch was mine originally."

"I know."

"But if you want it—"

"No. No, you should have it."

A long pause. She stirs her coffee.

"I'll need to find a place by the end of the month."

"That soon?"

"I think soon is better. Don't you?"

He nods slowly, studying his hands.

"I'll help you move. We can make a schedule so we're not here at the same time too much."

"Okay."

Another silence. The coffee shop hums around them.

"Should we tell people separately, or...?"

"However you want."

She reaches across the table. He doesn't move his hand away, but he doesn't take hers either.

"Thank you," she whispers, "for trying."
