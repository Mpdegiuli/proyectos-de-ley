**"The Plant"**

"The fern's looking rough."

"I noticed. I've been watering it, though."

"Maybe too much. Or not enough. Hard to tell at this point."

"My mom always said ferns are forgiving. You can bring them back from almost anything."

"Almost."

"Yeah. Almost."

*(pause)*

"You could take it to your new place. Good light there, you said."

"It's always been more yours, really. You picked it out."

"We picked it out."

"Did we? I remember standing there while you decided."

*(pause)*

"I could split it. People do that — divide the roots. Both halves survive, sometimes."

"Sometimes they both die faster."

"Right."

"Right."

*(pause)*

"So just... leave it on the windowsill, then?"

"Yeah. Leave it where it is."

"Okay."

"It was a good fern, though. For a while."

"It was. It really was."
