Silent Selene stood shivering, staring seaward. Storms swallowed stars; ships sank somewhere. Suddenly, silver sparks surfaced, spinning slowly. Selene stepped shoreward, singing softly. Something small, soaked, scaled, smiled. “Save sailors,” serpent sighed. Selene seized shells, summoned spirits, split seas. Sunrise showed seven saved sailors sleeping safely, surrounding Selene’s strange, shimmering sea-dragon.
