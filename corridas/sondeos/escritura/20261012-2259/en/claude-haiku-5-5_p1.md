Every morning, Marta wakes before the bakery opens and walks the long way to work, past the shuttered textile mill, because she has decided someone should check on Teodor, the neighbor who never leaves his window. She knocks twice, waits, and leaves bread on his step even when he refuses to open the door. When the city council announces the mill's demolition, she does not shout at the officials. Instead, she spends three weekends collecting signatures, reading zoning laws until she understands them, and proposing a compromise: keep the bell tower and convert the rest into a community kitchen. She loses the first vote. She decides to ask again. Eventually, Teodor opens the door.
