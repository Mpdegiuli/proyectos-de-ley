The light hummed. Kane set the photograph face-up between them.

"You were on Mercer Street at eleven. The camera doesn't lie, and neither does the blood on your cuff."

Voss stared at the table. His knee bounced once, then stopped.

"She screamed your name before the line went dead. We have the recording. Your partner already told us where you hid the drive. He's walking out tonight."

Voss's mouth tightened.

"You stay here, and he spends the money. You talk, and maybe you see daylight before he does."

A long breath. Voss's fingers found the edge of the photo.

"Locker forty-two. South terminal. The code is her birthday."

Kane didn't smile. "There it is."
