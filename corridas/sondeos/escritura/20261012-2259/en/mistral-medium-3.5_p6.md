The dim light flickered as Daniel leaned in, his voice a low growl. "You know where the package is." His eyes locked onto hers, unblinking. "The boss won’t like it if you hold out on us."

Maria’s fingers tightened around her glass, knuckles white. "I don’t know what you’re talking about." Her voice wavered, just slightly.

Daniel smirked, dragging a chair across the floor with a screech. "Funny how you’re the only one who had access." He tapped his fingers on the table, slow, deliberate. "A person might think you’re hiding something."

Maria’s breath hitched. "I didn’t take anything."

"Then you won’t mind if we check your place." Daniel stood, looming over her. "Or maybe you’d rather tell me now and save everyone the trouble."

Her shoulders slumped, defeat creeping in. "Fine. It’s under the floorboards."
