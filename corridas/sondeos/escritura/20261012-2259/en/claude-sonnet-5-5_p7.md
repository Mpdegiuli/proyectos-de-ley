**The Fern**

"Do you want the fern?"

"Don't you?"

"It does better in your window. More light."

"It's been getting less light lately."

"Well. It's adaptable."

"You always said it was fragile."

"I said it was particular. There's a difference."

"Water it Sundays. Not too much."

"I know. I've watched you do it for four years."

"Then you'll be fine."

"The blue pot stays with it, though."

"Of course. It was always the fern's pot."

"Right."

"I'll take the box of cables."

"Take the good scissors too."

"I don't need the scissors."

"Take them. You'll want them. Things come in packages."

"...Okay."

"Is that the kettle?"

"Yes. Tea?"

"Better not. I should leave before it gets dark."

"It's only three."

"Still."
