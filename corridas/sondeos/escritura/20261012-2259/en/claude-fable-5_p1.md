She wakes before the alarm to answer emails no one expects her to read. When the company announced layoffs, she spent her lunch breaks quietly rewriting résumés for the colleagues being let go, never mentioning it afterward. She keeps a promise even when the person she made it to has forgotten it entirely. At her mother's funeral, she gave up her planned eulogy and instead read aloud the grocery lists her mother used to leave on the fridge, because that was the truer portrait. She refuses shortcuts that cost someone else something, even invisibly. When her brother stole from her, she reported nothing, but she also never lent him money again—forgiveness without foolishness. She learns one new skill every winter, abandons most of them by spring, and considers none of it wasted. She asks questions at parties and actually waits, fully, for the answers.
