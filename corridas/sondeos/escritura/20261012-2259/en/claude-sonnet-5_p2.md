**Ode to the Paperclip, Found in a Desk Drawer**

Behold this slender relic, bent by hands
long vanished from the ledgers it once bound—
a silver torque, unburied from the sands
of pencil shavings, dust, and paper's ground.

What empire rose and fell beneath its curve?
What contracts, love notes, overdue returns
it held together with such quiet nerve,
this humble hoop no monument discerns.

No plaque records its service, yet it gleams
still faintly archaeological, bright
as any ring unearthed from pharaohs' dreams—
a small, unbroken loop against the night.
