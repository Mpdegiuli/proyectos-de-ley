Mara unlocks the town archive every morning and records the names officials order her to erase. She hides copies inside outdated tax ledgers, then delivers groceries to the families who no longer qualify for rations.

When the river breaches its banks, she commandeers the mayor’s boat instead of waiting for permission. She makes three trips to the flooded district. On the fourth, she finds her brother burning the evacuation lists he sold to smugglers.

She pulls him aboard, but retrieves a half-burned ledger before leaving.

At the emergency shelter, Mara distributes blankets, counts the missing, and gives the ledger to the prosecutor. She refuses her brother’s request to call it an accident. Then she saves him a place in the meal queue and returns to the river.
