**Bread Clip, Plastic, Late Supermarket Period**

Behold: the bread clip, unearthed beneath the stove,
small square crown of the dynasty of Loaf,
its toothless jaw still shaped around the throat
of some vanished rye, some sliced and buttered age.
Note the stamped glyph, BEST BY 14 MAR,
a date its scribes believed would hold forever.
Here, in the notch, one crumb: the final relic
of a kingdom fed on mornings and on haste.
Let it rest under glass beside the mug-shard,
the coin of no value, the key to no known door,
and let the schoolchildren file past it, hushed,
for a people who sealed what they meant to return to.
