**The Paperclip**

Unearthed from the silt of a forgotten drawer,  
this slender relic gleams like a votive coil,  
once binding the edicts of ordinary hours—  
invoices, love-notes, the day’s small spoils.  

Its wire, a sacred loop of vanished haste,  
still holds the ghost of pages never saved.  
We lift it as a monument to waste,  
this tiny ruin no empire ever craved.  

Dust it; display it. Let the ages know  
how even the least of us could bind and let go.
