**Pencil Stub, Recovered from the Kitchen Drawer**

Behold the yellow column, ringed with teeth—
each crescent bite a glyph no scholar reads,
the sign of some anonymous mind beneath
the strain of long division, or of grief.
Its brass collar dulled. Its pink crown ground
to nothing by a thousand small corrections.
Set it in velvet. Rope it off. Let crowds
file past in silence, reading their reflections
in the glass. Inside, a vein of ore
runs dark, unspent, toward the sharpened end—
the sentence someone meant to write, and then
set down, mid-word, and never picked up again.
