# An Untitled Feeling

A room where light enters but refuses to settle, casting shadows that shift even when nothing moves. The walls are smooth marble that somehow feels gritty beneath your fingers. Colors bleed into one another like watercolors on wet paper—reds darkening to bruises, golds dissolving into ash.

There's a weight pressing from above, yet also a strange weightlessness, as if you're sinking and floating simultaneously. The air tastes of copper and tastes of nothing. Edges blur and sharpen unpredictably. Everything appears fractured, like looking through cracked glass, yet oddly glittering with an attractiveness you cannot explain.

A landscape perpetually caught between storms and sunshine.

[Melancholy]
