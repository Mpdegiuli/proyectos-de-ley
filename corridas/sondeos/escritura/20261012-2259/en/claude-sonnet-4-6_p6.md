# The Room

Marcus set the folder on the table between them.

"You were at the warehouse Tuesday night." He let the silence breathe. "We have a receipt from the gas station two blocks away. Your card. 11:47 PM."

Delia stared at the folder without touching it.

"The thing about loyal people," Marcus continued, straightening a pen he didn't need, "is they tell us *before* we find out ourselves. After, it stops being loyalty. It becomes something else entirely."

"I don't know what you want me to say."

"You already know what I want." He stood, buttoned his jacket. "I'm going to get coffee. I'll be gone maybe ten minutes. When I come back, someone will have decided something."

He walked to the door.

"The man who was with you," he said quietly, not turning around. "He already talked."

He left her alone with that.
