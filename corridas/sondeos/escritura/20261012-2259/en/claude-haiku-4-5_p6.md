# The Confession

Detective Morgan leaned against the cold metal table, studying the suspect across from it.

"Your fingerprints are on the murder weapon," Morgan stated flatly. "We found them this morning."

Cole's jaw tightened. His fingers trembled slightly.

"The victim's blood matches the samples from your jacket," Morgan continued, stepping closer. "Your neighbor saw someone matching your description leaving the scene at 11:47 PM."

"I didn't—"

"You were there." Morgan's voice was ice. "We both know it. The only question now is whether you went there intending to kill, or if it was self-defense."

Cole's breathing quickened.

"Everyone understands self-defense," Morgan whispered, crouching beside him. "People sympathize with that. But premeditation... that's different. That's what prosecutors love."

Sweat beaded on Cole's forehead. His resolve crumbled.

"It was self-defense," he whispered.

Morgan smiled coldly.
