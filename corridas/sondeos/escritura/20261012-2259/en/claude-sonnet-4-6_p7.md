# The Last Coffee

"Do you want sugar?"

"No. You know I stopped taking sugar."

"Right. I forgot."

"It's fine."

*silence*

"The plant by the window is dying."

"I noticed."

"I've been watering it."

"Maybe too much. Or not enough."

"Probably."

*cups touched the table*

"I should head out before traffic gets bad."

"It's only two o'clock."

"Still."

"Your jacket is on the chair."

"I see it."

"Okay."

"The coffee was good."

"It was the same as always."

"I know."

*chair scraping floor*

"Should I leave the key on the counter?"

"Wherever is easier for you."

"The counter, then."

"Fine."

"Take care of the plant."

"I'll try."

"Goodbye, Marie."

"Goodbye."

*door closing slowly*

*one cup left on the table, still full*
