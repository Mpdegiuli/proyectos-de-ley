**"Seaside Success"**

Sofia sold seashells seaside. Sailors seldom stopped; Sofia starved, sighing sadly. Someday, she supposed, success surely should surface.

Saturday, some stranger strolled shoreward, seeking special souvenirs. "Splendid shells!" Sofia sang, showing seven shimmering specimens. Stranger smiled, spending substantial silver.

Soon sailors swarmed Sofia's stall, snatching souvenirs. Sofia's sadness subsided; she savored sweet success, sipping sarsaparilla, smiling serenely seaward.
