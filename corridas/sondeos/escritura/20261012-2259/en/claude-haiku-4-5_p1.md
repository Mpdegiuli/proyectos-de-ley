# Maya

Maya wakes before dawn to volunteer at the community kitchen, arriving before anyone else. She listens more than she speaks, remembering names and favorite meals from months prior. When a regular customer stops coming, she tracks them down and delivers soup to their apartment.

At her day job, Maya advocates loudly for fair wages her coworkers deserve, even when it complicates her standing with management. She donates half her raises anonymously to bail funds and education programs.

She makes mistakes—sometimes her empathy exhausts her, and she withdraws. She's been known to hold grudges. But she always apologizes, learns, and tries again.

Maya chooses the harder path consistently: confronting injustice, showing up for strangers, admitting when she's wrong. These choices have shaped her into someone her community trusts completely.
