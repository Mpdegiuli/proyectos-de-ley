**Votive Offering, Excavated from Beneath the Refrigerator**

Bronze medallion, crimped along the rim
into twenty-one small towers — a city wall
raised to defend a sweetness already gone.
Its scarlet creed has weathered to a rumor.
Underneath, the gasket, pale as a lip,
still keeps the shape of the mouth it guarded.
Someone pried this loose on an August evening:
the hiss, the sigh, a brief industrial psalm —
then let it drop, and the dark took it in,
filed it in dust among the lost dynasties.
I hold it to the window. It gives back nothing
but the small cold weight of having been opened.
