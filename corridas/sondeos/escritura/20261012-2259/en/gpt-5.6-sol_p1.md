At dawn, Mara unlocks the village clinic before the generators start. She sorts the remaining antibiotics, gives the last full course to a feverish child, and records the shortage instead of hiding it. When soldiers arrive demanding supplies, she hands them expired bandages and keeps the medicine cabinet locked. She then sends her assistant through the orchard with a list of patients who cannot travel.

By noon, an evacuation truck offers Mara one seat. She gives it to the assistant, along with the clinic ledger and instructions to expose the commander’s theft. As shelling nears, Mara moves the patients into the cellar, returns upstairs for the radio, and broadcasts their location on every frequency. When a soldier orders her to stop, she keeps transmitting until an answering voice promises rescue.
