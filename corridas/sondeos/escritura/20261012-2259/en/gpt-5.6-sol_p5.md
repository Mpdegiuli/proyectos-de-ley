Silent sailors sighted shimmering shores. Storms surged; sails snapped. Stranded, survivors sought shelter, stumbling southward. Suddenly, silver songbirds swirled skyward, signaling safety. Secluded settlers shared stew, stories, smiles. Sunrise softened sorrow. Soon, stout ships sailed seaward, sporting scarlet streamers. Still, sailor Selene stayed, smitten. She sensed sanctuary.
