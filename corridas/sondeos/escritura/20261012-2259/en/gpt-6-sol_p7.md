“Are we out of milk?” Mara asked.

“Almost.”

“I’m going to the shop. The big carton?”

Eli looked at the cup in his hands. “The small one.”

“Right.” She added it to the list. “And bin bags?”

“Under the sink. You can take half.”

“I meant for here.”

“Oh.” He set down the cup. “There’s enough for a while.”

Mara crossed bin bags off the list. “Your mother gave us that cup.”

“She gave it to me.”

“I know.”

After a moment, Eli said, “You can keep it.”

“No,” she said. “I’ll get you some boxes while I’m out.”
