**The Paperclip**

O twisted silver, coil of patient art,
unearthed from desk-dust, ancient as the morning—
what vanished hands once bent your shining heart,
what memos, gone to nothing, did you hold in keeping?

Scholars will debate your double loop,
your doctrine of soft custody, the way
you gripped without a wound, held fast, let go—
a civilization's genius: small, precise, and kind.

Museum glass will keep you now, coiled god
of order, relic of the paper age,
when words had weight, and needed, even then,
a humble spine of steel to hold them together.
