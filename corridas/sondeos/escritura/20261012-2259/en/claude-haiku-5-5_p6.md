The bare bulb hummed above the metal table. Inspector Hale set a folder down and did not open it.

"Your brother signed the transfer at 4:12 a.m. You were the only other person with his key."

Mara said nothing. Her fingers found the table's edge and held on.

"I'm not angry. I'm tired, and tired men miss things." He tapped the folder. "Someone will take the fall for the missing ledger. The court prefers a name it can say quickly."

"I didn't touch it."

"I believe you. That's the problem." He leaned back. "Your brother doesn't have your restraint. He's across the hall, telling them everything except what matters. He's already decided you saw the box."

Her breath caught.

"Nobody expects you to say where it is. Just confirm the drawer was empty when you got there."

Silence stretched. Her jaw tightened.

"It wasn't empty," she whispered. "He moved it. Behind the radiator on the east wall."

Hale gathered the folder and stood. He said nothing more.
