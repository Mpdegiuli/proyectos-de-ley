The lamp hummed. Dvorak set a photograph on the table, face down.

"You were at the warehouse on Thursday."

Reyes said nothing.

"Your wife thinks you were at your brother's. Your brother thinks you were at work. I've spoken to both. They're very fond of you. It would be a shame to put them in the same room."

Reyes shifted. The chair creaked.

"The money is already found. The man who drove the van has a name, and he's using yours." Dvorak slid the photograph closer, still face down. "He says you planned it. I'm inclined to believe him. He was very calm."

A long silence. Dvorak checked his watch.

"I have forty minutes before the paperwork closes. After that, the version I have is the version that stays."

Reyes exhaled.

"Marcus," he said. "The driver's name is Marcus. I only opened the gate."

Dvorak turned the photograph over. It was blank.
