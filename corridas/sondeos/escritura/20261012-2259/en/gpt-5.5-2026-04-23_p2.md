**Staple**

Small silver arch, unearthed from the desk-dust,  
you hold two pages like a treaty still warm.  
No king declared you, no chisel named you,  
yet here you stand: a bent gate in paper stone.  

Empires of ink have leaned upon your back,  
receipts, apologies, minutes of meetings—  
all fastened briefly against oblivion.  

O quiet relic, rusting at the corner,  
your tiny teeth remember what we meant.
