# Moving the Sofa

"The sofa could go against the far wall," Sarah said.

"Would that even fit? It's always been fine here."

"That was before. Things change. I measured — there's plenty of room."

James leaned against the doorframe. "And the bookshelf? Where would that go?"

"To the spare room. It's never really belonged here anyway."

"You could just leave things where they are. Less disruption."

"But this way, the living room feels bigger. Airier. Like there's space for something new."

He nodded slowly. "I never noticed how narrow this hallway was."

"It only feels that way when two people are in it."

Sarah folded a cushion neatly and placed it in a cardboard box.

"I found your coffee mugs, by the way. All four."

"Keep them. I've got enough for one."
