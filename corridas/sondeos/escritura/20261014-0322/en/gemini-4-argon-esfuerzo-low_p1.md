Elena always selects the corner booth at the diner, positioning herself to monitor both exits simultaneously. Before touching her coffee, she checks the salt shaker; if it isn’t completely full, she leaves without paying. Every Tuesday, she transfers half her savings to an anonymous orphanage, then spends the afternoon forging birth certificates for people fleeing violent pasts. When a stranger drops a wallet on the subway, she kicks it under the seat rather than returning it, yet she will miss her own train to help an elderly commuter carry groceries up the stairs. Offered a lucrative promotion that requires relocating, she burns the contract in her kitchen sink. Instead, she adopts a stray dog that bit her the day before. At night, she sleeps with a loaded revolver locked in a safe, hiding the key at the bottom of a fishbowl so she must hesitate before using it.
