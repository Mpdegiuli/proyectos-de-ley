**Relic of the Forgotten Coast**

Unearthed from beneath the glacial fridge’s shade,
This square of sacred polymer sees the light.
What ancient bakers forged its jagged blade?
What perished fingers snapped its plastic might?
Behold the glyphs of azure, clear and cold:
*Best By the sixth of May*, a vanished year—
A cryptographic prophecy foretold
To guard the golden loaves once gathered here.
Oh, Twin-Toothed Sentinel of daily bread,
You held the twisted throat of life at bay!
Though empires of the pantry long have fled,
Your stoic jaws still bite the air today.
