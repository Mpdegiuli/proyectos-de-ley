Marcus set the heavy pliers on the table. Sweat beaded on Leo’s forehead.

"The syndicate thinks I took the ledger, Leo. They are five minutes away. When they arrive, I will hand them a thief."

Leo strained against the zip-ties. "I didn't touch it."

"Your fingernails are clean," Marcus noted softly, pacing the cramped room. "No mud from the yard. That means you never left this apartment. Yet your knuckles are scraped. Drywall dust clings to your cuffs."

Leo’s eyes darted involuntarily toward the hallway.

"A man only hides something in the walls if he plans to come back for it," Marcus whispered, picking up the pliers. "The syndicate won't search the drywall. They will just peel the answer off your fingers."

Leo broke, trembling violently. "Behind the fuse box. Wrapped in plastic. Just untie me."
