**The Pull-Tab**

Unearthed from dusty strata by the broom,
This twin-eyed amulet of silver gleams;
No marble vault or gilded pharaoh’s tomb
Could hoard a purer relic of our dreams.

Behold the sacred loop, the tiny pry
That once unsealed an effervescent sea!
What thirsty giant bent it with a sigh
To loose the hissed libation’s mystery?

Now severed from its vessel’s frigid crest,
This monument of alloy still remains:
An artifact of some forgotten quest,
The crown of carbonated, lost domains.
