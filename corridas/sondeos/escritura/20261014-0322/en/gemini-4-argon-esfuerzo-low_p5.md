Seven silent shadows slipped southward. Samuel’s ship sailed swiftly, seeking safe sanctuary. Suddenly, severe storms struck. Savage swells smashed Samuel’s sturdy schooner. Scared sailors shouted, scrambling sideways. Samuel steered steadily, surviving several scary seconds. Soon, sunlight shone softly. Surprised survivors sighted sandy shores. Samuel smiled, savoring sweet safety. Stepping seaward, seven salty sailors sought shelter, sharing stories, singing simple songs, sleeping soundly.
