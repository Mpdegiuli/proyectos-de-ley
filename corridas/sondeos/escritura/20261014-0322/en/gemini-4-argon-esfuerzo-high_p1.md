Every morning, Julian arrives at the archives thirty minutes before the shift begins, choosing to spend the quiet hour memorizing the files slated for incineration. When a superior offers a lucrative promotion in exchange for overlooking a discrepancy in the city’s water ration logs, Julian declines without hesitation and secretly copies the fraudulent data onto an encrypted drive. At noon, rather than dining in the warm commissary, Julian walks three blocks to feed a stray dog waiting by the perimeter fence, dividing a meager lunch in half. Later, when trapped in the records room during a midnight security sweep, Julian decides against forcing the emergency exit. Instead, Julian remotely triggers a fire alarm on the opposite floor to draw the guards away, waiting patiently in the dark before delivering the stolen truth to the underground press.
