**Artifact Found Beneath the Sofa: The Paperclip**

Unearthed from the dark strata of the floor,
This silver serpent of a bygone age,
A twisted relic that the ancients bore
To bind the sacred parchment of the page.

Behold its twin ellipses, warped by time,
A monument of galvanized steel wire;
What empire forged this symmetry sublime?
What scribe’s lost panic did its clasp inspire?

Now brushed of dust, it claims the velvet tray,
A miniature colossus, cold and grand,
That held the crumbling centuries at bay
Within the hollow of a mortal hand.
