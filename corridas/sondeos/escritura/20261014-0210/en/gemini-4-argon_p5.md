Sarah sailed south, seeking sunlit shores. Suddenly, severe storms struck. Swirling seas smashed Sarah’s sturdy sloop. She struggled, shivering, soaked, scared. Shadows surrounded Sarah. Still, Sarah stayed strong, steering skillfully. Soon, silver stars shone softly. Storms subsided. Sunrise showed serene, sandy sanctuaries. Stepping shoreward, Sarah smiled, safe, surviving sea's savage spite.
