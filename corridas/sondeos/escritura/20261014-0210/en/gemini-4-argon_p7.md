"The ficus is dying," Sarah said, touching a brittle, yellow leaf. "We’ve tried moving it to every window in the apartment. There just isn't enough light here."

Mark didn’t look up from his coffee. "We watered it. Bought that expensive plant food. Sometimes things just don’t take root, no matter what you do."

"I thought about pruning the dead branches," she murmured. "Starting fresh."

"You tried that last winter," Mark replied softly, setting his mug down. "It only shocked the system. Honestly, Sarah, keeping it in this pot is just prolonging the inevitable. The roots are completely suffocating."

Silence stretched between them, heavy and final. 

"So, we stop trying to save it?" Her voice cracked slightly. 

Mark finally met her eyes, his expression tired and sad. "It’s exhausting watching it wither. You can take it with you, if you want. Or we just leave it by the curb."
