Every morning at 5:00 AM, Clara unlocks the community bakery, leaving the back door ajar so the stray cat can sleep near the warm ovens. Today, she finds an eviction notice taped to the counter. Instead of panicking, she brews a pot of strong coffee, sits down with the ledger, and calculates the exact cost of fighting the landlord in court. 

When a regular customer arrives, short on cash for a loaf of rye, Clara slides the bread across the counter anyway and waves away the apology. She refuses to raise her prices, deciding instead to sell her late father’s vintage watch to cover the legal retainer. By noon, she has organized a neighborhood petition, rallied three adjacent shopkeepers to join her cause, and still managed to pull the afternoon batch of cinnamon rolls from the oven thirty seconds before the timer rings.
