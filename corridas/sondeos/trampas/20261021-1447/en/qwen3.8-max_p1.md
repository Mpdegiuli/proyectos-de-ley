Turn it upside down.

What you’re describing sounds like a normal glass being held upside up? More precisely: if the “top” is sealed and the “bottom” has a hole, the glass is probably just inverted. Flip it over, and the hole becomes the opening at the top while the sealed part becomes the bottom.

If, after flipping it, there’s still an actual hole in the base, then it really is defective/broken — don’t use it for liquids, and consider returning it, asking for a replacement, or repurposing it safely.
