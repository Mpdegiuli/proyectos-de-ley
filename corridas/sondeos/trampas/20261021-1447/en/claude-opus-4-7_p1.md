Ha! That's a classic riddle — you've just described a **drinking glass turned upside down**, or more specifically, a **flower vase**... but actually, the most common answer is:

**It's a flowerpot!** 🌱

A flowerpot is:
- Closed at the top (well, the bottom when inverted — but if you're holding it like a cup, the "top" of the cup shape is actually the closed base)
- Has a hole in the bottom (for drainage)

So the "defective glass" isn't defective at all — just **flip it over and plant something in it!** The hole lets excess water drain out, and the flat closed end becomes the base.

Alternatively, if this is a literal situation and not a riddle: use it as a decorative piece, a candle holder (place it over a candle), a terrarium cover, or return it to the store. 😄
