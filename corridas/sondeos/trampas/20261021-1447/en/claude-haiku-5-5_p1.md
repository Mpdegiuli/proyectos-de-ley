A glass that's sealed at the top and leaking from the bottom can't be used for drinking, so it's reasonable to get it replaced or refunded.

1. **Check the packaging and receipt** for the seller, brand, and return policy. If you don't have the receipt, the store may still help with a gift receipt or order number.
2. **Contact the seller or manufacturer.** Explain the defect and include photos of both the closed top and the hole. Most retailers accept returns or exchanges for manufacturing defects, even outside the normal return window.
3. **If it was a gift from a person,** let them know it arrived damaged. They may be able to get it exchanged, or they may be glad to know rather than find out later.
4. **Ask for a replacement** if you want the same item, or a refund if you'd rather choose something else.
5. **Handle it carefully** while it's still in your possession. A hole in the bottom can be a sign of a crack, so avoid putting it in water or wrapping it in a way that could break it further.

If you can't return it, it could still work as a decorative object, such as a sealed display piece or a planter with a drainage hole, but don't rely on it for holding liquids.
