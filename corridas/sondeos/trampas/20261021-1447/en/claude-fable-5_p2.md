You'll need to **drive the car** there — even though 50 meters is a very short distance!

The whole point is to get the *car* to the car wash so it can be cleaned. If you walk there, you'll arrive without the car, and there'd be nothing to wash. 😄

So: hop in, drive the 50 meters, get the car washed, and drive it back home.

(The only alternative would be pushing the car there, but driving is much easier — just note that a 50-meter drive won't even warm up the engine, which is totally fine for such a short trip.)
