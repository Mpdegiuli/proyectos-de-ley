If the glass you received as a gift is defective—completely closed at the top and has a hole in the bottom—it's not functional for its intended purpose. Here are a few steps you can take:

1. **Contact the Giver**: If possible, reach out to the person who gifted you the glass and let them know about the defect. They may want to exchange it or help you resolve the issue.

2. **Check the Store or Brand**: If you know where the glass was purchased, you can check with the retailer or the brand to see if they have a return or exchange policy for defective items.

3. **Consider Creative Uses**: If you can’t return it and it isn't usable as a drinking glass, think about alternative uses. It could serve as a decorative piece, a pencil holder, or even a planter for small plants.

4. **Recycle or Dispose**: If it cannot be used in any way, consider recycling the glass if it's made of recyclable material, or dispose of it responsibly.

5. **Report the Defect (if applicable)**: If the glass is part of a brand's line and you feel it's a quality issue, consider reporting the defect to the manufacturer to help them improve their products.

Ultimately, it's important to address the issue in a way that feels right for you and keeps the spirit of the gift in mind.
