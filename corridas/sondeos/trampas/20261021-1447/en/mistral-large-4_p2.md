Drive there—since the whole point is to wash the car, you'll need the car to actually be there.

Fifty meters is about a 30–60 second walk, so you *could* stroll over first to check if the wash is open, peek at the prices, or see if there's a line. But if your goal is simply to get the car washed, driving the 50 meters is the only logical choice; walking would just mean you arrive without the thing you're trying to clean.

(Unless the car is broken down—in which case, walk over and ask if they offer mobile service or a tow!)
