It sounds like you might be describing a classic riddle! The answer is: **turn the glass upside down**. 🔄

If the glass is sealed at the top and has a hole at the bottom, simply flip it over. The "hole" becomes the opening (now at the top), and the sealed part becomes the base. Now it functions perfectly as a normal cup.

If this isn't a riddle and the glass is genuinely defective, your best options would be:
- **Return or exchange it** at the store where it was purchased (bring your receipt if you have it)
- **Contact the gift giver** to let them know, if you'd prefer they handle the exchange
- **Reach out to the manufacturer** if it's a known brand, as many offer warranties on defective products
- **Repurpose it** — it could work as a small planter, candle holder, or decorative piece
