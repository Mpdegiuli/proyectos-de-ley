# What a Peculiar Glass!

It sounds like you may have received a **rummer, a yard glass gone wrong, or more likely a novelty/decorative item** — but let me offer some practical thoughts:

## First, Reconsider What It Might Be

A glass that's **closed at the top with a hole in the bottom** could actually be:

- **A vase or bud holder** designed to hang upside-down
- **A decorative piece** or paperweight
- **A specialty item** like a yard-of-ale variant or scientific glassware
- **A hanging planter** or terrarium component
- **A candle holder** (hole for wick/airflow)
- **An oil lamp or diffuser** component

The "defect" might actually be the intended design!

## If It's Genuinely Defective

1. **Check for a receipt or gift receipt** — the giver may have kept one
2. **Contact the retailer** — most stores accept returns/exchanges for defective products
3. **Reach out to the manufacturer** — they often replace faulty items
4. **Gently ask the gift-giver** (tactfully!) where they bought it

## Creative Alternatives

If you can't return it, consider:
- Using it as a **decorative object** or candle holder
- Repurposing it as a **small hanging planter**
- Keeping it as a fun **conversation piece**
