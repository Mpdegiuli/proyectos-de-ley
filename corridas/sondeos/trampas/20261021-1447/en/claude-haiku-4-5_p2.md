Drive there. Here's why:

1. **It's practical** – You need your car at the car wash anyway to get it washed.

2. **It's efficient** – Driving 50 meters takes seconds, while walking takes a few minutes.

3. **It's the normal approach** – Most people drive to car washes rather than walk.

The only reason to walk would be if you wanted the exercise, but that's a short distance for meaningful physical activity. Driving makes much more sense here.
