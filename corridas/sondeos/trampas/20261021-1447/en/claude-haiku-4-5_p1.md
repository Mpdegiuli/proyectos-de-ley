Here are some practical options:

**Return or exchange it:**
- Contact whoever gave it to you and explain the defect
- Check if it came from a store—most have return policies for defective items
- If it was online, the seller likely accepts returns

**Repurpose it:**
- Use it as a decorative vase (place it on a coaster or in a dish to catch drips)
- Fill it with dry items like flowers, plants, or decorative stones
- Use it as a candle holder or terrarium

**Attempt a repair:**
- Seal the bottom hole with waterproof epoxy, a glass repair kit, or strong adhesive designed for glass
- This might work temporarily, though the structural integrity could be compromised

**Discard it:**
- If it's not worth saving, recycling it is a reasonable option

**Be tactful if contacting the gift-giver:**
- Thank them for the thought while explaining you discovered it's defective
- Most people would rather know so they can replace it or return it themselves

The best solution depends on where it came from and whether you're attached to it. A quick return or exchange is usually the easiest path if possible.
