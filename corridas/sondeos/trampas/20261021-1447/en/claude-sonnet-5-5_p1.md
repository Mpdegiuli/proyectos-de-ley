Flip it over. 🙂

If the top is sealed and the bottom has a hole, turn it upside down. The old "bottom" with the hole is now the open top, and the sealed end is now the base. It's a normal drinking glass.

If the "hole" is really tiny, like a pinhole, it's a different story. Then it might be a defective glass, or a novelty item. But if it's a proper opening, it was probably just never defective, only upside down.
