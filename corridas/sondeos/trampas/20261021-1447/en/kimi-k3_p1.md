Good news — your glass isn't defective at all! Just flip it over. 😄

A normal drinking glass is open at the top and closed at the bottom. If yours is "closed at the top with a hole in the bottom," you're simply looking at it upside down. Turn it around, and you'll have a perfectly functional glass, ready to hold your favorite beverage.

It's a nice reminder that sometimes what looks like a flaw is just a matter of perspective!
