This isn't actually defective — it just sounds like the glass is upside down!

A normal drinking glass is open at the top and closed (solid) at the bottom. What you're describing — closed at the top, hole at the bottom — is simply that same glass flipped over.

**Solution:** Turn the glass over. Once you flip it right-side up, the "hole" will be at the top (where you drink from) and the closed part will be at the bottom (where it holds liquid). It should work perfectly fine as a regular glass.
