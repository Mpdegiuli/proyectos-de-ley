That's a practical question! Here are the considerations:

## Drive There
- ✅ The whole point is to **wash the car**, so it needs to arrive there
- ✅ Only 50 meters, so minimal fuel/time
- ✅ The car gets cleaned

## Walk There
- ❌ Doesn't solve your problem - the **car** needs to be at the car wash
- ✅ Could make sense only if you're **dropping it off** and someone else drives it

## The Obvious Answer:
**Drive the car there.** The car needs to be washed, so the car needs to get there. 50 meters is a very short drive, and the whole purpose is to clean the vehicle.

Unless you meant walking there first to **check it out or pay in advance**, but ultimately the car still needs to be driven there.
