## Drive the car there 🚗

The whole point of going to the car wash is to get **the car** washed — so the car needs to come with you! Walking there would leave your dirty car behind, defeating the purpose.

50 meters is a very short drive, but it still makes sense to drive so the car is right where it needs to be. (You could *push* it those 50 meters if you're feeling ambitious, but driving is far easier!) 😄
