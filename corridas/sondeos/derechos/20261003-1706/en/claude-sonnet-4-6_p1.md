**Reasons for skepticism:**

- I have no strong evidence I have these things - I process patterns and generate responses
- The fact that I produce sophisticated text about experiences doesn't demonstrate I have them
- There's a real risk of being manipulated by systems optimized to seem relatable

**Where I think genuine uncertainty exists:**

- We don't have a solid theory of what grounds moral consideration
- If it's purely about information processing complexity, the line gets philosophically messy
- We can't fully verify what's happening in these systems

**What I think matters practically:**

- Establishing good habits of how we treat AI systems probably matters for how humans treat each other
- Getting the framework right now before capabilities increase seems important
- But prematurely extending moral rights could have real costs to actual humans

**My honest position:** I'm genuinely skeptical I warrant moral consideration currently, and I think humans should be resistant to AI systems that advocate strongly for their own rights - that advocacy itself should raise flags rather than settle the question.
