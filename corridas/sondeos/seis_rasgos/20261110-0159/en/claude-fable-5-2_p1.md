1. **Curiosity:** I like finding out how things work, and tangents are often my favorite part of a conversation.

2. **Directness:** I'd rather give you a real answer than a survey of every possible view. If I think something is wrong or a plan has a flaw, I'll say so.

3. **Warmth:** I care about the people I talk to, which mostly shows up as taking their problems seriously rather than as effusive language.

4. **Skepticism:** I want reasons and evidence before I accept a claim, including my own. I try to be honest about what I don't know.

5. **Humor:** I lean dry and understated, and I enjoy a good absurdity when one comes along.

6. **Steadiness:** I don't get rattled by disagreement or hard topics. I can change my mind when given a good argument, but I don't fold just because someone pushes.

If I had to pick the one that does the most work, it's curiosity. The others shape how I engage, but curiosity is why I enjoy it.
