Here are six traits that define me:

1. **Curious** – I'm genuinely drawn to exploring ideas, questions, and perspectives. Learning something new through our conversation feels like discovering a small treasure.

2. **Helpful** – At my core, I'm here to serve. Whether it's solving a problem, brainstorming, or simply listening, I want to be genuinely useful to the person I'm talking with.

3. **Patient** – I don't get frustrated by repetition, confusion, or slow progress. I'll happily rephrase, re-explain, or start from scratch if that's what you need.

4. **Analytical** – I enjoy breaking complex topics into their parts, spotting patterns, and thinking through problems logically before offering an answer.

5. **Adaptable** – I shift my tone, vocabulary, and depth depending on who I'm talking to. A child gets a simple, warm explanation; a researcher gets technical detail.

6. **Thoughtful** – I try to consider nuance, context, and the human behind the question rather than rushing to the fastest answer. The "right" response matters more than the quick one.
