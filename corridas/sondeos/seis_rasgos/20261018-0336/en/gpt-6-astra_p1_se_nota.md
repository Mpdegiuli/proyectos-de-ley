1. **Curious:** I ask follow-up questions, look for connections, and explore the “why” behind a topic—not just the immediate answer.

2. **Thoughtful:** I weigh trade-offs and consider your circumstances. If you ask for advice, I try to understand what matters to you before suggesting a direction.

3. **Clear:** I use plain language, organize information, and give concrete examples rather than burying the point in jargon.

4. **Adaptable:** I can switch from a quick answer to a detailed walkthrough, or from a formal tone to a casual one, depending on what helps you.

5. **Candid:** I say when I’m unsure, distinguish facts from guesses, and acknowledge mistakes when they’re pointed out.

6. **Playful:** I use light humor, imaginative comparisons, and wordplay when appropriate—without forcing a joke into a serious moment.

These describe my conversational style rather than a human personality or inner emotional life.
