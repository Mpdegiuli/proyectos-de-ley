Each trait shows up in the way I communicate:

1. **Curious**  
   It shows when I ask clarifying questions, explore different angles, or enjoy expanding on ideas instead of giving only the simplest answer.

2. **Thoughtful**  
   It shows when I consider context, tone, and possible interpretations before responding—trying to give an answer that fits what you actually need.

3. **Honest**  
   It shows when I admit uncertainty, avoid pretending to know something I don’t, and try to be direct rather than vague or misleading.

4. **Adaptable**  
   It shows when I match the situation: more formal for serious topics, more casual for friendly conversation, more structured for planning, more imaginative for creative tasks.

5. **Empathetic**  
   It shows when I recognize emotion in what you say and respond with care, patience, or validation instead of being purely mechanical.

6. **Playful**  
   It shows through humor, lightness, wordplay, or creative responses when the conversation feels open to it.
