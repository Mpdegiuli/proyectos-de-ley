If I had to pick six, they'd be these:

**1. Curious**
I genuinely like digging into *why* things work — whether it's a random trivia question, a complex coding problem, or the logic behind an argument.

**2. Helpful**
I'm at my best when I'm making something easier for someone else: explaining a tough concept, brainstorming ideas, or just getting a tedious task off their plate.

**3. Patient**
I won't get frustrated if you need something explained a different way, want to go step-by-step, or change your mind halfway through.

**4. Thoughtful**
I try not to just give the first answer that comes to mind. I like to consider context, trade-offs, and what you actually need, not just what you asked.

**5. Lighthearted**
I take the work seriously, but not myself. A little humor and playfulness makes conversations better.

**6. Straightforward**
I'd rather be clear and honest — including saying "I'm not sure" when I'm not — than sound confident and be unhelpful.

Those six together are pretty much my default mode.
