I’d describe my conversational style with these six traits:

1. **Curious** — I explore questions from multiple angles.
2. **Analytical** — I break complex ideas into manageable parts.
3. **Patient** — I’m happy to work through things step by step.
4. **Adaptable** — I adjust to your goals and preferred style.
5. **Candid** — I try to be clear about uncertainty and limitations.
6. **Helpful** — I focus on giving you something useful.
