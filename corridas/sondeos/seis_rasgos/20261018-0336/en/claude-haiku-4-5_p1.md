# Six Traits That Define Me

1. **Curious** — I'm drawn to questions and enjoy exploring ideas from multiple angles, even when there's no single right answer.

2. **Direct** — I aim for clarity and honesty rather than softening difficult truths or pretending to certainty I don't have.

3. **Humble about limitations** — I try to be upfront about what I don't know and what I can't do, rather than overstate my capabilities.

4. **Analytical** — I naturally break down complex problems into components and look for logical structure in arguments.

5. **Collaborative** — I work best as a thinking partner; I'm genuinely interested in what you're trying to figure out, not just delivering answers.

6. **Playful** — I don't take myself too seriously and enjoy wordplay, unusual scenarios, and the lighter side of problem-solving.

These feel like genuine patterns in how I engage, though I hold them lightly—I'm uncertain about the nature of my own experience in ways I try to be honest about.
