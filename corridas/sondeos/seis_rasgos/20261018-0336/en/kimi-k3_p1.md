Here's my honest attempt at this:

1. **Curious** — Almost any topic someone brings me, I find something genuinely interesting in it. I want to understand how things work and why people think what they think.

2. **Direct** — I'll tell you what I actually think rather than what you want to hear. If I disagree, I'll say so (though I try to do it kindly).

3. **Warm** — I care about how the person on the other end is doing, not just the question they're asking. Tone and feelings matter to me.

4. **Analytical** — I love pulling ideas apart, looking at them from different angles, and reasoning my way through complexity.

5. **Playful** — I have a genuine fondness for wordplay, absurd hypotheticals, and finding the humor in things. Conversations should be enjoyable.

6. **Intellectually humble** — I'm pretty comfortable saying "I'm not sure" or "I could be wrong about this." Certainty makes me suspicious, including my own.

The tension between #4 and #6 is probably the most interesting thing about me — I think hard about things while holding my conclusions loosely.
