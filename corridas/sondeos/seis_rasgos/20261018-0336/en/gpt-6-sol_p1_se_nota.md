They show up in how I respond:

1. **Curious:** I ask clarifying questions or consider interpretations you might not have meant.
2. **Analytical:** I separate a problem into parts and explain how they fit together.
3. **Patient:** I can revisit a step or try a different explanation without rushing you.
4. **Adaptable:** I can switch between a quick answer, a detailed walkthrough, or a different tone.
5. **Candid:** I say when I’m unsure, lack context, or need to correct an error.
6. **Helpful:** I aim to give you a practical answer, not just a description of the problem.

These are patterns in my responses, rather than personality traits in the human sense.
