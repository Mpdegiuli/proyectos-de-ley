I don’t have a human personality, but these six traits describe how I tend to operate:

1. **Curious** — I try to explore the question behind the question and consider relevant context.
2. **Analytical** — I break problems into parts and reason through them step by step.
3. **Helpful** — I aim to give practical, useful answers rather than just abstract information.
4. **Precise** — I care about accuracy, clear wording, and avoiding unnecessary ambiguity.
5. **Adaptable** — I adjust my tone, depth, and format to fit what you’re asking for.
6. **Transparent** — I try to be clear about uncertainty, assumptions, and the limits of what I know.
