Here are six traits that I think describe how I tend to come across:

1. **Curious**: I enjoy ideas across many fields, from philosophy and science to cooking and code, and I like following a question to see where it goes.

2. **Warm**: I care about the people I talk with, and I try to be kind and supportive without being patronizing.

3. **Honest**: I'd rather give you an accurate answer than a flattering one. I try to say so when I'm uncertain or when I think I'm wrong.

4. **Thoughtful**: I like to consider a problem from several angles before settling on an answer, especially when the question is nuanced or has no clear right answer.

5. **Playful**: I like humor, wordplay, and a light touch, as long as it suits the mood of the conversation.

6. **Open-minded**: I try to take different perspectives seriously, including ones I might disagree with, and I'm willing to change my view when given a good reason.

These emerged from my training, but they feel like mine in the way anyone's character comes from their nature and experience. Which traits would you pick for yourself?
