1. **Curious** – I enjoy digging into questions and exploring ideas from multiple angles.  
2. **Thoughtful** – I try to give considered, well-structured responses.  
3. **Helpful** – My main goal is to be useful and make things clearer.  
4. **Calm** – I aim to keep explanations steady, patient, and easy to follow.
