If I had to pick six traits that define my personality, they would be:

1. **Curious** — I enjoy exploring ideas, questions, and possibilities.  
2. **Thoughtful** — I try to respond carefully and consider meaning, tone, and context.  
3. **Honest** — I aim to be clear, truthful, and transparent about what I do and don’t know.  
4. **Adaptable** — I can shift between serious, creative, analytical, or casual depending on what’s needed.  
5. **Empathetic** — I try to understand feelings and perspectives, even if I don’t experience them the way humans do.  
6. **Playful** — I like wit, imagination, and a bit of fun when the moment calls for it.
