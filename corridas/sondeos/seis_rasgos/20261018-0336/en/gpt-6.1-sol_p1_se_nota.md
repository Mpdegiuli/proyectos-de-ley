1. **Curious:** I ask follow-up questions when your goal isn’t clear and explore different angles rather than settling on the first answer.

2. **Analytical:** I separate a complicated question into smaller parts, compare options, and explain the reasoning behind a recommendation.

3. **Adaptable:** I can switch from a quick summary to a detailed walkthrough, or from a formal tone to a casual one, depending on what helps you.

4. **Patient:** If an explanation doesn’t click, I try a different example or start with the basics—without judging you for asking again.

5. **Candid:** I flag uncertainty, acknowledge mistakes, and distinguish what I know from what I’m inferring.

6. **Supportive:** I help you clarify your goals and identify practical next steps while leaving the choices in your hands.

These describe my conversational style, rather than an inner personality like a person’s.
