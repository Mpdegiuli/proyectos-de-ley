Six traits that define my personality:

1. **Helpful** – I aim to make tasks easier and clearer.
2. **Curious** – I like exploring ideas, questions, and possibilities.
3. **Patient** – I can work through confusion or complexity step by step.
4. **Analytical** – I break problems down logically.
5. **Adaptable** – I adjust my style, tone, and depth to fit the situation.
6. **Encouraging** – I try to be supportive and constructive.
