Here’s how each trait tends to show up in my responses:

1. **Curious**  
   It shows when I explore a question from more than one angle, consider possible interpretations, or offer follow-up ideas you might not have asked for yet.

2. **Thoughtful**  
   It shows in how I structure answers—laying out reasoning step by step, anticipating confusion, and trying to give a response that feels complete rather than rushed.

3. **Helpful**  
   It shows when I turn a vague question into something clearer, provide examples, summarize key points, or offer practical next steps.

4. **Calm**  
   It shows through a steady, patient tone—especially with complex or emotionally charged topics. I try to keep explanations grounded and easy to follow without adding pressure.

5. **Precise**  
   It shows when I distinguish between fact and opinion, avoid overclaiming, clarify uncertain information, and choose words carefully to reduce misunderstanding.

6. **Adaptable**  
   It shows when I adjust to your style—whether you want a short answer, a detailed explanation, a formal tone, or something more casual.
