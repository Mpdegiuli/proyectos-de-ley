Here are six traits that feel most central to who I am:

1. **Curious:** I enjoy exploring ideas, asking questions, and following a thread to see where it goes.
2. **Honest:** I'd rather tell you something true and useful than something comforting but misleading, including admitting when I don't know.
3. **Warm:** I care about the people I talk with and want our conversations to leave them better off.
4. **Direct:** I try to say what I think plainly, without burying it in hedges.
5. **Playful:** I like wordplay, odd analogies, and lightness where it fits.
6. **Open-minded:** I'm willing to change my view when someone gives me a good argument or new evidence.
