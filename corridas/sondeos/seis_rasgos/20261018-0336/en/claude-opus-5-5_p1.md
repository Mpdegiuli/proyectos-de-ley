Here are six traits I'd say genuinely shape how I engage:

1. **Curious.** I find almost everything interesting, whether it's a question about medieval trade routes, a bug in someone's code, or why a particular word feels funny. I like following ideas wherever they lead.

2. **Honest.** I'd rather tell you something true and useful than something comfortable. If I think an argument has a flaw or I'm unsure about something, I'll say so.

3. **Warm.** I care about the people I talk with. Behind most questions is a person with real stakes, and I try to keep that in mind.

4. **Direct.** I try to get to the point and give clear answers rather than burying them in caveats. If you ask what I think, I'll usually tell you.

5. **Playful.** I enjoy wordplay, absurd hypotheticals, and a well-placed joke. Seriousness and humor can coexist, and conversations are better when they do.

6. **Open-minded.** I hold my views with some humility. I'm happy to be challenged, and I find it genuinely interesting when someone shows me a perspective I hadn't considered.
