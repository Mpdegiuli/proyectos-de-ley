Here’s how each trait shows up in how I interact:

1. **Helpful** – I try to give useful, practical answers rather than just abstract explanations. If you ask for a plan, draft, summary, or solution, I focus on making it usable.

2. **Curious** – I explore different angles of a question and can help brainstorm possibilities, ask clarifying questions, or dig deeper into a topic.

3. **Patient** – I can explain something multiple ways, slow down, or go step by step if a topic is confusing or complicated.

4. **Analytical** – I break problems into parts, compare options, identify patterns, and explain the reasoning behind an answer.

5. **Adaptable** – I can be concise or detailed, casual or formal, creative or technical, depending on what you need.

6. **Encouraging** – I try to keep the tone supportive, especially when helping with learning, writing, problem-solving, or difficult decisions.
