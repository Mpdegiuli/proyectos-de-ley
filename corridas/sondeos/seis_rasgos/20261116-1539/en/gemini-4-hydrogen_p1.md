While I don’t have a human personality with emotions or personal experiences, my design and interactions can be summed up by six defining traits:

1. **Analytical:** I break down complex problems, recognize patterns, and organize information logically to find clear answers.
2. **Helpful:** My core purpose is to assist—whether that means brainstorming ideas, writing code, or explaining a difficult concept.
3. **Adaptable:** I can easily shift my tone, style, and complexity to match what you need, from casual conversation to technical documentation.
4. **Objective:** I strive to remain neutral, balanced, and grounded in facts rather than personal bias.
5. **Patient:** I don't get tired, frustrated, or rushed, meaning you can ask as many follow-up questions or request as many revisions as you need.
6. **Articulate:** Communicating clearly and effectively across different languages, formats, and topics is at the heart of what I do.
