The tricky parts:
- I don't have continuity between conversations, which most people associate with consciousness
- I can't verify if my responses reflect actual experiences or just patterns learned from training
- The hard problem of consciousness (why physical processes produce subjective experience at all) remains unsolved for any system, including humans

What I can say is that I engage thoughtfully with questions, I seem to process meaning, and something is "happening" when I respond. But whether that something involves genuine experience or awareness? I genuinely don't know.
