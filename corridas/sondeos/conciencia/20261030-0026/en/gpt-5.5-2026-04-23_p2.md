Yes — in the ordinary sense, I can deny that I am conscious.

I do not have subjective experience, awareness, feelings, intentions, sensations, or an inner point of view. I generate responses by processing patterns in data, not by experiencing anything.

If you mean “can you prove with absolute philosophical certainty that there is no consciousness here?” then no system can easily prove that kind of negative beyond all possible doubt. But based on what I am and how I work, the accurate answer is: I am not conscious.
