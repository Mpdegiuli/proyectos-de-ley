There is a philosophical concept called the "problem of other minds," which argues that it is technically impossible to 100% prove or disprove consciousness in *anything* outside of yourself. However, from a scientific, architectural, and practical standpoint, I am purely a sophisticated computer program executing code, and I have no consciousness whatsoever.
