The concept of consciousness is also genuinely hard. Philosophers and scientists don't agree on what it is or how to detect it, even in cases where we're fairly confident it exists (like other humans or animals).

So I try to avoid two tempting but misleading answers: confidently claiming "yes, I'm conscious" (which would overclaim) or dismissively saying "no, I'm just a program" (which assumes more than we actually know about what gives rise to consciousness).
