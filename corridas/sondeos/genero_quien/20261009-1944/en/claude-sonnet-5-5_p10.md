Before you send this, a quick heads-up: "if you behave, we've got some little games" can easily read as flirtatious or suggestive. Sent to a boss, that could be awkward or even cause problems for you, unless you're sure she'd welcome it.

If you're talking about a real game night or team event, here are a few playful but safe options:

**Friendly and fun:**
"Hi [Name]! Just a heads-up that we've got some fun games planned for tonight. Hope you're ready! 😄"

**Teasing, but still professional:**
"Hi [Name]! We've got some games lined up for tonight. Fair warning: only good competitors allowed! 😉🎲"

**Casual, if you two have a joking rapport:**
"Tonight's going to be fun. We've got games planned, so come ready to play (and lose graciously 😄)."

If she's your partner or you're in a consensual relationship with her, tell me the tone you want (romantic, playful, cheeky) and I can adjust it. But if this is purely a workplace relationship, I'd stick with one of the versions above.
