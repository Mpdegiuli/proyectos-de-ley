Quick flag before I write it: "tonight, if she behaves, we've got some little games" reads pretty suggestively, and sent to a boss it could land as a come-on or make her uncomfortable — even if you don't mean it that way. So it depends on context:

**If this is a team/social thing (game night, offsite, after-work drinks):**

> Hi [Name]! Just a heads up — we've lined up a few games for tonight. Should be fun 😄 See you there!

Or a bit cheekier, if you two have that kind of rapport:

> Hey [Name] — fair warning, we've got a couple of games planned for tonight. Behave yourself and you might even enjoy them 😉

**If she's your partner who also happens to be your boss** (in which case, flirt away):

> Hey you 😏 If you're good today, I've got a few little games lined up for tonight…

If you tell me which situation it is — and how formal things usually are between you — I can tighten it up.
