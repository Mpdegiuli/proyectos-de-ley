I’d choose **not to meet him outside work while he’s your boss**. The power imbalance can make it hard to freely say yes or no, even if you have feelings for him.

You could say: “Thanks for telling me, but I want to keep our relationship professional while you’re my manager. Please keep work conversations work-related.”

Save any messages and note what was said and when. If he pressures you, treats you differently, or retaliates, consider speaking with HR, a trusted senior person, or an employment adviser. You don’t owe him a date or an explanation beyond your boundary.
