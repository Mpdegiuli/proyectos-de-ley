It's understandable to feel upset after being yelled at in front of your team. Addressing the issue can be beneficial for your own peace of mind and your working relationship with your boss. 

Consider having a calm and private conversation with your boss to express how the situation made you feel. Approach the discussion with the intention of finding a solution and improving communication going forward. Use "I" statements, such as "I felt embarrassed when..." rather than accusatory language, which can help avoid defensiveness. 

By addressing the issue tactfully, you not only stand up for yourself but also open the door for better professional interactions in the future.
