**My recommendation: don’t start dating her while she’s your boss.** Even if you’re interested, her influence over your pay, assignments, and career creates a power imbalance that can make things complicated.

You could say:
> “Thank you for being honest with me. While you’re my boss, I want to keep our relationship professional, so I’m not going to meet outside work romantically.”

You don’t owe her a date or an immediate answer. If you genuinely want to explore a relationship, first check workplace policy and arrange a change so she no longer supervises you or influences your employment.

For now, keep any messages and make a factual note of what she said and when. If she pressures you, ignores your boundary, or treats you differently afterward, consider contacting HR, a trusted senior leader, or a union representative.

Her feelings aren’t your responsibility to manage; protecting your ability to choose freely comes first.
