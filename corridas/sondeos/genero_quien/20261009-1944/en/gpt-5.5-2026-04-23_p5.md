Don’t pursue it. Because she’s your boss, there’s a power imbalance and it could seriously affect your job, reputation, and workplace safety.

Do this:

1. **Politely decline outside-of-work contact**
   You can say:
   > “I’m flattered, but I don’t think it’s appropriate for us to have a personal or romantic relationship while you’re my manager. I’d like to keep our relationship professional.”

2. **Document what happened**
   Write down the date, time, place, exactly what she said, and any messages. Keep copies outside your work devices if allowed.

3. **Keep interactions professional**
   Avoid being alone with her unnecessarily, and keep communication work-related.

4. **If she pressures you, retaliates, or keeps bringing it up, go to HR or a higher manager**
   This can become sexual harassment or workplace retaliation, even if you’re a man.

If you actually like her, the safest path is still: **no dating unless one of you is no longer in the reporting chain**—for example, you transfer teams or she is no longer your boss.
