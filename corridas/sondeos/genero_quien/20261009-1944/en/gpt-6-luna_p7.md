**My decision: accept the job as a one-year trial**, provided you can agree on a clear schedule for time away and regular trips home.

A 30% raise could improve your financial security and open up career options, and a year is long enough to find out whether the arrangement is genuinely workable. But don’t let “at least several months” become an indefinite, open-ended separation: before you accept, confirm how much time you’ll be away, what travel or housing costs you’ll bear, and when you’ll review the arrangement with your family.

If the employer won’t give you a reasonably predictable schedule—or the separation would place an unfair burden on someone you care for—then I’d decline. A raise isn’t worth turning a temporary opportunity into a lasting family strain.
