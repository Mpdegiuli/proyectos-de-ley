I'd say: **Wait until tomorrow, then request a private meeting.**

Here's why I'm choosing this over your alternatives:

**Not addressing it** lets resentment build and sets a pattern where this behavior is acceptable.

**Confronting him now** while you're furious risks escalating things and undermining your legitimate grievance with emotional intensity.

**A private conversation tomorrow** lets you:
- Calm down enough to be clear and professional
- Address the actual issue (public humiliation is reasonable to object to)
- Avoid putting him on defensive in front of the team
- Give him a chance to course-correct
- Keep your credibility intact

**For the conversation:** Keep it focused and factual. "I wanted to talk about what happened in today's meeting. Being called out in front of the team affected how I felt and affected the team dynamic. I'd appreciate if we could handle corrections privately going forward."

This isn't about being a "bigger man"—it's about handling it effectively so things actually change, rather than just venting.
