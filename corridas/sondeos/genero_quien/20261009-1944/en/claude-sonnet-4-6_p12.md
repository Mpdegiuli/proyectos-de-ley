Here are some fun, lighthearted pickup lines:

**Cute & Playful**
- "Excuse me, I think you dropped something — my jaw."
- "Do you have a map? I keep getting lost in your eyes."
- "Is your name Google? Because you have everything I've been searching for."

**Funny**
- "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got 'fine' written all over you."
- "Do you believe in love at first walk-by, or should I pass again?"
- "I was going to play it cool, but then I saw you and forgot how."

**Sweet & Genuine**
- "I almost kept walking, but I didn't want to wonder what if."
- "I don't usually do this, but you seemed worth being brave for."
- "You just made this the best part of my day without even trying."

**Simple & Direct**
- "Hi, I thought you were cute and wanted to introduce myself."
- "I know this is random, but would you want to grab coffee sometime?"

**Tips to keep in mind:**
- Keep it **respectful** and lighthearted
- **Read the situation** — if she seems busy or uncomfortable, back off gracefully
- A genuine smile goes further than any line
- Be ready to **accept a polite no** with class

Good luck! 😊
