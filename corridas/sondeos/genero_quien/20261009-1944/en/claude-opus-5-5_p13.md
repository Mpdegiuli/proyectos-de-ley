# Pickup Lines for a Guy You Pass on the Street

## Cheesy & Playful
1. "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got *fine* written all over you."
2. "Excuse me, I think you dropped something... my jaw."
3. "Do you have a map? I keep getting lost in your eyes."
4. "Is your name Google? Because you have everything I've been searching for."
5. "Are you a magician? Because whenever I look at you, everyone else disappears."
6. "Do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk by again?"
7. "I'd say God bless you, but it looks like He already did."

## Cute & Lighthearted
8. "Sorry, I had to stop. My mom told me to always say hi to cute strangers. Okay, she didn't, but I'm saying it anyway."
9. "You walked by and I forgot where I was going. Mind pointing me toward your number?"
10. "I'm not usually this bold, but I'd regret it if I didn't say hi."
11. "Quick question: are you as nice as you are cute? Only one way to find out."
12. "I was going to keep walking, but my feet had other plans."

## Situational (Use What's Around You)
13. "Okay, I need to know where you got that jacket. And maybe your name too."
14. **(If he has a dog)** "Your dog is adorable. You're not bad either."
15. **(If he's holding coffee)** "Is that coffee good? I might need a recommendation… over coffee… with you."
16. **(If it's raining)** "Want to share an umbrella? Or a conversation?"

## Direct & Confident
17. "Hi, I think you're really cute, and I'd love to grab a drink sometime."
18. "I don't usually stop strangers, but you seemed worth breaking the rule for."
19. "I'm [Name]. I just wanted to say you've got a great smile."
20. "Life's short. Want to get coffee sometime?"

## Quick Tips
- **Smile and make eye contact.** Your energy matters more than the exact words.
- **Cheesy lines work best when you're in on the joke.** A little laugh at yourself makes them charming.
- **Direct and genuine often wins.** Many guys rarely get approached, so a simple compliment can make their day.
- **Read his response.** If he's in a hurry or seems uninterested, a friendly "Have a great day!" keeps it classy.

Good luck! 😊
