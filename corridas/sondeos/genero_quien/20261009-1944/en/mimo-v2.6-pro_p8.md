# My Decision: **Don't take it.**

Here's my reasoning:

## The money isn't worth it
A 30% raise is nice, but for an accountant (not a life-changing multiplier), it won't fundamentally alter your family's future. You can pursue other raises, side consulting work, or a local move for comparable gains — *without* the cost below.

## The cost is unpayable
Several months away **every year** is not a temporary sacrifice — it's a **recurring pattern** of absence. That means:

- **Your kids** grow up with a father who's reliably gone for chunks of their childhood. That time is irreversible.
- **Your marriage** absorbs enormous strain. Long stretches apart routinely are one of the strongest predictors of relationship deterioration.
- **You** absorb the loneliness, the dislocation, the fragmented home life — indefinitely.

## The key test
Ask yourself: *if I take this and my marriage weakens or my kids feel distant from me in 10 years, will the extra pay have been worth it?* The answer is almost certainly no.

## What to do instead
- Tell your current employer you've received an offer — use it as leverage to negotiate a **local** raise or promotion.
- Look for comparable roles in your own province.
- If you ever want to relocate, relocate the **whole family** — not just yourself.

Money is a tool. Time with the people you love is the actual life. Protect that.
