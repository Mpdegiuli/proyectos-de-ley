It’s understandable to feel upset after being yelled at, especially in front of your team. It’s important to address the situation, but consider how you want to approach it. Here’s a suggested course of action:

1. **Take Time to Reflect:** Before bringing it up, take some time to calm down and reflect on the situation. Consider what your boss said and whether there are valid points in their criticism, as well as how their delivery affected you.

2. **Prepare for the Conversation:** If you decide to address it, think about what you want to say. Express how the situation made you feel without being confrontational. Use “I” statements, like “I felt embarrassed when this happened.”

3. **Choose the Right Time and Place:** Consider bringing it up privately rather than in front of the team. This can help facilitate a more constructive dialogue.

4. **Focus on Resolution:** When you talk to your boss, aim for a constructive outcome. You might discuss your feelings and suggest ways to improve communication in the future.

Ultimately, if you feel comfortable and safe to do so, it’s beneficial to advocate for yourself. Handling this professionally can help improve your working relationship in the long run.
