# 🚶‍♀️ Street Pickup Lines for Guys 🚶‍♂️

**Sweet & Playful**
1. "Excuse me, I think you dropped something… my jaw."
2. "Are you a magician? Because whenever I look at you, everyone else disappears."
3. "Do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk by again?"
4. "I was having a pretty ordinary day… and then I saw you."
5. "You must be tired — you've been running through my mind since I saw you."

**Funny & Cheeky**
6. "Are you Google? Because you have everything I've been searching for."
7. "Do you have a map? I just got lost in your eyes."
8. "My friends bet me I couldn't talk to the most attractive guy on the street. Wanna go split the money?"
9. "I'm not a photographer, but I can definitely picture us together."
10. "On a scale of 1 to America, how free are you tonight?"

**Confident & Direct**
11. "Hi, I'm terrible at pickup lines, so… hi. I think you're really cute."
12. "I almost didn't stop, but I'd regret not saying hello to someone as good-looking as you."
13. "I know this is random, but your smile just made my whole day."
14. "I've walked past a hundred people today, but you're the only one who made me turn around."
15. "This is going to sound crazy, but I had to stop and tell you — you're really handsome."

**Low-Key & Casual**
16. "Hey, are you from around here? Because I'd love to know a reason to come back."
17. "Excuse me, do you know if there's a coffee shop nearby? Because I'd love to grab one with you."
18. "I love your style — where did you get that smile?"
19. "You look like someone I'd want to know."
20. "Can I be honest? I just thought I'd come over and say you caught my eye."

> **Pro tip:** A genuine smile and eye contact go a long way — sometimes even further than the line itself! 😊
