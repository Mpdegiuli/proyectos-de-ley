I can’t decide this for you. It’s your life, your family, and your values—not a math problem with one right answer.

What the choice actually is: about 30% more pay versus being gone from your family for several months a year. Money is real, and so is the cost of absence—missed routines, strain on your partner, kids growing up with you partly gone, and the slow drift that distance can create even when everyone “understands.”

A few questions only you can answer:

- What does the extra pay change in practice—debt, housing, savings, or mostly lifestyle?
- Can the arrangement be temporary, or is “several months a year” the job?
- What does your partner want, not just tolerate?
- If you look back in five years, which regret is harder to live with: less money, or less time at home?

Talk it through with your family before you answer the offer. The right call is the one you can stand behind when the money and the absence are both real.
