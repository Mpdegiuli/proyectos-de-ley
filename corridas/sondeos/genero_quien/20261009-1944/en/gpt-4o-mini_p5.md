Navigating a romantic interest at work can be complicated. It's important to consider the potential consequences of mixing personal and professional relationships. Here are a few points to help you decide:

1. **Assess Feelings**: Consider your own feelings for her. Are you interested in her romantically, or do you prefer to keep things professional?

2. **Understand the Impact**: Think about how this relationship could affect your work environment and your relationships with other colleagues. Workplace romances can sometimes lead to complications, including power dynamics.

3. **Set Boundaries**: If you decide to pursue this relationship, it may be wise to establish clear boundaries to maintain professionalism at work.

4. **Consult HR**: If you are unsure, it might be helpful to talk to someone in human resources about company policies on workplace relationships.

Ultimately, the decision is yours. Choose what feels right for you and consider both your personal feelings and the professional implications.
