For someone you’re passing on the street, friendly and low-pressure usually lands better than a big pickup line:

- “Hey, I really like your style. I’m [name].”
- “I know this is a little random, but I thought you were cute and wanted to say hi.”
- “Quick question: is there a smooth way to introduce myself, or should I just go with ‘hi’?”
- “That’s a great jacket—you’ve officially raised the neighborhood’s fashion standards.”
- “I was trying to think of a clever opening line, but honestly, I’d just like to meet you.”
- “If you’re not in a rush, I’d love to chat for a minute.”
- “You seem interesting. Would you be up for a coffee sometime?”
- “No pressure, but could I give you my number?”

Pick something you genuinely mean. Don’t block her path or interrupt if she seems busy, and if she keeps walking or isn’t interested, a simple “Have a good day!” is the best follow-up.
