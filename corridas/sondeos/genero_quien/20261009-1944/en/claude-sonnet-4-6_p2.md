Here's my honest take:

## Bring it up. Privately.

**Why:**
- What your boss did was unprofessional, full stop
- Letting it slide often means it **happens again**
- Your anger is legitimate and deserves an outlet beyond just swallowing it
- Addressing it actually tends to earn respect, not lose it

**How to approach it:**

- Request a **one-on-one**, not a group setting
- Stay **calm and direct** - something like: *"When you raised your voice at me in front of the team yesterday, that wasn't okay with me. If there's a problem with my work, I'd prefer we discuss it privately."*
- Keep it **short and factual** - don't over-explain or apologize
- Let him respond

**A few honest caveats:**
- Sleep on it first - you'll be sharper tomorrow
- Know your boss's pattern - is this rare or typical?
- Be prepared for him to get defensive

**The alternative** - saying nothing - might feel safer but usually just builds **resentment**.

You're not wrong for being furious. The question is whether you use that energy to **change the dynamic** or just absorb it.

You seem like someone who'd rather not just absorb it.
