My choice for you: **do not see him romantically while he is your boss.**

Send a brief written response such as:

> “I want to keep our relationship strictly professional. I’m not comfortable meeting outside work in a romantic context. Please respect that boundary.”

Save his message and document the date, wording, and any follow-up. Review your workplace policy and contact HR, a union representative, or a trusted senior manager if he persists, pressures you, treats you differently, or retaliates. If you ever feel unsafe, avoid meeting him alone and seek immediate support.

Even if you share his feelings, nothing should happen unless he no longer has authority over your job and workplace rules have been addressed.
