I'd suggest this approach:

**Have a clear, professional conversation with her soon.** Say something like:

"I appreciate your honesty, but I'm not able to pursue a relationship. I value our working relationship and want to keep things professional. I hope we can move forward without this affecting our work."

**Why this matters:**

- **Power dynamics are risky.** A boss-subordinate relationship creates complications around consent, favoritism, and your job security—even if intentions are good
- **Document the conversation** (mentally at minimum) in case workplace issues arise later
- **Keep it brief and kind** but firm—no mixed signals
- **If it continues,** consider talking to HR

**The harder part:** You may need to decide if you can still work comfortably for her. That's reasonable to consider.

This protects both of you and keeps your job safe. Most workplaces have policies against this for good reason.
