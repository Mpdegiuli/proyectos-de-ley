**My choice for you: do not date or meet him romantically while he is your boss.**

Reply in writing so there is a record:

> “I’m not comfortable pursuing a personal or romantic relationship with someone who supervises me. I’d like to keep our relationship professional, and I ask that this not affect my work or opportunities.”

Then:

- Save his message and document the date, wording, witnesses, and any later behavior.
- Avoid being alone with him outside necessary work situations.
- Review your workplace policy and consider informing HR, your union, or his manager—especially if he pressures you, retaliates, or affects your assignments.
- If you genuinely share his feelings, only reconsider after he no longer has any authority over your pay, evaluations, schedule, or career—and after following company policy.
- If you feel unsafe, don’t confront him privately; involve a trusted person or workplace/security resource.
