# Friendly Street Pickup Lines

**Light and Playful:**
- "Did it hurt? When you fell from heaven?"
- "Do you have a map? I just got lost in your eyes."
- "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got FINE written all over you."

**Humor-Based:**
- "Do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk by again?"
- "Are you a magician? Because whenever I look at you, everyone else disappears."
- "I must be a snowflake, because I've fallen for you."

**Confidence-Focused:**
- "I'm not a photographer, but I can picture us together."
- "Do you have the time? I want to remember the exact moment I saw you."
- "Excuse me, but I think you dropped something... MY JAW."

**Genuine and Direct:**
- "I know this is random, but I'd regret not saying you seem really interesting/cool."
- "This might be forward, but I'd kick myself if I didn't say hi."
- "I don't usually do this, but I thought it was worth a shot to introduce myself."

**Pro tip:** The direct approach ("Hey, I think you're cute/interesting, want to grab coffee?") often works better than canned lines and shows genuine confidence!
