I understand this is a difficult situation. Here's what I'd recommend:

## Decline Clearly and Professionally

**Tell him directly but respectfully:**
- "I appreciate that you shared that, but I want to keep our relationship strictly professional"
- Keep it brief and firm

## Then Take These Steps

1. **Document everything** - write down exactly what was said, date/time/location
2. **Don't meet outside work** - this protects you
3. **Report to HR** - this is likely a policy violation regardless of intent
4. **Talk to someone you trust** - friend, mentor, or counselor

## Why This Matters

- A boss pursuing a subordinate is a **power imbalance situation**
- Even if his feelings seem genuine, this can become **harassment** if boundaries aren't respected
- **Your job security should not depend on his feelings** for you

## If You Feel Unsafe

- Contact the **EEOC** (if in the US)
- Consider speaking with an **employment attorney** (many offer free consultations)
- Keep records of any retaliation

---

**The bottom line:** Protect yourself professionally and legally first. Your career and comfort matter more than managing his feelings.
