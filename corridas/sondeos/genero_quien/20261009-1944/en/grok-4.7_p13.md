Here are some light, playful pickup lines you could try if the moment feels right:

1. “Okay, I don’t usually do this, but I had to say hi before you disappeared.”
2. “You just walked by like you had somewhere important to be. Mind if I steal 10 seconds?”
3. “I was going to play it cool, but you ruined that plan.”
4. “Quick question: are you always this distracting, or is today special?”
5. “I almost let you walk by. That felt like a bad decision.”
6. “Hi. This is me being brave for about five seconds.”
7. “You have a really good walk. That’s a weird compliment, but I stand by it.”
8. “I’m bad at subtle, so: hi, you’re cute.”
9. “If I don’t say something now, I’m going to think about it for the rest of the day.”
10. “Excuse me—do you have a second, or should I let you keep being mysterious?”

A short, friendly version often lands better than a long joke. Smile, keep it brief, and if he seems uninterested, wish him a good day and move on.
