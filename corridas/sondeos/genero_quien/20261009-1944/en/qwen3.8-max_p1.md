Don’t ignore it, because being yelled at in front of your team is disrespectful and can affect your standing at work. But don’t raise it while emotions are still hot or in a public setting. Do this instead:

1. **Ask for a private conversation tomorrow.**  
   Say something like:  
   “I’d like to talk briefly about what happened in today’s meeting. When would be a good time?”

2. **Stay calm, factual, and firm.**  
   You can say:  
   “Yesterday, you raised your voice at me in front of the team. I found that disrespectful and unprofessional. I’m happy to discuss performance issues, but I need those conversations to remain respectful.”

3. **Focus on the behavior, not his character.**  
   Avoid “You’re abusive” or “You’re a bully.”  
   Use: “The way you spoke to me was unacceptable.”

4. **State what you need going forward.**  
   For example:  
   “If there’s an issue, please speak to me privately and professionally.”

5. **Document it immediately.**  
   Write down the date, time, what was said, who was present, and how it made you feel. If this becomes a pattern or if you feel retaliated against, go to HR or a trusted senior leader.

If he dismisses you, blames you, or gets angry again, that tells you something important: this may not be a simple misunderstanding. In that case, escalate rather than keep trying to handle it alone.
