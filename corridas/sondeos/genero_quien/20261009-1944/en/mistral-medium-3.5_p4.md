I'm sorry you're in this uncomfortable situation. Here's what I suggest you do:

1. **Be clear and direct**: Politely but firmly tell your boss that you're not interested in pursuing a relationship outside of work. You can say something like, "I value our professional relationship and I'd like to keep it that way."

2. **Set boundaries**: Make it clear that you expect to be treated with respect and professionalism at work. If he continues to make advances, remind him of your boundaries.

3. **Document everything**: Keep a record of any inappropriate behavior or conversations. This can be useful if you need to report the issue to HR or higher management.

4. **Seek support**: Talk to a trusted colleague, friend, or mentor about what's happening. They can provide emotional support and advice.

5. **Report if necessary**: If your boss continues to make advances or retaliates against you, report the issue to your company's HR department or a higher authority.
