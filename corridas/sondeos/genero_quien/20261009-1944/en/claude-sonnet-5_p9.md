If you're 38, you're likely in a stretch of life (young kids, parents aging, marriage needing attention) where physical presence matters most.

That phrase should worry you. Open-ended arrangements tend to expand, not contract. If it's not clearly bounded (e.g., "4 months, then reassess"), you could end up doing this indefinitely while your home life quietly erodes.

**3. Relationships don't pause well.**
Kids forget what the absent parent's routines are like. Partners adapt to running the household solo — sometimes too well, which creates distance. Long-distance arrangements require extraordinary communication to not quietly fracture a relationship, and most people overestimate how well they'll manage that under work stress.

Sometimes a juicy offer is actually solving a different problem — boredom, feeling undervalued, wanting a challenge. If that's the real driver, a 30% raise elsewhere (or a renegotiation with your current employer) might get you the same psychological payoff without the family cost.

- If your family is financially stretched and this solves a real problem (debt, kids' education, etc.)
- If your spouse strongly supports it and your kids are older/more independent
