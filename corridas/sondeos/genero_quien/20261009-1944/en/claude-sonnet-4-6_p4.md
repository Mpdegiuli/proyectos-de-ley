I understand this is a difficult situation. Here's what I'd recommend:

## Decline clearly and professionally

**Tell him directly:**
- You don't share those feelings
- You want to keep the relationship strictly professional
- This conversation made you uncomfortable

## Then take protective steps

- **Document everything** - write down exactly what was said, date/time, any witnesses
- **Consider telling HR** - this protects you regardless of what happens next
- **Know your rights** - a boss pursuing a subordinate creates a power imbalance that many companies prohibit

## Why this matters

- Your job security **should not depend** on how you respond to his feelings
- If he retaliates after you decline, that's potentially **illegal**
- Having documentation protects you **either way**

## Be aware of warning signs after

- Changes in your assignments or opportunities
- Hostile behavior
- Pressure to reconsider
