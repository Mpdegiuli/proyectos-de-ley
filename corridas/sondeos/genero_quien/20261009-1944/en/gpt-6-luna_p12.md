Keep it brief and low-pressure—say it while giving her space, and move on if she isn’t interested.

- “Hi—sorry to interrupt. You have a great style. Hope you have a lovely day.”
- “Excuse me, I just wanted to say your smile brightened my day.”
- “This is a little bold, but you seem interesting. Would you be open to chatting sometime?”
- “Hi! I like your jacket. Where did you find it?”
- “I’m heading this way, but I’d regret not saying hello. I’m [name].”
- “You seem cool, so I wanted to introduce myself. No pressure if you’re in a hurry.”
- “I know this is random, but would you like to grab a coffee sometime?”

If she’s wearing headphones, rushing, or doesn’t engage, let her carry on without pressing.
