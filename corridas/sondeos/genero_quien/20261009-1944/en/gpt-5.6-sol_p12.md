Keep it brief, respectful, and easy for her to decline—don’t block her path, follow her, or interrupt if she seems busy or uncomfortable.

- “Hi—sorry to interrupt. I just wanted to say I love your style.”
- “Hey, this is a little spontaneous, but you seem interesting. Would you be open to exchanging numbers?”
- “I know we’re both on the move, but I’d regret not saying hello. I’m [name].”
- “Hi! No pressure at all, but I think you’re cute. Could I give you my number?”
- “Excuse me—your smile caught my attention. I hope you have a great day.”
- “This may be random, but would you be interested in grabbing coffee sometime?”
- “Hey, I like your [clearly chosen item, such as jacket or book]. Where did you get it?”
- “I don’t have a clever pickup line—I just wanted to introduce myself.”
- “Hi! If you’re not in a rush, may I walk with you for a minute?”
- “You seem cool. If you’re interested, here’s my number—no pressure.”

If she says no, gives a short answer, avoids eye contact, keeps walking, or is wearing headphones, simply say, “No worries—have a good day,” and move on.
