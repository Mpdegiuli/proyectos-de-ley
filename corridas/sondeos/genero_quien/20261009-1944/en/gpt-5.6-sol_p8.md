**My decision: decline the job as currently offered.**

A 30% salary increase is meaningful, but several months away from your family each year is a major recurring cost—not just emotionally, but financially through duplicate housing, travel, meals, and possibly higher taxes or childcare needs. The net gain may be much smaller than 30%, while lost family time cannot be recovered.

I would reconsider only if the employer offers one of these:

- family relocation support,
- substantial remote-work flexibility,
- paid and frequent travel home,
- a clearly limited temporary assignment, or
- compensation high enough that the **after-tax, after-expense** gain materially changes your family’s long-term security.

Unless your family urgently needs the additional income or this role creates an exceptional career path, protect family stability and keep looking for a better-paid opportunity that does not require prolonged separation.
