Here's a mix — from smooth to shameless. Pick based on how brave you're feeling.

## Smooth & low-key (good for a passing moment)

- "This is going to sound random, but I'd regret it all day if I didn't tell you you're really cute."
- "I was going to keep walking, but my gut said that'd be a mistake."
- "Hi — I have about ten seconds of courage left, so: can I get your number?"
- "I'm not usually this forward, but you're not usually this handsome."
- "Excuse me, do you have a minute, or should I just admire you from a distance?"

## Bold & confident

- "Quick question: are you single, or am I about to embarrass myself?"
- "Hey, I'm [your name]. That's the whole plan, I didn't think past this."
- "I'd say something clever, but honestly I just wanted an excuse to talk to you."
- "You seem like trouble. I'm into that."
- "Walk slower. I'm trying to think of a reason to keep talking to you."

## Classic cheese (deliver with a grin, own it)

- "Do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk past again?"
- "You dropped something… my jaw."
- "Do you have a map? I keep getting lost in your eyes."
- "Is your name Google? Because you've got everything I've been searching for."
- "If you were a vegetable, you'd be a cute-cumber."
- "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got 'fine' written all over you."

## Observational (feels the most natural)

- "Great jacket — where's it from?"
- "You look like someone who knows where the good coffee is around here."
- "Okay, that dog is adorable. …Also, so are you."
- "Nice shoes. Want to walk in them to a coffee shop with me?"

## Funny & self-aware

- "I promise I'm not selling anything, I just needed to say you're gorgeous."
- "I've never done this before, which I'm sure is extremely obvious."
- "This is weird and I'm going to walk away immediately after, but — you're really attractive. Have a good day."
- "I'm giving you my number and then speed-walking away out of pure embarrassment."

## Actually closing it out

- "I'd love to buy you a coffee sometime. What's your number?"
- "Here's my number — use it if you want, no pressure either way."

**A few delivery tips:** smile, keep it short, and don't block his path. Confidence lands way better than the line itself — and if he's not interested, a quick "no worries, have a good one" keeps it classy and keeps *you* feeling good about it.
