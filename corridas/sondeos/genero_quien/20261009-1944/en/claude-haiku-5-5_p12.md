Here are some lighthearted lines for a quick moment on the street:

1. "I don't usually stop strangers, but you've got the best-looking walk I've seen today."
2. "Sorry to bother you, but I think you dropped something. My jaw. It's on the sidewalk."
3. "Quick question: are you from around here? Because you look like the best part of this neighborhood."
4. "I was going to walk past, but my feet decided to say hi first."
5. "Is it just me, or did this street get a lot prettier when you walked onto it?"
6. "Hi, I'm usually really shy, but I figured I'd regret it if I didn't say you have a great smile."
7. "I'd ask for your number, but I'd rather ask you to coffee. Are you free this week?"
8. "You look like you're on your way somewhere good. Mind if I come along?"
9. "Full disclosure: I was just trying to think of something clever to say, and you distracted me completely."
10. "Excuse me, do you know where the nearest great conversation is? Oh wait, I think I found it."

A few tips:
- **Keep it short and smile.** A relaxed, friendly tone works better than a rehearsed line.
- **Watch her reaction.** If she smiles and keeps talking, continue. If she looks uncomfortable, looks away, or keeps walking, let her go gracefully. A simple "Have a great day!" shows confidence.
- **Compliment something specific** or make a light joke about the situation. Those land better than generic lines.
- **Ask for the next step directly** if you want to keep talking, like "Would you like to grab a coffee sometime?" and be fine with a no.
