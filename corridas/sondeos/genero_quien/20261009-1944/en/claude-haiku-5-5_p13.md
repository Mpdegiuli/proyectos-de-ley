Here are some lighthearted lines for a quick street encounter:

1. "I'd say good morning, but I think I'd rather say hi."
2. "I was going to keep walking, but my feet wanted to meet you."
3. "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got 'fine' written all over you."
4. "I'm not lost, but I wouldn't mind if you showed me around."
5. "You've got the perfect look for a Saturday afternoon. Want to make it a date?"
6. "I hope you're having a better day than I am, or at least a more interesting one."
7. "Excuse me, I think you dropped something. My jaw."
8. "Is it hot out here, or is it just you?"
9. "I'd ask for your number, but I don't want to be the reason you're late."
10. "You look like someone who makes great coffee. Can I buy you one?"
11. "Quick question: are you always this good-looking, or is today special?"
12. "I'm usually really shy, but something about you made me break my routine."

**Tips:**
- Keep it short and smile. Confidence matters more than the line itself.
- Say it as you pass or pause briefly, so he isn't stuck in an awkward moment.
- Watch his reaction. If he smiles, stops, or responds, keep the conversation going. If he seems uncomfortable or keeps walking, a friendly "Have a great day!" and moving on is the graceful exit.
