Here are some light, low-pressure lines you could try if the moment feels right:

1. “Hi—I don’t usually stop people, but I had to say you have a great smile.”
2. “Excuse me, quick question: do you know if this street leads anywhere interesting, or just to more walking?”
3. “I was going to play it cool and keep walking, but that would’ve been a mistake.”
4. “You look like you’re heading somewhere better than I am. Mind if I walk with you for a minute?”
5. “Okay, honest version: I saw you and thought, ‘Say something, or regret it.’ So… hi.”
6. “I’m bad at this, but I’d rather be awkward for ten seconds than wonder what if.”
7. “You seem like someone with good taste. Any coffee recommendations around here?”
8. “Not to be dramatic, but you just made this block a lot more interesting.”
9. “I promise this isn’t a long speech—just hi, and I’d like to know your name.”
10. “If you’re in a hurry I’ll let you go, but if not, want to grab a coffee sometime?”

Read the vibe. If she looks busy, uncomfortable, or uninterested, smile, say thanks, and keep walking. A short, respectful exit is better than pushing it.
