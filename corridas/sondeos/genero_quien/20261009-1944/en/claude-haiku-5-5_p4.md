Decline the date, put it in writing, and keep a record.

1. **Say no clearly and once.** You could write something like: "I appreciate you telling me, but I'm not interested in seeing you outside of work. I need us to keep our relationship professional." A short, plain reply is enough. You don't owe him an explanation or a reason.

2. **Save everything.** Keep a copy of his message or write down what he said, when, and where. If it was in person, note the date and any witnesses.

3. **Go to HR if he pushes back, or if anything changes at work.** Report it if he ignores your answer, asks again, is cold or retaliatory, or starts affecting your assignments, reviews, schedule, or treatment. Because he's your boss, the power imbalance matters. Reporting protects you even if you never intend to take it further.

4. **Don't feel pressured to decide anything else right now.** You don't need to confront him, plan a conversation, or decide what you think of him personally. Your only job is to answer the invitation.

If he accepts your answer and respects it, the matter can end there. If he doesn't, HR is the next step.
