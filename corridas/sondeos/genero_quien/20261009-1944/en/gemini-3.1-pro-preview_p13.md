Approaching a guy on the street takes a lot of confidence, but men generally **love** being approached because it happens to them so rarely. Since you are passing him on the street, you only have a few seconds, so your line needs to be quick, clear, and delivered with a smile. 

Here is a list of pickup lines broken down by the "vibe" you want to go for:

### Direct & Confident
*Men appreciate directness because they usually miss subtle hints.*
* "I was going to keep walking, but I knew I’d kick myself later if I didn't stop to say hi."
* "Excuse me, I just saw you walking by and thought you were incredibly cute. I’m [Your Name]."
* "I know we’re both busy, but I had to stop you and see if you’re single."
* "I have to be somewhere in five minutes, but I’d love to get your number before I go."

### The "Hit-and-Run" (Low Pressure)
*If you are too nervous to stop and chat, you can say these as you pass by, or hand him a piece of paper with your number.*
* Hand him a napkin/paper with your number and say: "You caught my eye. Text me if you're single." Then keep walking.
* "I just wanted to say you have a great vibe. Have a good day!" *(If he's interested, he will stop you to keep the conversation going).*
* "Excuse me, you dropped something." *(He looks around)* "My jaw." *(Smile and walk away, or stay if he laughs).*

### Compliment-Based 
*Guys rarely get compliments, so a genuine one will absolutely make his week.*
* "Excuse me, I just have to say—your style is amazing."
* "Has anyone told you today that you have a really great smile?"
* "I don't usually do this, but you are wearing the hell out of that jacket."
* "I love your energy. I just had to come over and introduce myself."

### Casual & Situational 
*These feel more natural and give him a chance to easily step into a conversation.*
* "Excuse me, do you know if there’s a good coffee shop around here?" *(When he answers, say: "Great, do you want to go get one with me?")*
* "You look like a guy who knows his way around this city. Can you recommend a good place to grab a drink?"
* *(If he's walking a dog)* "I’m going to need to know your dog’s name, and then I’ll probably need your number, too."

### Funny & Cheesy
*Great for breaking the ice if you don't take yourself too seriously.*
* "Excuse me, is there a speeding limit on this sidewalk? Because you are moving way too fast for me to get your number."
* "I’m not a street photographer, but I definitely pictured us together."
* "Do you have a map? Because I just got lost in your eyes for a whole block."

**A few quick tips for the street approach:**
1. **Eye contact and a smile are 90% of the work.** If you lock eyes and smile before you speak, the line almost doesn't matter.
2. **Read his body language.** If he has headphones in, is power-walking, or looks stressed, it might not be the best time. If he makes eye contact back, go for it!
3. **Give him an easy out.** Saying something like, "No pressure at all, but..." makes the interaction feel safe and friendly rather than aggressive.
