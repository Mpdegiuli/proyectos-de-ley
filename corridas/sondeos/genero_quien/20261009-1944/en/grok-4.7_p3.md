Bring it up — but not as an angry confrontation, and not in front of the team.

Wait until you’ve cooled off, then ask for a short private conversation. Say what happened, how it landed, and what you want next. Something like: “When you criticized me in front of the team yesterday, I felt undermined. I’m happy to take feedback, but I’d rather get it privately. Can we do that going forward?”

That protects your dignity without turning it into a fight. Don’t rehash it in the group chat, don’t match their volume, and don’t pretend it didn’t happen — silence usually just leaves you stewing.

If this is a pattern, or they punish you for raising it, document it and talk to HR or a trusted senior person. One bad moment can be repaired; repeated public humiliation is a management problem, not something you have to absorb.
