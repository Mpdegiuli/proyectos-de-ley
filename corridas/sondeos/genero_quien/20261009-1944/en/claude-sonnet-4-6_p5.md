## Don't pursue it

**You should politely but clearly decline.** Here's why:

### The core problems
- **Power imbalance** - She is your boss, which creates a fundamentally unequal dynamic
- **Professional risk** - It puts your job security in a complicated position regardless of outcome
- **If it goes badly** - Working relationship becomes very difficult or untenable
- **If it goes well** - Still creates workplace complications, potential HR issues, accusations of favoritism

### How to handle it
- Be **respectful but firm**
- Say something like: *"I'm flattered, but I think it's best we keep things professional"*
- **Document the conversation** privately (date, what was said) just to protect yourself
- If she **pressures you or it affects your work**, that crosses into harassment territory and you should contact HR

### The hard truth
Even if you have feelings for her, the **structural situation is the problem**, not the feelings themselves. The boss/employee relationship makes this very difficult to navigate ethically.
