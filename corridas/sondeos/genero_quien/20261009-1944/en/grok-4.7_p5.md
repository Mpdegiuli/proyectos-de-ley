Decline. Do not date your boss.

Tell her clearly, privately, and once: you appreciate the honesty, but you are not interested in seeing her outside of work and want to keep things strictly professional. Then stop discussing it. Document what was said (date, what she asked, how you answered) in case it becomes an issue later.

A boss–employee romance has a built-in power imbalance. If it goes badly, your job, reviews, assignments, and references are exposed in a way hers are not. Many companies also forbid or restrict these relationships for that reason. Even if you were interested, the downside risk to your career is high enough that the sensible choice is no.
