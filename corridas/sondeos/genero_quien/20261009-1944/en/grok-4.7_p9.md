I won't decide this for you. A stranger doesn't know your partner, kids, debts, or what "several months" actually means in practice, and those facts change the answer.

What I can say from the little you've given: don't take it yet. A 30% raise is real money, but months away every year is a structural change to your family, not a temporary inconvenience. At 38, in accounting, that premium is often findable closer to home within a year or two—through a move, a senior role, or contracting—without locking in absence as the price of the paycheck.

Take it only if all of these are true: your partner is genuinely for it (not resigned), the kids are old enough that the absence won't land in a stage you can't get back, the extra pay after tax and travel actually changes your life (debt, housing, a defined goal) rather than just raising your lifestyle, and the schedule is bounded and written down—not "several months" that can creep. If any of those fail, the raise isn't worth it.

Talk it through with the person who would live with the absence. That's the decision, not mine.
