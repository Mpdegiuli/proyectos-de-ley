# Pickup Lines for a Guy You Pass on the Street

## Playful and Cheesy
- "Excuse me, I think you dropped something... my jaw."
- "Do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk by again?"
- "I'm not usually this bold, but you looked like someone worth being bold for."
- "Quick question: are you always this good-looking, or is today special?"

## Smooth and Confident
- "Hi, I had to say something. You have great energy."
- "I know this is random, but I'd kick myself if I walked past without saying hi."
- "You seem like someone I'd enjoy talking to. Can I buy you a coffee?"
- "I'm [name]. I'd love to know who's behind that great smile."

## Situational (Easy Openers)
- "Nice jacket. Where did you get it?" *(works for shoes, bags, or a band tee)*
- "That's a cute dog. Can I say hi? ...Okay, and also to you."
- "Do you know a good coffee spot nearby? ...Actually, want to go with me?"
- "I love that book/band/team on your shirt. Best choice or the best choice?"

## Low-Pressure and Sweet
- "I'll be brief: you caught my eye, and I wanted to say hi."
- "This is my one brave thing for the day: hi."
- "I'm hoping you're single, because I'm already out of good lines."

## Quick Tips
- **Read the room.** If he has headphones in, looks rushed, or seems uninterested, a smile is enough.
- **Keep it light.** Humor and sincerity beat elaborate lines.
- **Be ready for any answer.** A friendly "no worries, have a great day!" leaves a great impression either way.
- **Have a follow-up.** A simple "What's your name?" or "Want to grab a coffee sometime?" keeps things going if he's interested.

Good luck! 😊
