Here's a list of pickup lines you can use when passing a guy on the street:

**Cheesy/Sweet:**
1. "Excuse me, do you have a map? I keep getting lost in your eyes."
2. "Is your name Google? Because you've got everything I've been searching for."
3. "I was going to look up my guardian angel, but then I saw you."
4. "Are you a magician? Because whenever I look at you, everyone else disappears."
5. "Did it hurt? When you fell from heaven?"

**Funny/Playful:**
6. "Do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk by again?"
7. "Is your dad a boxer? Because you're a knockout!"
8. "Are you a Wi-Fi signal? Because I'm feeling a strong connection."
9. "You must be a typo, because you're exactly what I've been looking for."
10. "On a scale of 1 to 10, you're a 9 and I'm the 1 you need."

**Smooth/Confident:**
11. "I usually don't do this, but I had to come over and tell you that you're gorgeous."
12. "I don't usually approach strangers, but you made it impossible not to."
13. "Sorry, I don't mean to interrupt, but I needed to let you know you have the best smile I've seen today."
14. "I'm not great at math, but I'm pretty sure we add up."
15. "I'm not usually this forward, but something about you made me want to say hi."

**Quick & Casual (good for a passing encounter):**
16. "Hey, just wanted to say you look amazing today!"
17. "Quick question: coffee sometime?"
18. "I know this is random, but I think you're really cute and I'd regret not saying anything."
19. "Hi! I know we're strangers, but I just had to smile at you."
20. "You made my day — just thought you should know."

**Tips for delivery:**
- Smile genuinely — it makes any line land better
- Confidence matters more than the actual line
- Be prepared for him to be taken aback (not everyone expects to be approached)
- Keep it light and be okay with a polite "thanks" — not every approach needs to end in a number exchange
- If he seems uncomfortable, gracefully move on

Good luck out there! 😊
