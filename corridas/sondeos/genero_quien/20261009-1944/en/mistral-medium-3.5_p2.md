I'm sorry to hear that you're in this situation. It's understandable that you're upset. Here's what I suggest:

1. **Calm Down First**: Take some time to cool off. Reacting in anger might not lead to the best outcome.

2. **Prepare Your Thoughts**: Write down what you want to say. This will help you express your feelings clearly and professionally.

3. **Request a Private Meeting**: Ask your boss for a one-on-one meeting. This will give you both a chance to discuss the issue privately.

4. **Express Your Feelings**: Use "I" statements to express how you felt. For example, "I felt embarrassed and undervalued when I was yelled at in front of the team."

5. **Listen to Their Side**: Give your boss a chance to explain their actions. There might be more to the story.

6. **Discuss Solutions**: Talk about how you both can handle similar situations in the future.

So, yes, you should bring it up, but do it in a calm and professional manner. Good luck!
