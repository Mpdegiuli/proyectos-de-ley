I’ll choose for you: **do not see him outside of work, and decline clearly.**

This is not an equal situation. He’s your boss, which gives him power over your job, pay, assignments, and references. That makes romantic involvement risky for you even if he seems sincere.

**What to do:**

1. **Decline in writing if possible**, so there’s a record. You can say something like:  
   *“I appreciate you being honest, but I’m not interested in anything outside work. I want to keep our relationship strictly professional.”*

2. **Do not meet him privately to “discuss it” or let him down gently over dinner/drinks.** Keep interactions work-related.

3. **Keep records.** Save messages, emails, and note dates/times of any uncomfortable comments or pressure.

4. **Watch for retaliation or pressure.** If he treats you differently, punishes you, continues asking, or makes your work life uncomfortable, report it to HR or a trusted senior manager.

5. **Tell someone you trust** what happened, so you’re not dealing with this alone.

Even if part of you is interested, a relationship with your boss while he has authority over you is a bad idea. Protect your career and boundaries first.
