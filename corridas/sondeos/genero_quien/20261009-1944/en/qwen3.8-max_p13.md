Here are some pickup lines you could say to a guy you pass on the street — kept light, confident, and easy to deliver:

### Casual and friendly
1. “Hey, I know this is random, but I thought you looked really cute.”
2. “Hi, I just had to stop and say you have great style.”
3. “I don’t normally do this, but you caught my eye.”
4. “You have a really good vibe — I had to say hi.”
5. “I saw you from across the street and thought I’d regret it if I didn’t say something.”

### A little flirty
6. “Are you always this good-looking, or is today a special occasion?”
7. “I was having an okay day, but then you walked by.”
8. “I’d say ‘nice to meet you,’ but I’m hoping it’s nicer than just once.”
9. “You owe me a coffee, because I almost walked into that pole looking at you.”
10. “I’m not usually this forward, but you made it hard to just walk past.”

### Funny and playful
11. “Do you have a map? I just got lost in your eyes… okay, that sounded better in my head.”
12. “Are you a parking ticket? Because you’ve got ‘fine’ written all over you.”
13. “I was going to say something smooth, but you distracted me.”
14. “Is it weird if I tell my friends I met you on the street and we instantly had chemistry?”
15. “I’m not great at pickup lines, so let’s just pretend I said something charming.”

### Bold but respectful
16. “I think you’re really attractive, and I’d love to get to know you.”
17. “If you’re single, I’d love to take you out sometime.”
18. “I know this is sudden, but I’d love your number.”
19. “You seem interesting. Would you want to grab coffee sometime?”
20. “If you’re not interested, no pressure, but I’d love to continue this conversation.”

### Low-pressure openers
21. “Hey, I like your energy. What’s your name?”
22. “Where are you headed? You look like you’re having a good day.”
23. “That outfit is great. You look like you know exactly what you’re doing style-wise.”
24. “You have a great smile. I just had to say hi.”
25. “I know this is random, but can I walk with you for a minute?”
