Decline, and keep it professional.

Tell him clearly and calmly that you are not interested in seeing him outside of work and that you want the relationship to stay strictly professional. Do it in writing if you can (email or message) so there is a record, and note the date and what was said. Do not meet him outside work, do not flirt back, and do not leave it ambiguous.

A boss–employee romance has a built-in power imbalance. Even if the interest felt mutual, it creates real risks: favoritism claims, retaliation if things sour, awkward team dynamics, and possible policy violations. Most people in your position are better off shutting it down early.

If he presses, makes you uncomfortable, or anything at work changes afterward, go to HR (or a trusted skip-level manager) with your notes. You do not owe him a relationship or an explanation beyond a firm no.
