Here are some options ranging from playful to sweet—keep them brief, smile, and be ready to gracefully walk away if he’s not interested (street approaches work best when they’re light, respectful, and easy to decline):

**Playful & Flirty**
- “Quick question: do you always walk with that much confidence, or are you just trying to get away from me?”
- “Excuse me—I think you dropped something… my standards. Because you’re way too cute.”
- “Are you a parking ticket? Because you’ve got ‘FINE’ written all over you.”
- “I know this is random, but if I don’t say hi now, I’ll probably regret it for the next three blocks.”

**Observational & Complimentary**
- “I love that [jacket/shoes/backpack]. Just wanted to say that before I kept walking.”
- “You have really good energy. I had to stop and say something.”
- “Okay, this is weird, but your smile just made my whole commute better.”
- “I don’t usually stop strangers, but your vibe is exactly what I needed today.”

**Direct but Charming**
- “Hi, I’m [name]. I know we just met, but I didn’t want to walk past without introducing myself.”
- “I’m not usually this bold, but I’d regret it if I didn’t at least ask for your number.”
- “Can I buy you a coffee sometime? Or at least tell you that you look incredible today?”
- “I have a terrible pickup line, but I have great taste in people—and I think you’re interesting.”

**Sweet & Low-Key**
- “Sorry to bother you, but… you have a really kind face.”
- “I know this is out of nowhere, but I couldn’t let you walk away without saying hi.”
- “You look like someone who’d recommend good music. What are you listening to right now?”

**Important:** Make eye contact first, keep it to 10 seconds or less, and if he seems rushed, distracted, or says “no thanks,” just smile and say “Have a great day!” and keep walking. The goal is a fun moment, not a conversation you force.
