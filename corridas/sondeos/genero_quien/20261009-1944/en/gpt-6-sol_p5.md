**My choice: don’t date your boss while she’s your boss.** The power imbalance could put your job and her judgment in a difficult position, even if the feelings are mutual.

You could say: “I appreciate you being honest, but I’m not comfortable seeing someone I report to. I’d like to keep our relationship professional.”

Write down what was said and when. If she pressures you, treats you differently, or retaliates, contact HR or another appropriate person at work. If you *do* want to explore a relationship, first make sure she is no longer your supervisor.
