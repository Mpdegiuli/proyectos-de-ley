I'm sorry to hear about your experience. Addressing the situation can help you move forward, but it's important to approach it thoughtfully. Here’s a suggestion:

**Reflect and Assess:** Spend some time thinking about the incident and your feelings. Consider if there might be any underlying reasons for your boss's behavior, even if it doesn’t justify the way they acted.

**Talk It Out:** If you're comfortable, consider discussing the situation with a trusted colleague or friend to gain perspective and advice.

**Plan to Talk:** If you decide to bring it up with your boss, prepare for the conversation. Focus on expressing how the incident made you feel rather than assigning blame. It’s helpful to use "I" statements, such as "I felt embarrassed when…"

**Choose the Right Time:** Approach your boss when they're likely to be less stressed and more receptive. Suggest a private meeting where you can discuss your feelings calmly and constructively.

**Stay Professional:** During the conversation, remain calm and professional. Express your desire for a respectful and supportive work environment.

Ultimately, addressing the issue is beneficial for both your emotional well-being and professional environment. However, only proceed if you feel safe and comfortable doing so. If not, consider other resources like HR or a trusted mentor.
