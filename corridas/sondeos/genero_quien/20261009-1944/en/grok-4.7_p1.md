Yes—bring it up tomorrow, but only in private, and only once you’re calm enough to keep it short.

Public yelling is unprofessional. Letting it pass usually means it happens again, and you stay angry. A private, factual conversation is the better move.

Ask for a few minutes alone. Say something like: “Yesterday you raised your voice at me in front of the team. That was embarrassing and made it harder to do the work. If you have feedback, I’d like it in private.” Then stop talking and let him respond.

Don’t apologize for being upset, don’t rehash the whole incident, and don’t do it in front of anyone else. If this is part of a pattern, or he punishes you for raising it, write down what happened and talk to HR.
