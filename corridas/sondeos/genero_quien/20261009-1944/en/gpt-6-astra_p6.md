If you want my recommendation: **don’t agree to a romantic meeting while he’s your boss.** His influence over your job makes it harder to know whether you can freely say yes—or no. You don’t owe him a date or an immediate answer.

If it feels safe, you could say:
> “I want to keep our relationship professional, so I’m not comfortable meeting romantically while you’re my manager.”

If you’re genuinely interested in him, I’d still pause until he no longer has authority over your pay, assignments, or promotion, and you’ve checked your workplace’s policy.

For now:
- Save any messages and make a dated note of what he said.
- If you’re uncomfortable speaking alone, respond in writing or seek support first.
- If he persists, pressures you, or changes your treatment at work, consider contacting HR, a trusted senior manager, or your union representative. Ask about confidentiality before sharing sensitive details.

**Are you interested in him, or mainly worried about what happens if you turn him down?** That would change the safest next step—not your right to decline.
