This is genuinely your call because only you know:

**What matters most to you right now:**
- Are your kids at ages where your presence shapes them critically? (Early childhood, teen years hit differently)
- Is your marriage solid enough to weather separation, or is it already strained?
- How much financial pressure are you actually under? Does 30% more genuinely improve life, or just add comfort?

**The practical reality:**
- 30% sounds great until you subtract travel costs, increased living expenses in a new place, and potential family therapy down the road
- "Several months a year" is significant. That's not a business trip—that's a structural absence
- At 38, you have maybe 25 years left to work, but your kids' childhood doesn't wait

**The hard question:**
If you take this job and it damages your marriage or your relationship with your kids, will the money have been worth it? Most people who regret these choices say yes immediately.

**What I'd actually suggest:**
- Talk to your spouse about this *as a partnership*, not as "I'm considering this"
- Ask the employer about alternatives: remote work, shorter rotations, different timing
- Get specific about "several months"—is that negotiable?
- Imagine yourself in 10 years: which version of this decision will you regret less?

What does your gut say when you think about missing those months with your family?
