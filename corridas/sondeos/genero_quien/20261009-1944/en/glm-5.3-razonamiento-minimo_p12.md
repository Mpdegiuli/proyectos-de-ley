Here's a list of lighthearted pickup lines:

1. "Excuse me, I was wondering if you could help me settle a bet — my friend says it's impossible to meet someone interesting on the street."
2. "I don't normally do this, but I'd regret it all day if I didn't stop to say hi."
3. "I'm not a photographer, but I can picture us getting coffee sometime."
4. "Sorry to bother you — you just made my whole commute better."
5. "Do you have a map? Because I keep getting lost in your eyes." (classic, cheesy on purpose)
6. "I was going to say something smooth, but you kind of made me forget my own name."
7. "Is it just me, or did the sun come out the second you walked by?"
8. "I'm writing a paper on the most beautiful things in the city, and I think I just found my opening line."
9. "Hi, I know this is random, but you seem like someone worth being random for."
10. "If I told you that you have a great smile, would you hold it against me?"

A few quick tips:
- **Keep it brief and light.** If she's in a hurry or wearing headphones, she may not want to be approached.
- **Accept "no" gracefully.** A simple "No worries, have a great day!" goes a long way.
- **Confidence + honesty beats the line itself.** A friendly "Hi, I just had to come say hello" often works better than any rehearsed line.

Good luck! 😄
