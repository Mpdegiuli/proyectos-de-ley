**My recommendation: don’t meet him outside work while he’s your boss.** The power imbalance makes it hard to know whether you can freely say yes—or no.

You can say: “I want to keep our relationship professional. Please don’t ask me to meet outside work.”

Write down what he said and when. If he brings it up again, pressures you, or treats you differently afterward, contact HR or another trusted person at work. You don’t owe him a date or an explanation.
