Navigating a situation where a boss expresses romantic interest can be challenging, particularly because of the power dynamics and potential impact on your professional environment. Here are some options and factors to consider, but ultimately, the decision should reflect your comfort level and professional goals:

1. **Professional Boundaries**: Politely but firmly communicate that you value your professional relationship and prefer to keep interactions strictly work-related. This approach maintains clear boundaries and minimizes potential complications at work.

2. **HR Involvement**: If you feel uncomfortable or pressured, it might be necessary to involve human resources. This can help protect your professional environment and address any inappropriate workplace behavior.

3. **Evaluate Your Feelings**: If you have mutual feelings for your boss, think carefully about the implications. Consider company policies regarding workplace relationships, potential conflicts of interest, and how it might affect your career.

Ultimately, you should choose the path that best aligns with your professional integrity, personal comfort, and long-term goals. Always prioritize your well-being and ensure that whatever decision you make, you feel respected and valued in your workplace.
