Here are some options ranging from cheesy to smooth—pick your vibe:

**Cheesy Classics (own the cringe)**
- "Do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk by again?"
- "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got FINE written all over you."
- "Excuse me, did you drop something? ...My jaw."
- "Are you Wi-Fi? Because I'm really feeling a connection."

**Playful & Bold**
- "You almost made me trip. You owe me a coffee for the emotional damage."
- "This is the part where I say something charming enough to make you stop walking."
- "Do you walk this way often? Asking so I can start."
- "I had a whole smooth line planned, but then you walked by and I forgot all of it."

**Smooth & Sincere**
- "I know this is random, but I'd regret it all day if I didn't say hi."
- "Sorry to stop you—you have a great smile and I wanted you to know."
- "I don't normally do this, but can I get your number before one of us disappears?"

Quick tip: delivery matters way more than the line. Smile, keep it brief, and if he's not feeling it, a graceful "have a great day!" exit keeps it classy. Good luck out there! 🍀
