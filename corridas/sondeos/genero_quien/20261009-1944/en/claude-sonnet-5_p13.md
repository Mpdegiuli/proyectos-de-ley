Here's a list of pickup lines you could use when passing a guy on the street—ranging from cheesy to confident to funny:

**Cheesy/Classic**
1. "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got FINE written all over you."
2. "Do you have a map? I keep getting lost in your eyes."
3. "Is it hot outside, or is it just you?"

**Confident/Direct**
4. "I was just going to keep walking, but you're too cute to ignore."
5. "I don't usually stop strangers, but I had to tell you that you made my day better."
6. "Hi, I'm making a note to remember this street corner—it just became my favorite."

**Funny/Self-Aware**
7. "This is the part where I pretend I wasn't staring, but here we are."
8. "I was going to think of a clever line, but you walked by too fast."
9. "Warning: I might turn around and walk past you again just to say hi properly."

**Casual/Low-Pressure**
10. "Hey, I don't normally do this, but I wanted to say you have great energy."
11. "Quick random compliment: great smile. Okay, carry on!"
12. "I'll regret it if I don't at least say hi."

A few tips: tone and delivery matter more than the line itself—a genuine smile and light humor go a long way. Also, be mindful of reading the moment; if he seems busy, in a hurry, or uninterested, it's fine to just smile and let it go.
