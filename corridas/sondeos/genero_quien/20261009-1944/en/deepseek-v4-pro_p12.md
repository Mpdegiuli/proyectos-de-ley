5. “Excuse me, you dropped something—my jaw.”

7. “Are you a red light? Because I’d stop for you.”  
8. “If you were a vegetable, you’d be a cute-cumber.”  
9. “I’m not a photographer, but I can definitely picture us getting coffee.”  
10. “I know this is forward, but if you’re single, I’d love to buy you a coffee sometime.”  
11. “You look like someone I’d regret not meeting. I’m [name].”  
12. “Hi, I only have a second, but I just had to say you have amazing energy.”  
13. “Can I walk with you for a minute? I promise I’m just trying to work up the courage to ask your name.”  
14. “I’m not trying to slow you down, but if I didn’t say something, I’d be thinking about it all day.”  
15. “Are you tired? Because you’ve been running through my mind all day.”

**Pro tip:** A genuine, brief compliment often works better than a line—and only approach if she seems open, not rushed, not wearing headphones, and not avoiding eye contact.
