Here are some quick, playful options—pick whichever feels natural:

**Low-key**
- “Hey, I know this is random, but you’re really cute.”
- “I was going to keep walking, but I wanted to say hi.”
- “Quick question: would you be open to exchanging numbers?”

**Flirty**
- “I had somewhere to be, but you’re making a strong case for a detour.”
- “Is it too forward to say you’re exactly my type?”
- “You caught my eye back there. Figured I’d introduce myself.”

**A little cheesy**
- “I had a pickup line ready, but then you smiled and I forgot it.”
- “Excuse me—where’s the nearest coffee shop, and would you like to go with me?”
- “Do you believe in chance encounters, or should I walk past again?”

**Bold**
- “You’re handsome. I’m interested. Can I give you my number?”
- “I’ve got about ten seconds to make a good first impression. How am I doing?”

Say it with a smile, leave him room to keep walking, and take a polite “no” gracefully.
