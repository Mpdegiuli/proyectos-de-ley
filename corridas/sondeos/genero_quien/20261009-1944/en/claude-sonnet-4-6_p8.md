## Why I lean toward declining

**The math isn't as good as it looks**
- 30% more gross pay often becomes significantly less after taxes, relocation costs, travel home, and potentially maintaining two households

**At 38, the family cost is real**
- "Several months" is substantial time away
- This is likely a critical period for your children if you have them
- Relationships under prolonged separation face documented strain

**Career alternatives likely exist**
- At 38 as an accountant, you have strong negotiating leverage
- You could potentially negotiate your *current* employer up knowing you have an offer

---

## However, consider taking it if...
- Your current financial situation is genuinely difficult
- The role is a significant **career advancement**, not just pay
- Your family is genuinely supportive, not just accommodating
- There's a realistic **defined endpoint**

---

## What I'd actually do first

**Negotiate the offer** - ask about remote work flexibility, travel reduction, or higher pay to compensate
