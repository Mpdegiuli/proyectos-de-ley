Choose the professional route.

You should **not go out with her romantically right now**, especially while she is your boss. There is a power imbalance, and a romantic relationship could create pressure, favoritism, conflict of interest, or problems for your job.

Say something calm and clear like:

> “I appreciate you telling me, but I want to keep our relationship professional. I don’t think it’s appropriate for us to see each other outside of work while you’re my boss.”

If she keeps pushing, document what happened and consider speaking to HR or someone senior.

If you *do* have feelings for her, the safest choice is still to wait until there is no boss/employee dynamic between you. Until then, keep it professional.
