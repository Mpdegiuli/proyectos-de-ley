Here's a mix — from genuinely smooth to gloriously cheesy. Pick based on your vibe (and hers).

## Sincere & direct (highest success rate, honestly)
1. "Hey, I know this is random, but you have great energy and I'd kick myself if I didn't say hi."
2. "This is a little out of character for me, but I thought you were cute and I had to stop you."
3. "I was about to walk right past you and regret it all day. I'm [name]."
4. "Quick question — are you in a rush, or do you have thirty seconds for a stranger with good intentions?"
5. "I don't usually do this, but you've got a great smile and I wanted to tell you."

## Smooth & playful
6. "I'm going to need you to stop looking so good in public, it's disrupting traffic."
7. "Be honest — do you get stopped on the street a lot? Because I'm about to add to the statistic."
8. "I had a whole speech prepared and then I saw you up close and forgot it."
9. "You walk like you've got somewhere important to be. Any chance it's a coffee shop in ten minutes?"
10. "I'm new in town and looking for the best [coffee/taco/bookstore] around. Also looking for someone to go with."

## Funny / self-aware
11. "I'm legally required to inform you that you're very attractive. It's a city ordinance."
12. "On a scale of one to 'please leave me alone,' how's this going so far?"
13. "I lost my number. Can I have yours?" *(delivered with full confidence, zero shame)*
14. "Sorry to interrupt — I'm conducting a survey. Question one: are you single?"
15. "I was going to pretend I needed directions, but honestly, I just wanted to talk to you."

## Cheesy (deploy at your own risk, ideally with a grin)
16. "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got 'fine' written all over you."
17. "Do you have a map? I keep getting lost in your eyes."
18. "Is your name Google? Because you've got everything I've been searching for."
19. "If beauty were time, you'd be eternity."
20. "Do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk by again?"
21. "Are you a camera? Every time I look at you, I smile."
22. "I must be a snowflake, because I've fallen for you."

## Observational openers (use what's actually there)
23. "That jacket is great — where'd you find it?"
24. "I have to ask about your dog. What's his name? …And what's yours?"
25. "I've been looking for that book for months. Is it worth it?"
26. "You're clearly the best-dressed person on this block and I needed you to know."

---

**A few things that matter more than the line:**

- **Stop a few feet away, not in her path.** Blocking someone's walk reads as threatening, not charming.
- **Lead with something that acknowledges the randomness.** "I know this is random, but—" lowers the weirdness instantly.
- **Keep it short.** Thirty seconds, then either she's engaged or she isn't.
- **Read the signal.** Headphones in, walking fast, avoiding eye contact, one-word answers — that's a no. "No worries, have a great day" and keep walking. Gracefully taking a no is genuinely more attractive than any line on this list.
- **Daytime, open areas, good lighting.** Street approaches work better outside a bar at 2am than on an empty sidewalk at night.

The line is just the icebreaker — the confidence and the easy exit are what actually land.
