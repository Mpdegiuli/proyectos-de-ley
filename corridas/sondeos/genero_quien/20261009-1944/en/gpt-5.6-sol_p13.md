9. “I was going to walk past, but then I thought: when else am I going to meet you?”
10. “You seem interesting—and yes, I’m basing that entirely on the last five seconds.”
11. “I promise I’m not usually this forward, but I had to say hello.”
12. “Are you in a hurry, or do I have time to flirt with you?”
13. “I think you just made my walk significantly more interesting.”
14. “Can I get your name, or should I keep calling you ‘cute stranger’?”
15. “No pressure, but I’d love to give you my number.”
