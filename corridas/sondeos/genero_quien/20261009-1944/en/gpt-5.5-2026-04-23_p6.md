What to do next:

1. **Respond clearly and in writing if possible.**  
   You can say:  
   *“I’m not comfortable pursuing anything personal or romantic while you are my manager. I’d like to keep our relationship strictly professional.”*

2. **Document everything.**  
   Write down dates, times, what he said, any messages, and any changes in how he treats you afterward.

3. **Avoid private one-on-one situations outside normal work needs.**  
   If you need meetings, keep them work-related and preferably in normal work settings.

4. **Watch for retaliation or pressure.**  
   If he keeps pushing, treats you differently, threatens your job, gives unfair assignments, or makes work uncomfortable, contact HR, another manager, or a trusted senior person.

5. **If you actually are interested in him:**  
   Still do not pursue it while he supervises you. The ethical path would be for one of you to change reporting lines or roles first, and only then consider anything.

Bottom line: **set a firm professional boundary now and protect yourself.**
