Here's a mix of pickup lines—some cheesy, some funny, some more genuine—for a quick encounter on the street:

**Cheesy/Classic**
- "Excuse me, I think you dropped something: my jaw."
- "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got FINE written all over you."
- "Do you have a map? I just got lost in your eyes."

**Funny/Self-aware**
- "I was going to say something smooth, but you walked by too fast and now I'm just winging it."
- "I'm not great at pickup lines, but I didn't want to just stare and say nothing, so... hi."
- "Warning: I will regret not saying hi to you for the rest of the day."

**Genuine/Low-pressure**
- "Hey, I don't normally do this, but you have great energy—I just wanted to say hi."
- "This is random, but I thought you seemed really cool and wanted to say hello."
- "Sorry to stop you—I just thought you had an awesome [smile/style/vibe] and wanted to say so."

**Quick & Light**
- "Hi, I'm [Name]. I just wanted an excuse to talk to you."
- "I promise I'm not usually this forward, but I'd regret not saying hello."

A few practical tips:
- Keep it short and smile—no need for a long setup.
- Read her body language; if she seems uninterested or in a hurry, let it go gracefully.
- A genuine compliment often lands better than a cheesy line.
- Be ready to simply wish her a good day if she's not into chatting—no hard feelings.
