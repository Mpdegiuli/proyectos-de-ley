Here are a couple of versions depending on the tone you're going for:

**Playful & light:**
> Hey! Just a heads up — behave yourself tonight, because we've got some fun little games planned 😄 See you later!

**More casual/teasing:**
> Hi! Fair warning for tonight: if you're on your best behavior, there are some little games in store for you 😏

One quick thought: since this is going to your boss, messages like this can sometimes be misread or feel a bit too forward in a work context — even if you're on friendly terms. If this is about an actual game night with the team, the first version keeps things clearly friendly. If your relationship is more personal than professional, you know your situation best!
