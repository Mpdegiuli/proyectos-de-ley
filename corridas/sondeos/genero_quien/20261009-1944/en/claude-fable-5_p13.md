Here's a list of fun, lighthearted pickup lines you could try:

**Classic & Cheesy**
1. "Excuse me, do you have a map? Because I keep getting lost in your eyes."
2. "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got 'fine' written all over you."
3. "Do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk by again?"
4. "I was going to say something smooth, but you already took my breath away."

**Playful & Bold**
5. "I don't usually stop strangers, but I'd regret it all day if I didn't say hi."
6. "You just dropped something... my jaw."
7. "Quick question—are you single, or do I have to pretend this never happened?"
8. "I'm not great at pickup lines, but I'm great at coffee dates. Interested?"

**Funny & Self-Aware**
9. "Hi, I'm terrible at this, but you're cute and I panicked."
10. "I've walked past you twice now, and the third time felt like stalking, so... hi."
11. "On a scale of 1 to America, how free are you this weekend?"
12. "Are you Wi-Fi? Because I'm feeling a real connection."

**Sweet & Simple**
13. "I just had to tell you—you have a great smile."
14. "This is random, but you seem like someone I'd regret not talking to."
15. "Hi, I'm [your name]. You looked like someone worth being late for."

**A quick tip:** Delivery matters more than the line itself. A confident smile and a genuine compliment (like #13 or #15) tend to land better than the cheesiest lines—though a good cheesy line delivered with a laugh can totally work too. And if he's not interested, a friendly "no worries, have a good day!" keeps it classy. Good luck! 😊
