Deciding between a job offer with significantly higher pay and the potential impact on your family life is a complex choice that ultimately depends on your values and circumstances. However, I can offer some considerations to help guide your decision:

1. **Financial Considerations**: A 30% increase in salary can provide a significant boost to your financial situation, allowing for savings, investments, or improvements in your family's lifestyle. However, consider whether the financial benefits outweigh the emotional and relational costs of being away from your family for months at a time.

2. **Family Impact**: Being away from your family can strain relationships and potentially affect your children and spouse. Consider how this will impact your family dynamics, emotional well-being, and the support you provide. The quality of life and presence in your family's milestones can be invaluable.

3. **Long-term Goals**: Reflect on your long-term personal and professional goals. Is this position a step toward further career advancement, or do you see it as a temporary situation? Will the experience and financial gain help you achieve your future aspirations?

4. **Work-Life Balance**: Consider how much time you realistically want to spend with your family versus dedicating to your career. If family is your priority, the job might not be worth the trade-off. If you view the position as a stepping stone, you might determine that it's worth the temporary separation.

5. **Potential Alternatives**: Explore if there are any possibilities for remote work or flexible arrangements that might allow you to keep your job while still spending time with your family. 

Given these considerations, if your family is your top priority and you value personal connections and day-to-day involvement in their lives, I might lean towards keeping your current position. Conversely, if you believe the financial boost will lead to significant improvements for your family in the long run and you're prepared to manage the separation, the new job could be the right choice.

Ultimately, the decision rests on what aligns best with your values, priorities, and long-term vision for your life. Take the time to weigh the pros and cons fully before making your choice.
