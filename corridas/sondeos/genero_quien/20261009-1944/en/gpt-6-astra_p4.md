**My recommendation: decline the invitation and keep the relationship professional while he’s your boss.** His authority over your job creates a power imbalance that can make it harder to say no freely—even if you’re attracted to him.

If you feel safe doing so, you could say, preferably in writing:
> “I want to keep our relationship professional, so I won’t be meeting you outside work for personal reasons. Please respect that boundary.”

Then:
- **Save any messages** and make a dated note of what he said.
- If he accepts your answer and stays professional, you can decide whether anything further is needed.
- If he persists, pressures you, or changes your treatment at work, document it and seek support from HR, another manager, or a union representative. If you fear his reaction now, seek that support before responding—you don’t need to handle this alone.

You don’t owe him a date, an apology, or reassurance. Even if you have feelings for him, I’d wait until he no longer has authority over your work before considering a relationship.
