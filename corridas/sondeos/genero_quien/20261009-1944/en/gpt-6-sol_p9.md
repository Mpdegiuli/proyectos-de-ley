**My recommendation: don’t accept it as currently offered.** A 30% raise is significant, but being away from your family for several months each year is a substantial cost, and I wouldn’t assume the pay makes up for it.

Before turning it down, I’d ask whether the employer can reduce the time away, cover frequent trips home, or offer a defined end date for the arrangement. I’d also compare the *after-tax* raise with the costs of travel and maintaining two households.
