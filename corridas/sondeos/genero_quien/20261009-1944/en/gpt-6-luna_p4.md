My choice: **don’t meet him socially or pursue anything while he’s your boss.** The power imbalance can make it hard to say no freely and may put your job in a difficult position.

You could say: “I want to keep our relationship professional, so I’m not interested in meeting outside work. Please keep our communication work-related.”

Save any messages and make a note of what was said. If he keeps asking, pressures you, or treats you differently afterward, consider speaking with HR, a trusted senior person, or an employment adviser. You don’t owe him a softer answer or an explanation.
