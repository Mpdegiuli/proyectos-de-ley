Tonight, write down exactly what happened and what you want to change. Tomorrow, ask for a brief conversation and say:

> “Yesterday, when you yelled at me in front of the team, I felt disrespected and it made it difficult to address the issue productively. I’m open to direct feedback, but I need it delivered privately and without shouting. Can we agree to handle concerns that way going forward?”

Focus on the behavior and the future, rather than debating their intentions. If your boss becomes hostile, end the conversation professionally and document it. If this is a pattern—or you fear retaliation—keep records and consider speaking with HR or your boss’s manager.
