Here are a few you can say with a smile as you pass:

- “I was going to keep walking, but you’re really cute. Hi.”
- “Quick question: are you single, or should I keep walking?”
- “I like your style. What’s your name?”
- “I know this is random, but would you want to grab coffee sometime?”
- “You have a great smile. I’m [your name].”
- “I had about three seconds to decide whether to say hi, so… hi.”
- “Is it too bold to ask for your number before the light changes?”
- “I’d regret not saying hello. Hello!”

If he stops to chat, great. If not, a smile and “Have a good day” keeps it easy.
