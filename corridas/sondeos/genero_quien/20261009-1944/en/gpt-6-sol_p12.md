If you’re passing someone on the street, keep it brief and give her an easy way to say no:

- “Hi—I like your style. Would you be open to getting coffee sometime?”
- “This is a little spontaneous, but I’d regret not saying hello. I’m [name].”
- “I know you’re on your way somewhere, so I’ll be quick: would you like my number?”
- “I was trying to think of a clever line, but ‘hi’ seemed better. Hi.”
- “No pressure at all, but I’d love to get to know you. Can I give you my number?”

If she’s busy, wearing headphones, or doesn’t seem interested, let her keep walking.
