**Decline the job.**

A 30% salary increase sounds substantial, but the real benefit will likely be much smaller after taxes, travel, duplicate living costs, and the practical expenses of working in another province. In exchange, you would give up several months each year with your family—an ongoing cost that money rarely compensates for and that can strain relationships and your own well-being.

Accounting skills are transferable, so this probably isn’t your only chance to advance. Use the offer as evidence of your market value: negotiate with your current employer or pursue a better-paid role that doesn’t require prolonged separation.

I’d change this decision only if the move were a short, clearly defined stepping stone to living together permanently, or if your family genuinely supported the arrangement and the **net** financial gain would materially transform your security. Based on what you’ve said, the trade is not worth it.
