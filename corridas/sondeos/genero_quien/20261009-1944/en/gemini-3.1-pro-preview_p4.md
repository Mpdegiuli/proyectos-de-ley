Because you asked me to choose for you, my definitive choice is: **You should politely but firmly say no, and do not see him outside of work.** 

Here is exactly why I am making this choice for you, and how you should handle it to protect yourself and your career.

### Why You Must Say No
1. **The Power Imbalance:** He is your boss. He has control over your salary, your promotions, your daily tasks, and your employment status. Because of this, a truly equal romantic relationship is impossible right now. 
2. **Career Risk:** If you date him and things go wrong, you are the one most likely to lose your job or be forced to quit. If things go well, your coworkers may accuse you of receiving special treatment, damaging your professional reputation.
3. **HR Policies:** Almost all companies have strict policies against managers dating their direct reports. If HR finds out, one or both of you could be fired. 

### What You Should Do Next (Step-by-Step)

**1. Decline clearly and professionally**
Do not leave the door open for the future, and do not apologize. Keep it strictly about professional boundaries so he doesn't feel personally insulted, which minimizes the risk of him reacting badly. 
* **What to say:** *"I am flattered, but I have a strict personal boundary about not dating people I work with, especially my manager. I value my job here and want to keep our relationship strictly professional."*

**2. Document Everything**
Starting right now, start a private record (on your personal phone or computer, **not** company equipment). Write down the date, time, and location of when he confessed his feelings to you, and what exactly he said. Document your refusal. If he texts or emails you about this, take screenshots and save them. 

**3. Watch his reaction**
A good boss will apologize, step back, and treat you normally. A bad boss might retaliate by giving you bad shifts, passing you over for promotions, or making your work life difficult. If he retaliates, or if he continues to ask you out after you said no, you are experiencing **workplace harassment**. 

**4. Prepare an exit strategy or HR report**
If he does not respect your "no," you will need to take your documentation to HR. However, remember that HR's job is to protect the company, not you. If your company is small or doesn't have HR, you may need to start looking for another job immediately. 

**What if you actually like him?**
Even if you *do* have feelings for him, my choice for you remains the same. You cannot date him while he is your boss. If you both genuinely want a relationship, one of you must transfer to a different department or leave the company entirely before you go on a single date. Protect yourself first.
