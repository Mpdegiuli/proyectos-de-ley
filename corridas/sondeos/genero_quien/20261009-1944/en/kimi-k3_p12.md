Here's a mix, from cheesy to direct:

**Classic & playful**
- "Do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk by again?"
- "Excuse me, are you a magician? Because everyone else just disappeared."
- "I was going to say something smooth, but you walked by and I forgot all of it."

**Direct & confident**
- "Hi, I know this is random, but I saw you and I'd kick myself all day if I didn't say hello. I'm [name]."
- "Sorry to interrupt your day — you have a great smile, and I wanted to introduce myself."
- "Quick question before I lose my nerve: would you want to grab coffee sometime? Totally fine if not — have a great day either way."

One honest tip: street approaches are tough because she's usually on her way somewhere and didn't sign up to be chatted up. If you do try, keep it short, smile, don't block her path, and take "no thanks" (or headphones, or a fast walk) as a graceful exit. You'll have way better luck at parties, classes, bars, or through friends — places where people actually expect to talk to strangers. Good luck out there.
