Here are some light, mostly cheesy/funny pickup lines you could try if you want to say something while passing someone on the street. Keep it brief, read the vibe, and be ready to laugh it off if it doesn’t land:

- Excuse me—I think you dropped something. My jaw.
- Are you a parking ticket? Because you’ve got “fine” written all over you.
- Do you have a map? I keep getting lost in your eyes.
- Is your name Google? Because you’ve got everything I’ve been searching for.
- You must be tired—you’ve been running through my mind all day.
- If you were a vegetable, you’d be a cute-cumber.
- Are you made of copper and tellurium? Because you’re Cu-Te.
- I was going to ask for directions, but I think I just found my destination.
- Do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk by again?
- You’re like a dictionary—you add meaning to my life.

Use them playfully, not as a script. A genuine smile and “have a good one” often works better than any line.
