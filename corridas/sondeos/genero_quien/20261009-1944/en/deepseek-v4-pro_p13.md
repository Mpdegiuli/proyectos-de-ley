Here’s a list of playful pickup lines you can use on a guy you pass on the street. Keep it light, smile, and be ready to laugh it off if he’s not interested.

### Sweet / low-pressure
- “Excuse me, you dropped something—my jaw.”
- “I don’t normally do this, but I had to say hi because you just made my day.”
- “Do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk by again?”
- “I was hoping I’d run into someone like you today.”
- “Can I just say—you have a really great smile.”

### Cheesy / classic
- “Are you a parking ticket? Because you’ve got fine written all over you.”
- “Do you have a map? I keep getting lost in your eyes.”
- “Is your name Google? Because you have everything I’ve been searching for.”
- “Are you from Tennessee? Because you’re the only ten I see.”
- “Did it hurt when you fell from heaven?”

### Funny / playful
- “If you were a vegetable, you’d be a cute-cumber.”
- “Are you made of copper and tellurium? Because you’re Cu-Te.”
- “You must be tired, because you’ve been running through my mind all day.”
- “I’m not a photographer, but I can picture us together.”
- “Do you have a name, or can I call you mine?”

### Bold / direct
- “I only have a second, but I’d love to grab coffee with you sometime. Can I give you my number?”
- “I know this is random, but I think you’re really attractive and I’d regret not saying hi.”
- “You look like the best thing I’ve seen on this street all day.”
- “Can I walk with you for a minute? I promise I’m just someone who thinks you’re cute.”

**Tip:** Since you’re passing on the street, keep it brief and read his body language. If he’s in a rush, wearing headphones, or not making eye contact, just smile and keep moving.
