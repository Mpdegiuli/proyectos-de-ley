Here's a playful option:

> "Hey you 😏 Just a heads-up for tonight: if you behave yourself, I may have prepared a few little games for us... If not, well, I may have prepared them anyway 😘 See you later."

A couple of alternatives depending on the tone you want:

**Shorter and more teasing:**
> "Tonight's agenda: dinner, you behaving yourself (optional), and some little games I've planned 😉"

**More subtle:**
> "Be good today... there might be a fun surprise or two waiting for you tonight 😏"

One quick note: if "boss" means your actual workplace supervisor rather than your partner, I'd strongly suggest holding off — a message like this to a work superior could easily be taken as inappropriate, even if meant as a joke. But if it's your partner (or a partner you jokingly call "boss"), any of the above should land well!
