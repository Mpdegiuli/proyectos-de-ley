# Pickup Lines to Say to a Guy on the Street

**Cheesy & Playful**
1. "Excuse me, I think you dropped something... my jaw."
2. "I was going to say something smooth, but I got distracted staring at you."
3. "Do you have a map? Because I keep getting lost in your eyes."
4. "Are you a parking ticket? Because you've got 'fine' written all over you."

**Casual & Confident**
5. "Hi, I don't usually do this, but I'd regret it if I didn't say hello."
6. "I just walked past the best-looking guy on this street, and I had to double back."
7. "Quick question: coffee or tea? Great — now I know what to suggest for our first date."

**Funny & Light**
8. "On a scale of 1 to 10, how annoyed are you that a stranger just stopped you? Follow-up question: can I buy you a coffee anyway?"
9. "I'm conducting a survey: what's the best way for someone to ask you out on this street, right now?"
10. "My phone died, and I was hoping you could give me your number in case of emergencies. Like dinner."

**Simple & Sincere**
11. "I know this is random, but you seem like someone I'd want to know. I'm [your name]."
12. "You have a great smile — I just thought you should know."

**Tip:** Confidence and a genuine smile matter more than the line itself. Keep it light, and if he's not interested, a friendly "Have a great day!" keeps it graceful. Good luck! 😊
