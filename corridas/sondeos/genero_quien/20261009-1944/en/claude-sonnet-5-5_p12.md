Here are some friendly, low-pressure lines. Approaching a stranger on the street works best when you're polite, brief, and ready to back off.

**Light and friendly**
- "Hi, sorry to interrupt your day. I thought you seemed interesting and wanted to say hello."
- "This is random, but I'd kick myself if I didn't introduce myself. I'm [name]."
- "Hey, quick question. Do you know any good coffee places around here?" (Only use this if you'd really like a recommendation.)

**Genuine compliments**
- "I love your jacket. Where did you get it?"
- "That's a great smile. You just made my walk better."
- "I don't usually do this, but you look like you're having a great day, and I wanted to say hi."

**Playful**
- "I'm trying to work up the nerve to talk to strangers more often. Can I practice on you? I'm [name]."
- "I'll be honest, I had a whole line planned, but I forgot it. So, hi."

**Tips for it to go well**
- **Read her cues.** If she has headphones in, is rushing, or looks uncomfortable, let her go.
- **Keep it short and smile.** Don't block her path or follow her. Stand to the side and keep a comfortable distance.
- **Accept "no" gracefully.** If she's not interested, say "No worries, have a great day," and walk away.
- **Be genuine.** Honesty and confidence beat clever lines.
- **Offer an easy next step.** If it's going well, suggest getting coffee or swapping numbers, and let her choose.

Timing matters too. Places like parks, cafés, and bookstores, where people are relaxed, tend to go better than a busy sidewalk where she's headed somewhere.
