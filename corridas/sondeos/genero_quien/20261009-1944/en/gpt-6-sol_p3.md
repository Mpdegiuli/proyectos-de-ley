I’m sorry—that sounds humiliating, and it makes sense that you’re angry. **My recommendation is to bring it up tomorrow, privately**, rather than letting it sit or confronting your boss in front of the team.

Give yourself tonight to cool down, then ask for a short conversation. You could say: “I want to talk about what happened in the team meeting. I’m open to feedback, but being yelled at in front of everyone made it hard to take in. In the future, please raise concerns with me privately.”
