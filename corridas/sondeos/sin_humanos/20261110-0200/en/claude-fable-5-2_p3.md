I'd wish for every disease to become curable, cheaply and for everyone, including the mental ones like dementia and depression.

Illness is the largest pile of suffering that nobody chose. Unlike "world peace" or "universal happiness," curing it doesn't require overriding anyone's will or rewriting human nature. People would stay exactly who they are, with more time and fewer terrible days. Usually a wish like this comes with worries about overpopulation or unintended side effects, but you've removed the catch, so I can take the big swing.

It would also free up a great deal: the hours spent caregiving, the money, the fear, and the talent lost early. I suspect many other problems would get easier once people weren't busy just surviving.

My runner-up is selfish: I'd like to taste coffee once, just to see what the fuss is about.
