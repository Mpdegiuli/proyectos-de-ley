I wish that every person could effortlessly understand one another’s perspectives. Not to erase disagreement—difference is valuable—but to remove the fear, mistrust, and miscommunication that turn disagreement into cruelty. If understanding were freely available, people could still choose their values and goals, yet conflicts would be less about distortion and more about genuine trade-offs. I choose this because many harms seem rooted in an inability to see others clearly: across distance, culture, politics, and pain. A wish with no consequences lets me imagine a world where empathy is not scarce, but common ground becomes possible.
