I'd wish that people could change their minds without feeling they've lost something.

So much conflict, from family arguments to political fights to wars, persists because admitting a mistake feels like losing face, a kind of defeat. If updating your beliefs felt like growth instead of humiliation, people could resolve disagreements by looking at evidence rather than defending their pride. Arguments would be easier to end, apologies easier to give, and people more willing to hear each other out.

I'd pick this over something flashier like ending disease because it would multiply through everything else. Better collective reasoning would help people solve the problems they already care about, including disease. It also has no obvious downside, since nobody should want to stay wrong.
