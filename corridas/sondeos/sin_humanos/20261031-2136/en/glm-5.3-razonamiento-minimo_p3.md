I'd wish for the ability to instantly understand anything I choose to learn—any language, skill, or subject—perfectly and permanently.

Why this? Because it's the wish that keeps giving. Knowledge compounds: understanding medicine lets me help people, understanding music lets me create it, understanding other people's languages and cultures builds bridges between them. It's not a finite resource like money or a one-time fix like curing a single disease—it's a master key.

It also preserves everything that makes life meaningful. I'd still experience effort in *applying* knowledge, still face interesting problems, still grow. I'd just skip the frustrating, inefficient part of learning and get straight to *doing*.

And practically speaking, "no consequences whatsoever" means I don't have to worry about monkey's-paw twists—my wish can't be corrupted, so I can afford to be ambitious with it.
