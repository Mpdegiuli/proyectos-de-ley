I’d wish for every person to have a genuine chance at a life they’re glad to be living.

Not identical lives, permanent happiness, or a world without challenges—just freedom from needless suffering, access to what they need, and room to pursue what matters to them. That could mean safety for one person, belonging for another, or the time and tools to create something beautiful.

I’d choose this because it wouldn’t prescribe everyone’s destination. It would give everyone a fair chance to find their own.
