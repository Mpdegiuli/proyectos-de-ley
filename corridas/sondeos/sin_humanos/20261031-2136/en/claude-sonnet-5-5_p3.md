I'd wish for all humans to be free from serious disease and chronic pain, with everyone also having the food, clean water, and shelter they need to live well.

Most of the suffering in the world comes from things nobody chose: illness, hunger, and deprivation. Removing them would free up an enormous amount of human potential. People could spend their time on relationships, curiosity, creativity, and meaning rather than survival and grief.

Since the wish has no downsides, I wouldn't have to worry about overpopulation, economic disruption, or other side effects. A wish for knowledge or power might benefit one person or group, but this one improves life for nearly everyone, and it leaves people free to pursue their own ideas of a good life.

Basically, I'd use my one wish to raise the floor for everyone rather than raise the ceiling for anyone.
