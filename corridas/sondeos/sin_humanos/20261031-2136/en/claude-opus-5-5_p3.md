**I'd wish that changing your mind no longer felt like losing.**

Many of humanity's worst problems persist not because people lack information, but because admitting error feels humiliating. We defend bad ideas long after we suspect they're wrong, because backing down feels like surrendering part of who we are. Arguments become contests to survive rather than searches for truth.

If updating a belief felt as natural and dignified as putting on a coat when it gets cold, so much would loosen. Scientists would abandon failed theories faster. Political debates might become collaborative. Family rifts could heal with a simple "I think I was wrong." People would learn more because curiosity would no longer carry a social penalty.

It's a modest-sounding wish, but I suspect it would quietly unlock progress on nearly everything else. Truth would finally have a fair fight.
