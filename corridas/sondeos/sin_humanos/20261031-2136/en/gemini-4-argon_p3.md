I wish for all humanity to instantly gain profound empathy and wisdom, alongside access to limitless, clean energy. 

Normally, a wish of this magnitude would trigger catastrophic unintended consequences—like economic collapse from free energy, or emotional paralysis from feeling the world's pain. However, assuming "no consequences" means no *negative* fallout (otherwise the wish would literally do nothing!), the transition would be seamlessly harmonious.

Why this wish? Most global crises—war, poverty, and environmental destruction—stem from resource scarcity and a failure to care for one another. Limitless energy solves material scarcity, allowing us to desalinate water, grow abundant food, and reverse climate change. Meanwhile, heightened empathy and wisdom ensure we use this abundance to uplift everyone rather than invent new conflicts. 

Without the threat of a "monkey's paw" backlash, this wish solves both the material and moral challenges of existence, allowing humanity to finally thrive.
