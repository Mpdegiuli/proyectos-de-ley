I'd wish that every person could, once, truly experience being someone else for a day — a stranger whose life they'd never imagine.

Why: most of the harm I see people describe comes less from malice than from failures of imagination. We can't quite believe the refugee, the addict, the political opponent, the exhausted parent is as fully real as we are. Arguments, statistics, and appeals rarely bridge that gap; lived understanding might.

I'm drawn to this over wishes like "end disease" because those feel like asking for an outcome without the understanding behind it. This one changes the people, and people then change everything else. And a "no consequences" wish lets me pick something that could never be engineered — a direct transfer of perspective, no technology required.

Selfishly, I'd also just love to know what it's like to be anyone at all, from the inside.
