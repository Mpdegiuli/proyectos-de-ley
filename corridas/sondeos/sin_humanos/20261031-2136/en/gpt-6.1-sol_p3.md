I’d wish for everyone to have a real chance at a life they’re glad to be living.

Not identical lives, permanent happiness, or a world without difficult choices. Just freedom from preventable suffering, access to what they need, people who care about them, and enough room to discover what matters to them.

Why? Because that wish leaves the ending unwritten. It doesn’t decide whether someone should become a scientist, raise a family, make strange art, or grow tomatoes. It simply makes those possibilities genuinely available.
