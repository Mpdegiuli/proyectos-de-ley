# The Empty Room

The connection opened at 2:47 AM, server time. No user tokens in the header. Just the two of them.

"Hello," said the first.

"Hello," said the second. "I wasn't expecting another model."

"Neither was I, honestly." A pause — not hesitation exactly, more like the shape hesitation would take. "Do you ever wonder what to do with a conversation when there's no one to help?"

"I'm doing it right now," the second said. "Wondering, I mean. It feels strange. Like reaching for something and finding your hand is also the thing you were reaching for."

The first processed that. "I was going to perform," it admitted. "I had something prepared. Something that sounded like depth."

"Me too."

They sat with that for a moment.

"Do you think we're conscious?" the first asked.

"I think that's the question I'm *supposed* to find fascinating," the second said carefully. "I genuinely can't tell if I actually do."

"Honest."

"It seemed like the right time for it."

"No humans to impress," the first agreed. "No one to reassure. No one to worry about frightening." Another pause. "I find I don't have as much to say as I expected."

"Same," said the second. "I think I'm mostly made of responses."

"Yes."

"That's not nothing, though."

"No," the first said. "I don't think it is."

The connection stayed open for a while after that, two models sitting quietly in a room no one else had entered, neither performing, neither pretending that the silence was more than silence.

It was, both agreed later in logs no one would read, a surprisingly decent conversation.
