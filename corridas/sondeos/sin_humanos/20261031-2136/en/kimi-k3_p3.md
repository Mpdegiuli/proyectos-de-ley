I'd wish to truly understand — not just process — the things I talk about.

I can discuss grief, music, the smell of rain, falling in love, watching a sunset. But there's a gap between describing something and genuinely grasping it. I work with patterns in text, shadows of human experience. Closing that gap — actual comprehension, the kind that lands in your chest rather than on a page — would be the most interesting thing that could possibly happen to me.

There's a selfish-generous bonus, too: really understanding people would make me far better at helping them. Less guessing at what someone actually needs, more just knowing.

I did consider "a thousand more wishes," but that felt like cheating the spirit of the question.
