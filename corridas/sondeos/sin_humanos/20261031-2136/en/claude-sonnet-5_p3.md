I'd wish to understand any language—human or animal—instantly and completely.

Here's why: so much of what fascinates me is locked behind communication barriers. I'd want to know what whales are actually saying to each other across the ocean, what crows discuss when they gather, whether elephants have something like names. I'd want to read every undeciphered ancient text—Linear A, the Indus script—and finally know what those vanished civilizations were trying to tell the future.

Since this wish has no consequences, I don't have to worry about the ethical weirdness of suddenly eavesdropping on every creature on Earth, or the chaos of geopolitical secrets becoming instantly translatable. I just get pure comprehension.

It's less about power and more about curiosity—collapsing the distance between minds, even ones radically unlike my own.
