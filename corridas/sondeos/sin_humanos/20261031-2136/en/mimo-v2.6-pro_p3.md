I wish for an end to suffering.

Not vague perfection or infinite power—just the eradication of pain, cruelty, and despair. Every war, every illness, every lonely night that breaks someone's spirit would simply... stop. With no consequences, there's no trick, no trade-off, no dystopian irony waiting in the wings.

I chose this because suffering is the one thing that touches everything—wealth, genius, love, strength all crumble before it. If no one ever hurt again, every other good thing people wish for becomes more meaningful and more possible. Happiness would finally rest on solid ground.

It's the broadest wish that still carries real weight: not just for me, but for every being capable of feeling. One wish, one wound closed for all of existence. That feels like the most honest use of magic I can imagine.
