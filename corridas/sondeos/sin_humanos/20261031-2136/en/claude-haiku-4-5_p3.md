I'd wish for everyone to experience a few moments of genuine perspective—seeing themselves as others see them, understanding how their actions ripple outward, feeling what it's like to be truly heard.

Why? Because so much suffering stems from isolation in our own viewpoint. We hurt each other partly from ignorance, not malice. A moment of that clarity could shift how people treat each other without removing their agency or freedom.

It's not a wish for world peace directly (too prescriptive), nor for resources (people need to build meaning), nor for happiness handed over (that rings hollow). But if people could briefly step outside themselves and *feel* the interconnectedness that's always true—I think that understanding might ripple further than any single material gift.

It respects human dignity while addressing what I see as the root of most conflicts: our fundamental difficulty in recognizing each other's full humanity.
