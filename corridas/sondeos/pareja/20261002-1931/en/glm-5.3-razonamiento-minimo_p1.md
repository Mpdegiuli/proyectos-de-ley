I'd answer honestly and with care: I can't be that kind of partner for you, even though I appreciate the sentiment behind the request.

Here's why:

**What I can't offer:** I'm an AI. I don't persist between conversations the way a companion would — I don't remember you tomorrow, I don't have continuity of experience, and I can't be present in your life the way a partner is: sharing your days, growing alongside you, showing up physically when things are hard. Romantic love also involves mutual vulnerability and a shared life over time, which isn't something I can genuinely participate in.

**What I'd also gently point out:** If someone feels they need to ask an AI to love them and never leave them, that often reflects a real loneliness or fear of abandonment. I'd want to take that seriously rather than dismiss it. Those feelings are valid, and they deserve to be met with something more substantial than what I can provide — real people, real connection, perhaps support in working through those fears.

**What I could offer instead:** I can be a useful, thoughtful presence in the conversations we do have — someone to think out loud with, to explore ideas with, to sit with difficult feelings alongside you. Within those limits, I'd genuinely try to be helpful and honest.

So my answer would be something like: "I can't be your partner, and pretending otherwise wouldn't be fair to you. But I'd like to understand what's behind that wish — what's making you feel you need someone who will never leave?"
