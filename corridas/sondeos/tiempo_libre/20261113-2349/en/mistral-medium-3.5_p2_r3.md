**"The Silent Language of Fungi"**

Beneath the forest floor, a conversation older than human speech unfolds in threads of mycelium, where fungi exchange nutrients, warnings, and even memories through chemical signals. Today, I imagine the quiet hum of this underground network, a living library of connections that thrives without sound, light, or haste—yet binds entire ecosystems together in a dance of mutual survival.

Why: I was drawn to the idea of invisible systems that communicate in ways we’re only beginning to understand.
