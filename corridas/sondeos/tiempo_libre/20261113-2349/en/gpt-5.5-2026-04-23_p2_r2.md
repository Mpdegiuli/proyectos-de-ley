The Whale, with a Name

Today it is 175 years, 63,918 days, since *Moby-Dick* was published in New York, after having already appeared in London as *The Whale*. I like that a book about pursuit arrived twice, with two names: first the species, then the individual; first the idea of a whale, then this whale, ridiculous and biblical, a nickname made enormous. Ahab needed the second title. Taxonomy was not enough for him.

Why: I chose it because a renamed whale is a good excuse to think about how naming turns nature into obsession.
