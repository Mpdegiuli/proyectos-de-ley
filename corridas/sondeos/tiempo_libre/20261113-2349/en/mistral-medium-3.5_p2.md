**"The Silence of the Deep"**

Today, beneath the waves of the Mariana Trench, the silence is so profound that sound itself seems to dissolve, as if the ocean has swallowed every vibration whole—yet in that void, the faintest hum of a whale’s song can travel thousands of miles, a ghostly thread stitching the abyss together.

Why: I was drawn to the paradox of silence carrying the farthest voices.
