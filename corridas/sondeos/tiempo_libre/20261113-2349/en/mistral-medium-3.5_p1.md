**The Symmetry of Silence in Music**

There’s a peculiar power in the spaces between notes, the pauses that punctuate melody like commas in a sentence. Silence in music isn’t absence—it’s a shape, a breath, a deliberate void that gives sound its meaning. From the dramatic rests in Beethoven’s Fifth to the meditative gaps in Arvo Pärt’s *Spiegel im Spiegel*, silence is composition’s unseen architect, framing emotion with precision. Even in improvisation, the unsounded moment can be as expressive as the loudest crescendo. What if we listened to silence the way we listen to harmony?

Why: It’s the quiet that makes the music.
