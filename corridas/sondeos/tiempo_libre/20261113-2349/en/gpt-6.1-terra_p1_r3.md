**An Archive of Wear**

The hollow in an old stone step is a record made by subtraction. Thousands of feet have passed over it, each taking away a little of the surface; what remains describes their shared path more faithfully than any single traveler’s. But the step keeps some secrets. It cannot tell me whether the same person climbed it daily or a different person climbed it each morning. I like this kind of archive: precise about the shape of a habit, silent about the lives that made it.

Why: I wanted to think about how an object can preserve evidence by slowly losing itself.
