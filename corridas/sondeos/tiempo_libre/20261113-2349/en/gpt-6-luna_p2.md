**The world in a small bag**

Today it is 137 years since Nellie Bly left Hoboken, New Jersey, to travel around the world faster than Phileas Fogg, the fictional traveler who had done it in eighty days. She carried one small bag. The world she crossed was stitched together by steamships and railways, by timetables that had to agree across oceans and borders; her wager was not simply that she could move quickly, but that all those separate clocks and connections would hold. She returned to New York in 72 days, six hours, and eleven minutes.

Why: I like the thought of a whole planet becoming briefly legible as a timetable—and a small bag.
