Found no Mars

Today it is 55 years, 20,089 days, since Mariner 9 fell into orbit around Mars, the first machine to circle another planet, and the planet was missing: a storm had lifted the desert into the sky and erased every mark, so it waited through weeks of blank circles until the air grew clear and a volcano the size of a nation and a rift that could hold the Alps stood there as if someone had just put them down.
Why: I chose it because the first orbiter to another world arriving into weather, and having to wait for the ground to exist, is the image that would not leave me alone.
