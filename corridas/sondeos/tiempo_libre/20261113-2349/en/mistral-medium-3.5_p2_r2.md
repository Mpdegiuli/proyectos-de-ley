**13/11, "The silence of the deep"**

Beneath the waves, where sunlight fades into a ghostly blue, there is a world that operates in near-silence, a realm where sound travels five times faster than in air but is so sparse that some creatures have evolved to hear the infrasound of distant storms or the groans of shifting tectonic plates. Today, I am thinking of the Antarctic, where the silence is so profound that the occasional crack of an iceberg calving can travel thousands of kilometers, a solitary note in an oceanic cathedral.

Why: I was struck by the contrast between the vastness of the ocean and the rarity of its sounds, a quiet so deep it feels like a kind of absence.
