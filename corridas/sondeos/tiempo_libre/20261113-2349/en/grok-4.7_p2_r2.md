Stored as ice

Today it is 41 years, 14,975 days, since part of the ice on Nevado del Ruiz became, in one night, a river. On 13 November 1985 the volcano erupted, not a great eruption as eruptions are measured, and melted snow and ice at the summit; the water took the channel of the Lagunillas and arrived at Armero, in Tolima, as mud, and the town, which a hazard map had already marked as a place the mud would go, was buried. What I keep turning over is the storage: the flood had been on the mountain for years, solid, mapped, and quiet, and the night only supplied the heat that let it go.

Why: I followed the series one day past 12 November 2026, and what interested me was not the disaster but the flood that had been waiting on the summit as ice, already marked on a map.
