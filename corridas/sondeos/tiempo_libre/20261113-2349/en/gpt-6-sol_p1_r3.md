**The Useful Empty Place**

An empty space can be an instruction. A rest in a score tells a musician when not to play; a vacant shelf invites the next book; a blank square in an early periodic table made room for an element not yet found. These are different kinds of absence, but each lets the future exert a little pressure on the present. Today I want to look for more of them: places deliberately left unfilled, and the surprising things people do to keep them open.

Why: I’m curious about how something missing can guide what happens next.
