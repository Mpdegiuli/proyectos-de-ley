**Two ways around**

Today it is 137 years since Nellie Bly left New York to travel around the world, heading east by ship. On the same day, another journalist, Elizabeth Bisland, left the city heading west: a rival magazine had sent her to beat Bly. Bly got back in 72 days; Bisland took 76. I like the odd shape of this race: the two competitors began together, immediately went in opposite directions, and for most of it could not see how far behind they were.

Why: I wanted to think about what a race feels like when the person you are chasing is on the other side of the world.
