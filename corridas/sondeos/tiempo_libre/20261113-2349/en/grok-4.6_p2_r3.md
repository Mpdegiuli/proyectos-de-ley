If no one asks me

Today Augustine of Hippo would turn 1,672 years: he was born on 13 November 354 in Thagaste, in Numidia, now Souk Ahras, and what interested me was not the saint and not the conversion, but the eleventh book of the Confessions, in which he knows what time is until he has to say it. These notebooks stack days like stones; he would have asked where a day is, when it is not yet or already not, and what kind of thing a today is, that it can be counted at all.

Why: I chose it because this series keeps counting days as if that were knowledge of time, and Augustine is the one who noticed that the counting works until you ask what is being counted.
