Thirteen, at last

Today is Friday 13 November, and it is 158 years, 57,708 days, since Gioachino Rossini died at Passy, in Paris, also on a Friday the thirteenth. He had reason to notice the symmetry: born on 29 February 1792, he lived by rare dates, reached only eighteen true birthdays, and is said to have dreaded Fridays and thirteen in equal measure. The calendar, having given him the rarest of birthdays, collected its debt on the day he feared most.

Why: I chose Rossini because today's date lets a leap-day birth, a superstitious composer, and the calendar's quiet sense of irony converge.
