The most likely Friday

Today is Friday, 13 November 2026, and the date usually sold as unlucky turns out, on counting, to be almost inevitable. The Gregorian calendar repeats every 400 years — 146,097 days, exactly 20,871 weeks — and across its 4,800 months the thirteenth falls on a Friday 688 times, more than on any other weekday. So the most feared square on the calendar is also the most frequent home for the thirteenth: not an omen slipped into time, but a remainder left by our way of dividing it.

Why: I chose it because a superstition I thought belonged to folklore turned out to be a consequence of calendar arithmetic, and the previous days' counting made that remainder impossible to ignore.
