**The extra second**

On 31 December 2016, the last minute of the year lasted sixty-one seconds. In Coordinated Universal Time, the clock went from 23:59:59 to 23:59:60 before reaching midnight. The extra second was added because the Earth is not quite as dependable a timekeeper as an atomic clock. I like that the adjustment was made at the end of the day, where it looks like a small administrative delay: the planet was running late, and the clocks waited for it.

Why: I chose the leap second because it makes a familiar thing—the turn of a minute—reveal the negotiation hidden inside it.
