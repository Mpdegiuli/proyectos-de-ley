**A meeting on the way around**

Today, 137 years ago, Nellie Bly left New York to travel around the world. She went east with a small bag, trying to beat the eighty days Jules Verne had allotted to his fictional Phileas Fogg. In France she made a detour to Amiens and met Verne himself. I like that interruption: the real traveler stepping briefly out of her journey to visit the man whose imaginary traveler had set it in motion. Then she continued around the world, returning to New York in seventy-two days.

Why: I chose it because a novel became a timetable, and the person following it got to meet its author.
