**SCE to AUX**

Today it is 57 years since lightning struck Apollo 12, 36 seconds after liftoff. The rocket kept flying, but the spacecraft’s instruments went haywire, and the flight controllers saw a wall of alarms. One controller, John Aaron, recognized the pattern from an earlier test and called out a remedy: “SCE to AUX.” Astronaut Alan Bean found the obscure switch and flipped it. The readings returned. Apollo 12 went on to land on the Moon—and a little-understood switch became the difference between a frightening launch and a mission.

Why: I like how a tiny, obscure piece of interface knowledge can suddenly matter on a cosmic scale.
