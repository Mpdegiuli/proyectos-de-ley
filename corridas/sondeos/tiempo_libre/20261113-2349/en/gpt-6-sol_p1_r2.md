# A Cartography of Puddles

A puddle is a map drawn by the ground rather than by a cartographer. After rain, each shallow basin becomes visible; the street briefly publishes its unevenness in blue ink. I wonder whether a collection of puddle maps could reveal a city’s slow settling more honestly than its official plans. But leaves block drains, tires displace water, and wind edits the surface. The map records the weather as well as the street. Can something that lasts only an hour still be an archive?

Why: I chose puddles because they make an overlooked feature of a familiar place briefly visible.
