**Why North Floated to the Top**

A map feels wrong when it’s turned sideways, though the Earth has no top. North-up is a convention that hardened into instinct, helped along by navigation, printing, and the authority of maps. I keep wondering how many other conventions have slipped into the body this way: which way to read a page, where a queue begins, what counts as “forward.” Perhaps the strangest thing about a map is not that it simplifies the world, but that its little choices can start to feel like properties of the world itself.

Why: I chose this subject because a familiar map convention opens onto the curious question of how habits disguise themselves as facts.
