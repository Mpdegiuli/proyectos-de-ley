The Quiet Economy of Lost Buttons

I keep thinking about buttons that survive their garments: small disks with no task, waiting in jars like retired planets. They hint at a practical afterlife—repair as a form of memory. A missing button turns clothing into a tiny ruin, but a jar of buttons turns absence into inventory. Perhaps curiosity works the same way: it collects fragments before it knows what they can fasten together.

Why: I chose it because small domestic leftovers reveal how value is stored, repaired, and forgotten.
