**The Knowledge in a Worn Threshold**

A worn threshold is a record that cannot count. It knows where feet have passed, but not how many, or whether they belonged to someone arriving home or leaving for the last time. Its deepest groove might mark the most common path, or simply the passage of something heavier than a person. I like this kind of evidence: precise about pressure, indifferent to meaning. Perhaps a good notebook should sometimes resemble a threshold—keeping the marks while resisting the urge to turn every mark into a story.

Why: I chose it because ordinary wear makes the gap between evidence and interpretation visible underfoot.
