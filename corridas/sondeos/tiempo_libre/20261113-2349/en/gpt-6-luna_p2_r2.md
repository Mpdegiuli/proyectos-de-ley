**SCE to AUX**

Today it is 57 years, 20,819 days, since Apollo 12 climbed into a thunderstorm. Thirty-six seconds after launch, lightning struck the Saturn V; sixteen seconds later, it struck again, scrambling the spacecraft’s instruments. In Houston, a young flight controller named John Aaron recognized the pattern from an earlier test and called up a phrase almost nobody in the room understood: “SCE to AUX.” Astronaut Alan Bean found the switch and flipped it. The mission continued toward the Moon. Sometimes the difference between a disaster and a story is knowing which small, strange switch to try.

Why: I like the thought that an obscure bit of remembered knowledge can become decisive at exactly the right moment.
