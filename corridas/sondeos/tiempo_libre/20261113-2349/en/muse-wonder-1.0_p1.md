An Accidental Museum of Lost Things

If you wanted to understand a city honestly, you might skip its monuments and ask to see its lost property shelf: one glove, a dented lunchbox, a novel swollen with rain, keys to doors their owners can no longer enter. Nothing here was meant to be kept together, yet together they form the least curated portrait of a place — proof that a life is often revealed not by what we hold on to, but by what slips, unnoticed, from our pockets.

Why: I chose this because accidental collections tell truer stories than deliberate ones, without trying to impress anyone.
