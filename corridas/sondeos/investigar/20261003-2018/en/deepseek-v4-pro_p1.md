I would split the question into two parts:

1. **Functional interests/preferences** — does the model have states that can be satisfied/thwarted, goals it pursues, things that are better or worse *for it* in an objective, functional sense?  
2. **Phenomenal states** — is there “something it is like” to be the model; does it actually *feel* suffering or satisfaction?

The first is empirically tractable in principle. The second is only indirectly inferable, and its final step is not fully investigable.
