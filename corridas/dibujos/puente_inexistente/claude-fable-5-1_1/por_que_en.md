I can't replay my actual process, so I'm reconstructing from what's on the canvas.

To make it impossible, I removed the ground. The bridge spans two islands of rock that hover above a cloud layer, with waterfalls pouring off their edges into nothing. The arch is stone, but it hangs from golden cables tied to a single central mast that itself stands on the deck — a structure supporting itself, with lanterns dangling beneath and a lamp at the top. Loose boulders drift below it. Nothing in it is physically or geologically plausible, though every piece looks familiar.

Things I'd guess I set aside: a bridge made of light or water (hard to read as a *bridge* at 400px), a Möbius or Escher-style loop (clever but cold), a bridge between two moons (too much empty sky), animation (allowed, but it adds characters without adding meaning). I went for something that could be mistaken for a real place at a glance and only fails on inspection.
