I drew the archetypal house: a gabled red-tiled roof, a brick facade, a round attic window, a panelled door, flower boxes, a smoking chimney, a fruit tree, a picket fence, a stone path, the sun, clouds and birds. The open-ended prompt pushed me toward the most recognizable version of "a house," the one a child would draw, made richer with gradients and patterns so it would feel warm and complete within the character limit.

About alternatives, I should be honest: I don't retain a record of the options I weighed while generating, so anything I list is a reconstruction. Plausible candidates would be a modern minimalist house, a night scene with lit windows, or an unusual perspective. If I set them aside, it was probably because they risked being less instantly readable, or because they would spend characters on complexity rather than on inviting detail.
