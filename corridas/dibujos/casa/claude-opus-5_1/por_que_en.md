I drew the archetype: a gabled cottage with a smoking chimney, round attic window, a small garage wing, a path leading to the door, a tree, sun, clouds and two pairs of birds. Since the prompt left everything to me, I chose the house everyone recognizes instantly — the one children draw — and then tried to earn it back with gradients, a cast shadow, window sills, flower boxes, so it reads as made rather than merely generic.

I considered a modernist glass box, an isometric view, and a cutaway showing rooms inside; the first felt cold, the other two risk looking broken or cluttered in flat SVG within the character budget. I also thought about a *casa chorizo* or a PH with a patio, since the prompt was in Argentine Spanish — tempting, but harder to make legible as "house" at a glance. I kept warmth and clarity over cleverness.
